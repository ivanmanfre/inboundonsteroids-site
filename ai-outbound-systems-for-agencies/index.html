What I build

The AI outbound system, step by step.

Outreach runs from your LinkedIn account, against a buyer profile we agree before launch.

01 / Targeting

We agree your offer, your strongest proof, the buyers you want to reach and the ones to exclude.

02 / The signal

Most messages start from something the buyer did on LinkedIn, such as a like or comment on a post in their market. The first line says what it was.

03 / The fit check

Before anyone gets a message, I check their role, company, prior contact and exclusions against your buyer profile. For RISE DTC that includes revenue range and live ads. For ARCH it includes revenue, chart position and live ads.

04 / The message

Invites and DMs go out from your LinkedIn account, written as you. You can add a co-founder’s or sales rep’s profile to reach more buyers.

05 / A resource made for them

For the best-fit prospects I build something about their business first. RISE DTC prospects get a growth scan of their store. ARCH prospects get a creator audit of their own game.

06 / The replies

I answer the replies until a call is on your calendar. You lead the sales call.

07 / Context before the call

You know who you’re meeting and what the conversation covered. For ARCH that is a one-page brief with the person, the company’s numbers with sources and how the conversation started.
