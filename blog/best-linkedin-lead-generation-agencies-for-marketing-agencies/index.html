Belkins: best for an SDR team across email, LinkedIn and calling

Belkins has a page for marketing and creative agencies, where its typical starter package for marketing agencies is “from $5,000”, with 1,500 leads a month, 3 outreach channels and 100 guaranteed appointments a year, which is about 8 a month. Its SDR teams run cold email, LinkedIn outreach and cold calling. Its main pricing page lists four plans with no dollar amounts.

LeadCookie: best for LinkedIn and email in one campaign

LeadCookie’s homepage lists Core campaigns from $1,500 a month and Boutique campaigns from $3,700 a month. Core is tailored LinkedIn and email outreach with targeting, messaging and manual outreach. Boutique is a wider LinkedIn and email campaign for a narrow market.

Salesbread: best for a lead guarantee on a month-to-month plan

Salesbread’s homepage says: “We’ll bring you 20+ sales qualified leads per month using ultra-personalized LinkedIn + email outreach, or your money back.” On commitment it says “It’s a month-to-month service.” It describes its clients as B2B companies and shows no price on its homepage.

Cleverly: best for the lowest published entry price

Cleverly’s own blog says “LinkedIn lead generation plans start at $397/month”, with no long-term contracts. It also sells bundles with cold email and cold calling. Its “10–30 qualified sales calls per month, guaranteed” line is for the cold calling service.

Pearl Lemon Leads: best for paying per lead

Pearl Lemon Leads lists “£2,497+ Per Month” and “$200 to $1,000 per lead” on B2B work. A booked prospect who misses the call is replaced. Its page leaves open whether the per-lead price replaces the monthly fee, so ask.

LinkedSelling: best for LinkedIn ads and outreach from one firm

LinkedSelling works with “B2B companies ranging from SMB all the way up to Fortune 500.” Its outreach gives “over 500 touchpoints per month across LinkedIn, Email, and Phone”, and it also runs LinkedIn and Facebook ads. It publishes no price: “there’s a monthly plan to fit your growth goals.”

How to choose a LinkedIn lead generation agency for your agency

Ask every agency these six questions before you compare prices. My answer is next to each one.

  1. Does it work with agencies like mine?

    I work only with agency founders, 10 at a time. Belkins has a starter package for marketing agencies.

  2. Does the fee cover content?

    Mine covers your LinkedIn posts and lead magnets next to the outreach. Ask every agency what its fee includes.

  3. Whose account sends the messages?

    At InboundOnSteroids, yours or a team member’s, so the audience you build stays with you.

  4. Who answers the replies?

    I do, until the call is on your calendar, and you get the context before you meet.

  5. What is guaranteed, and on which channel?

    Read the wording. Cleverly’s guarantee is for cold calling, and Salesbread’s is 20+ sales qualified leads a month or your money back. My target is $15k to $60k a month of new pipeline in 90 days.

  6. What is the term?

    I have no minimum term and no notice period, so you can stop after any month.
