Chapter 02: What a booked call costs

What a booked call costs on a retainer

A retainer has no price per call printed on it. You work it out: the monthly fee divided by the calls booked that month. Here is the arithmetic at my price of $3,000 a month.

Cost per booked call at $3,000 a month
Calls booked in the month3581015
Cost per booked call$1,000$600$375$300$200

Put that next to per-meeting pricing. At $200 to $500 a held meeting, a $3,000 retainer costs the same per call when it books between 6 and 15 calls in a month ($3,000 ÷ $500 = 6 and $3,000 ÷ $200 = 15). Under 6 calls the per-meeting agency is cheaper per call. Over 15 the retainer is. That comparison leaves out any setup fee and assumes every booked call takes place, so run the three checks below before you rely on it.

Check three things before you compare the two numbers

  1. Booked or held

    A per-meeting agency such as ROI Maximizer bills only for meetings that took place. A retainer figure usually counts bookings. Ask for held calls on both sides.

  2. The setup fee

    ROI Maximizer charges $1,000 to $3,000 once, before the first meeting. Spread over the first months, that raises its early cost per meeting.

  3. What else the fee buys

    A per-meeting fee buys the meeting. My retainer also runs your LinkedIn content and lead magnets, and those stay yours.

The one monthly call count I publish is from Kyle Hunt’s case: around 20 sales calls a month across the 90 days we ran his LinkedIn. Read Kyle’s case.

LinkedIn outreach vs cold email

Both put a message in front of a buyer who hasn’t heard from you before. They differ in three ways that change the price.

LinkedIn outreach compared with cold email
LinkedIn outreachCold email
Where the message landsA LinkedIn message arrives next to your profile, your posts and your mutual connections.A cold email arrives from a domain the buyer hasn’t seen before.
How volume growsLinkedIn limits how many invitations one account can send each week. To reach more buyers you add another real person’s account, which is why I price extra team accounts at $1,000 a month each.Cold email grows by adding sending domains and inboxes.
What the first line can sayOn LinkedIn the buyer’s activity is public, so my first message can name the post they liked or commented on.

If your cold email already books meetings at a cost you like, keep it. LinkedIn is worth adding when you want a second channel to the same buyers, or when you want content and outreach working on one audience.

LinkedIn outreach agency vs software you run yourself

The software is cheap. Sales Navigator Core starts at $119.99 a month and HeyReach is $79 a month per sender, so one account costs $198.99 a month in tools ($119.99 + $79).

The tools send what you load into them. Someone still chooses the buyers, checks each one against your client profile, writes the messages, answers every reply and books the call. At $3,000 a month, the $2,801.01 above the tool cost pays for that work and for the content side.

If someone on your team has those hours and likes the work, run it yourself. If new business sits with the founder and the hours are already spoken for, buy it.

Chapter 04: Questions and sources

Common questions

What is a LinkedIn outreach agency?

An agency that finds your buyers on LinkedIn, sends connection invites and messages from a LinkedIn account and handles the replies, usually through to a booked sales call. Some also run your LinkedIn content.

What is pay per appointment lead generation?

You pay for each meeting or lead the agency produces, and a month without one costs nothing. ROI Maximizer charges $200 to $500 per qualified meeting that took place, after a $1,000 to $3,000 setup fee. Pearl Lemon Leads prices B2B work at $200 to $1,000 per lead.

How much does a LinkedIn outreach agency cost in 2026?

Published prices for done-for-you LinkedIn outreach run from $397 a month at Cleverly to $3,700 a month for LeadCookie Boutique, and £2,497 and up at Pearl Lemon Leads. InboundOnSteroids starts at $3,000 a month. Belkins and LinkedSelling publish no price.

How much does Cleverly cost?

Cleverly’s own blog says its LinkedIn lead generation plans start at $397 a month with no long-term contracts. It gives no price there for its bundles with cold email and cold calling.

How much does LeadCookie cost?

LeadCookie’s homepage lists Core campaigns from $1,500 a month and Boutique campaigns from $3,700 a month, both for LinkedIn and email outreach.

How much does Belkins cost?

Belkins publishes no price. Its pricing page lists four plans with LinkedIn outreach included and no dollar amounts, and asks you to talk to its team.

What does a booked call cost with a LinkedIn outreach agency?

On a retainer, divide the monthly fee by the calls booked that month. At $3,000 a month, 5 calls is $600 a call and 10 calls is $300 a call. Agencies that charge per result publish $200 to $1,000 per lead or held meeting.

Is pay per meeting cheaper than a monthly retainer?

That comes down to the call count. Against per-meeting prices of $200 to $500, a $3,000 retainer costs the same per call at 6 to 15 calls a month. Under 6 calls, per-meeting is cheaper per call. That comparison leaves out any setup fee and assumes every booked call takes place. With per-meeting pricing a quiet month also costs nothing after any setup fee.

Is LinkedIn outreach or cold email better for an agency?

They reach buyers in different places. A LinkedIn message arrives next to your profile and posts and can name something the buyer did on LinkedIn. Cold email grows by adding domains and inboxes, and LinkedIn grows by adding real people’s accounts. If your cold email already books meetings at a cost you like, keep it and add LinkedIn as a second channel.

Can I run LinkedIn outreach myself with software?

Yes. Sales Navigator Core starts at $119.99 a month and HeyReach is $79 a month per sender, so about $199 a month for one account. You still choose the buyers, write the messages and answer the replies yourself.

Do LinkedIn outreach agencies guarantee results?

Some publish one. Cleverly’s blog says its cold calling service books “10–30 qualified sales calls per month, guaranteed.” ROI Maximizer bills only for meetings that took place. I don’t guarantee a number.

Is there a minimum contract?

Cleverly says its plans have no long-term contracts. ROI Maximizer says there is no minimum term. LeadCookie and Pearl Lemon Leads state no term on the pages I read. I agree the commercial and handover terms with each client before we begin.

What does done-for-you LinkedIn outreach include?

At InboundOnSteroids it includes buyer research, fit checks, invites and DMs written as you, custom resources for the best-fit prospects, reply handling through to booking and call context before you meet. The full service also includes LinkedIn content and lead magnets.

How do marketing agencies get clients on LinkedIn?

The system I run finds buyers from what they engage with on LinkedIn, checks each one against your client profile, sends invites and DMs from your account and handles the replies until a call is on your calendar. Content and lead magnets run next to it, so a buyer who looks you up finds your work.

How long until LinkedIn outreach books the first call?

Two published cases from my clients: ARCH’s first invites went out on August 31, 2026 and its first booked call came on September 18, 18 days later. RISE DTC’s outreach started in July 2026 and its first booked call came on August 6. Your timing depends on your offer and audience.
