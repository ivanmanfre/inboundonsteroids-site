The problem

The two-founder problem.

David and Billy run Lemonade together. Both of them post. Both of them sell. The brand has one point of view, but the two of them do not sound remotely alike, and a feed where they blur into one another is a feed nobody believes.

Most content tooling has exactly one voice setting. That is the thing that had to be solved before anything else was worth running.

The voice stack

Three layers, so both men sound like themselves.

voices, one shared point of view

03
Engine 1 · Posts
03 / 05

Idea to scheduled post, with four checks on the way.

Topics arrive on their own. A draft is written in the right man's voice. It gets graded before either founder ever reads it. Their job is to approve.

01
Topics arrive
Every Monday morning the week's industry news is swept and turned into rated suggestions. Their own client calls are mined for the arguments they already made out loud. Competitor posts are read for what is working.
02
Editorial call
Each idea is assessed against their audience and rated before a human touches it, so nobody spends attention on a weak topic.
03
Hooks
Ten opening variations per post. The strongest is used, the other nine stay visible so a founder can overrule it.
04
Draft
The full post written in the selected author's voice, pulling relevant excerpts from their own past calls to keep it specific.
05
Quality gate
Every draft is checked for voice drift and for the tells that make writing read as generated. A failed draft is rewritten before they see it, with the original kept alongside.
06
Scheduled
Approved posts go to the right founder's account at the time they set. One move, done.
The steering rule they use most. One sentence of direction on a task overrides the editorial take entirely. Say nothing and it runs on its own read. Either way a draft is waiting, in the right voice.
04
Engine 2 · Lead magnets
04 / 05

6 formats, built end to end.

Not a document with a cover on it. When a lead magnet is approved, every piece of it is produced at once: the asset itself, the launch post, the email, the page copy, the DM replies and the cover.

Calculators, checklists, templates

Produced as finished spreadsheets with working formulas, formatting and Lemonade branding. No manual build.

Guides and swipe files

Written, then published as a live resource page on their own domain, cover and capture form included.

Interactive assessments

A self-scoring tool a reader actually completes, which is the version that earns an email address worth having.

When a lead magnet goes live, its promotional post is created automatically and lands in the post engine, ready to approve.

05
Engine 3 · Capture
05 / 05

Every commenter scored, not just collected.

When a post asks readers to comment for the asset, everyone who does gets it. Then the interesting half of the job starts.

Everyone gets the asset

No reader is left waiting on a founder to send a link by hand.

Each one is scored

Every commenter is graded against their ICP, with the reasoning written down, so a wrong call can be seen and corrected.

Only fits reach the list

The email list stays made of people they would actually want on a call, instead of everyone who ever typed a word.
