Under the hood
03 / 04

The parts a buyer never sees.

01
State-routed documents
Every US state triggers at different thresholds under a different authority. An order is routed to the right template, generated, and filed to the project board without a human copying anything.
02
Outbound with receipts
The cold engine tracks its own opens and clicks on his domain, holds every unverified address before sending, and refuses to touch the same lead twice. Bounce rate runs near one percent.
03
Permit-expiry mining
State permits expire on a public schedule. The lead engine reads it, finds the operators whose compliance is about to lapse, verifies their contacts and feeds a steady daily drip. Prospecting off public records, before competitors notice.
04
Bid-winner mining
Public construction bid data is scraped daily for winners about to break ground, the exact moment a site starts needing stormwater compliance.
05
A real interface
Not a pile of invisible automations. His team works from a custom web app: leads, queues, priorities, one-click outreach. Hundreds of thousands of runs a month, quietly.
