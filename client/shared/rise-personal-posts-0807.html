Post 1The valuation trap
MD
Mattan Danino
CEO @ RISE DTC
I've had a few pretty good conversations with buyers about selling my agency. Every single one came back to the same issue 🤯 The valuation. An agency that runs on its founder is worth a lot less without him in it. So the offer ends up looking like this: → Stay on for 24 or 36 months → Become an employee of the agency you just sold → Walk away with no job and no agency And the money? About a couple of years of what I was already making anyway. I felt pretty silly about it 😅 The trap is building an agency where everything runs through you. I only found out what that costs when I tried to price it. It's still my fear on the agency side.
