02 / Reading a result

Start with the sample.

Every supported result carries the number of posts behind it. Open the public examples to see what was counted. A high rate in a small or unusual set is a reason to inspect the posts, not a prescription for your next one.

If a market is outside the three covered niches, the result says so and shows a labelled pooled fallback, with a nearest covered-market hint. It does not quietly pass that fallback off as niche-specific evidence.

03 / Limits

History gives context.

The results describe public posts we can observe. They do not forecast your reach, guarantee leads, write or publish a post, or schedule content. Availability and samples can change as source data changes.

Using this with client work?

Keep private drafts, client records and sensitive information out of tool inputs. The draft check only needs the category labels selected in ChatGPT.
