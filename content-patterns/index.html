How to read it

The sample
belongs beside
the answer.

A pattern is useful only when you can inspect what sits behind it.

01

Check the count.

Each supported comparison shows the number of posts behind the rate. A cell needs at least 25 posts and a nonzero baseline before it is shown.

02

Open the source.

Public example links let you inspect the posts. A requested 7, 28 or 90-day window selects those examples; the rates still come from the latest completed historical market study.

03

Keep the boundary.

An uncovered market gets a labelled pooled fallback and a nearest covered-market hint. The hint does not turn pooled numbers into niche-specific measurements.

The boundary

History gives
context.

The results describe public posts we can observe. They do not forecast reach or leads, write or publish a post, or schedule content. Samples and availability can change as source data changes.

Keep private drafts, client records and sensitive information out of tool inputs. Check draft uses category labels selected in ChatGPT. We do not request or store a full draft or your conversation in the application usage record.
