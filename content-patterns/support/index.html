01 / Contact

One email reaches us.

Write to im@ivanmanfredi.com for tool support, inaccurate-result reports, or a privacy request. Please say which tool you used: niche breakouts or check draft.

02 / Useful context

Give us enough to find the problem.

  • The approximate date and time, including your time zone.
  • The niche, window or category labels you selected.
  • The public post link or part of the result that looks wrong, if relevant.
  • What you expected to see.

Do not email passwords, payment details, private client posts or a full draft. A short description is enough to start.

03 / Before you write

A fallback is labelled.

The tools cover automation, ecommerce and mobile games. Other markets may return a labelled pooled fallback, with a nearest covered-market hint. Supported comparisons require at least 25 posts. Read the sample size and open the public source links before using a result as context for a decision.
