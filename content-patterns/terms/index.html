01 / Scope

Historical evidence

Niche breakouts shows observed rates, sample sizes and public examples for automation, ecommerce and mobile games over 7, 28 or 90 days. Check draft compares labels selected within ChatGPT with that evidence. Unsupported markets receive a labelled pooled or nearest fallback.

The tools do not predict reach or leads, publish posts or schedule content. A comparison may be incomplete as public data and source access change.

02 / Use

Keep inputs appropriate.

Use the tools lawfully. Do not submit passwords, payment information, health records, government IDs, private client material or other sensitive information. Check draft needs selected labels, not the draft body. Automated abuse or attempts to bypass service limits may cause access to be restricted.

03 / Public sources

Open the posts behind a result.

Public LinkedIn post content belongs to its respective authors and rights holders. Source links let you review the examples in context. A link or excerpt does not transfer rights to republish someone else's work.

04 / Availability and contact

Results may change.

We may update the tools, samples or available markets, or pause access when the service or its data source is unavailable. For an inaccurate result or a question about these terms, email im@ivanmanfredi.com.
