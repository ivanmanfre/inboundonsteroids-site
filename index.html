Before we speak.

Who is this for?

Agency founders with an established offer and client work to draw from. We check fit on the first call.

We already use HeyReach or Valley. What changes?

Those tools send messages for you. On top of that, we check each person against your offer and handle the replies until there’s a call.

Can my LinkedIn account get restricted?

LinkedIn can restrict accounts that send too much. We send about 200 invites a week, and you see exactly what will run before you connect yours.

Will the messages sound like AI?

Each message points at something specific about the person, and you approve the first ones.

We already run outreach. Will people hear from us twice?

Before we send, we check the list against the clients, open deals and outreach lists you share with us.

When do we see results?

Outreach starts once you approve the first list. We can’t promise a date for your first call.

Is it a subscription or pay per result?

A monthly fee for running outreach from your profile. There’s no per-meeting charge.

Are results guaranteed?

No. The pipeline figure is an aim. Results depend on your offer, audience and sales process.

What happens if we stop?

Your audience, list and content stay yours. Ongoing delivery ends. We agree the commercial and handover terms before starting.
