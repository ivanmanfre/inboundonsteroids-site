Before we speak.

Who is this for?

Agency founders with an established offer and client work to draw from. We check fit on the first call.

Do I need an audience?

An existing audience helps the Inbound plan. The $3k plan adds outreach beyond your followers.

How much of my time does it take?

An intake, source material and the reviews we agree on. I handle the ongoing delivery; you handle sales calls and closing.

Will the content sound like me?

I use your calls and past work as source material. We tune the first drafts together, including the opinions and wording you would actually use.

How do we get started?

First we review your offer and source material. Then we agree the content direction, tune the first drafts and set the publishing process. We confirm your launch date during onboarding.

Are results guaranteed?

No. The pipeline figure is an aim. Results depend on your offer, audience and sales process.

What happens if we stop?

Your audience, list and content stay yours. Ongoing delivery ends. We agree the commercial and handover terms before starting.
