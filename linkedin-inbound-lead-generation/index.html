Starting conditions

A small audience changes the starting plan.

A small or inactive audience may need time and relevant connections before many buyers see the work. We look at who the agency wants to reach, what the founder can say and where those buyers already spend attention.

Follower totals alone cannot forecast enquiries. The starting plan should account for the attention and named interest you can actually observe. That evidence can shape what to make and distribute next.

Working together

Your expertise stays in the work.

We need your opinions, source calls or material, permission to use any client example and decisions about your offer. You approve the claims and publication process. You also handle sales enquiries and calls.

We agree how feedback from readers and buyers gets back into the next content cycle. Your judgment matters most when a draft touches client facts or a view the agency must be willing to defend.

What to distinguish

Attention, interest and enquiries stay separate.

Content attention

What people viewed or engaged with on LinkedIn. It tells us about the material they noticed.

Named resource interest

People who requested a guide or joined the list through a capture path.

Direct inbound enquiries

Messages or requests that arrive without a preceding outreach conversation.

Outreach-assisted conversations

Conversations begun or continued through prospecting and replies.

An engagement or download is not a sales enquiry. A call reached through outreach is not automatically credited to a post.

Common questions

Before we speak.

Do I need a large audience?

No fixed follower threshold establishes fit. A small audience changes the starting plan and how the work reaches relevant buyers.

Do I have to write the posts?

We write from your source material and review the substance with you. You approve claims and client permissions.

Will every post generate a lead?

An engagement, resource request and sales enquiry are different events. We can agree which of these are observable for your setup.

What if I have no guide?

We can decide whether a useful resource fits the offer after reviewing the questions buyers ask.

Can this run with outreach?

Yes. The services can work together while each conversation keeps its proper source.
