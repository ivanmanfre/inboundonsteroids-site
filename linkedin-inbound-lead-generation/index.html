03 / Building a channel

The starting point shapes the work.

Your part

Share calls, opinions, buyer questions and client examples we have permission to use. Approve claims and publication, then handle sales enquiries and calls.

A small audience

We look at the buyers you want to reach and where they already spend attention. A small or inactive audience changes distribution and timing; follower totals do not forecast enquiries.

What we measure

Content attention, named resource requests, direct inbound enquiries and outreach-assisted conversations stay separate. A download is not a sales enquiry.

The scope

We agree posts, visuals, resources, audience work, capture and newsletters after reviewing your offer and source material. The quote follows that work.

A buyer may read your posts after an outreach message. The outreach service covers research and reply handling; a later booking is not automatically credited to the post.

Common questions

Before we speak.

Do I need a large audience?

No fixed follower threshold establishes fit. A small audience changes the starting plan and how the work reaches relevant buyers.

Do I have to write the posts?

We write from your source material and review the substance with you. You approve claims and client permissions.

Will every post generate a lead?

Content attention, a resource request and a sales enquiry are different events. We agree which of these are observable for your setup.

What if I have no guide?

We decide whether a useful resource fits your offer after reviewing the questions buyers ask.

Can this run with outreach?

Yes. The services can work together while each conversation keeps its proper source.

How is the work priced?

We quote the agreed content, resource, audience and newsletter work after an exploratory call.
