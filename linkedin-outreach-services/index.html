02 / The opening

The source changes what we can say.

When they engage with your post

They have seen your agency's work. If they explicitly ask for a resource, a follow-up can refer to that request after the fit and history check.

When they engage elsewhere

They may only know the discussion topic. A reaction does not reveal personal need, so the first message stays close to that public context.

When a conversation starts

Replies are handled within the agreed scope. If a suitable buyer wants a call, we pass on their question and the reason the call was arranged.

Published work

Give the buyer something concrete to assess.

The public RISE DTC case shows prospecting and conversations from Mattan's profile. The growth scan shown here is work prepared for a particular prospect. When research supports it, a comparison or diagnostic can help start a useful conversation.

03 / Working together

The criteria come from you. The research has to earn each message.

Before launch

We agree the offer, buyer criteria, exclusions, approved examples, sender account, access and who answers fit questions.

During delivery

You take sales calls and tell us what happened. That feedback shapes the next research and conversations.

In the report

Researched people, unique people contacted, messages, replies, qualified conversations, booked calls and attended calls are counted separately. A booking is different from an attended call.

For the quote

Scope follows your audience, account arrangement and sales process. Research can reach beyond your current followers when the buyer is identifiable.

Already investing in LinkedIn posts? The inbound service gives buyers more to assess after they visit your profile. We keep the source of each conversation clear.

Common questions

Before we speak.

Who sends the messages?

We agree a real client LinkedIn profile and account participation before launch. The setup depends on the campaign and your access preferences.

Does a reaction mean someone wants to buy?

No. It shows interest in a topic. Role, company, history and exclusions need checking before outreach makes sense.

Do I need a large following?

No fixed follower threshold establishes fit. Outreach research can find relevant people beyond your existing audience.

What happens when someone replies?

Replies are handled within the agreed scope. If the buyer meets the criteria and wants a sales conversation, we help arrange a call and pass you the context.

How do you report progress?

We keep researched records, contacted people, messages, replies, qualified conversations, booked calls and attended calls distinct.

How is the service priced?

We quote after reviewing your offer, audience, sender arrangement and sales process.
