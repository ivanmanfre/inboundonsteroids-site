Where research begins

The source changes what a message can say.

People who have seen your work

Where relevant buyers interact with your own posts or resources, we can follow up on that context. They may have encountered your agency already. Their activity still needs a fit check.

People interested in a topic

We also research people engaging with relevant businesses and topics. That can show interest in a subject. It gives us no reason to assume they know your agency or are ready to buy.

A message should make its actual reason for contact clear. We do not treat engagement as permission to invent familiarity.

Conversation work

From a checked prospect to a useful call.

Research

Check fit before contact

We review the person and company against the agreed criteria, then choose a reason to speak that relates to your offer.

Message

Use relevant context

We reach out from the agreed real client LinkedIn account. Access and your participation are set in the scope before work starts.

Reply

Keep the conversation moving

We manage relevant follow-up, answer within the agreed scope and find out what the buyer needs. A resource can help explain your work where it fits.

Handoff

Pass on the reason for the call

When a buyer fits and wants to talk, we help book the call and pass on the conversation context. You handle the sales discussion.

Have a buyer profile in mind? We can look at the offer, exclusions and sender account together.

Your part

We need your judgment at the start and during delivery.

Share your offer, buyer criteria, exclusions and approved examples of your work. We agree the sender account, access and account participation together. You answer fit questions that need your expertise and keep time available for sales calls.

Tell us what happened after a call. That feedback helps us revise the next research and conversations.

Reading the work

Know what each number means.

Researched prospect records

People reviewed against the buyer criteria.

Unique people contacted

Each person counts once, even if they receive follow-ups.

Messages sent

Initial messages and follow-ups, counted as messages.

Replies

Responses from contacted people.

Qualified conversations

Two-way exchanges where need and fit meet the agreed criteria.

Booked calls

Calls placed on the calendar.

Attended calls

Calls that actually took place.

Before work starts, we can agree which of these units are available and how to define them. A booked call and an attended call answer different questions.

Common questions

Before we speak.

Which agencies are a fit?

Agencies with a clear service, a buyer they can name and capacity to speak with suitable prospects. We check the details together.

Do I need to publish content first?

Existing posts or resources give buyers more context. We can also discuss outreach on its own and agree what material can support a conversation.

What if my audience is small?

We can research suitable people beyond your followers. We agree where to look and how fit will be checked.

Whose account sends the messages?

The agreed real client LinkedIn profile. We confirm access and participation before starting.

How soon should I expect calls?

We first confirm targeting and the account arrangement, then learn from replies. Timing depends on the starting conditions and sales fit.

How is the work priced?

We quote the agreed research, conversation and handoff work after reviewing your offer, audience and sales process.

What counts as a qualified conversation?

A two-way exchange where the buyer's need and fit meet criteria we agreed before outreach.
