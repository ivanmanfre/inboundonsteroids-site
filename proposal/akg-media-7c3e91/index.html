What each audit includes

  • Every creator post about the brand in the last 90 days, on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok
  • For each post: creator, follower count, platform, date, views, engagement and a link
  • A label on each post: paid and disclosed, likely paid (promo code or tracking link), or organic
  • Totals: creators, posts, views, and how much of it is paid
  • The same numbers for 2-3 competitors, side by side
  • 3-5 gaps, each backed by a number
  • Creators already posting for their competitors, with handle and views
  • A page in AKG's branding on your subdomain, every number linked to its post

How one audit gets made

  1. Add the brand

    On the board: name and website, competitors optional. Behind a team login.

  2. Find the campaigns

    YouTube via Google's official API, with views, channel size and the paid flag. Instagram and TikTok via Apify: tags, mentions, hashtags, paid-partnership labels.

  3. Read every post

    Claude sorts each one: paid and disclosed, likely paid (promo code or tracking link), or organic.

  4. Compare and write

    Same pass for each competitor, then the comparison and the gaps. Every number links to its post.

  5. Check and publish

    Claims without a source get rejected. The page goes live on your subdomain, the link shows on the board, Slack pings.

I build and tune it on my own accounts first, so sources get added or cut before anything is yours. Once the audits are right, it moves to AKG's accounts.
