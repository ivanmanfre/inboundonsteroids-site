01 / Paid ads

Test the homepage gift guide as the gift ad's landing page

Your gift ad is recorded landing on your best-sellers collection, which carries no gift messaging. Your homepage has a gift guide section ("From stocking fillers to collectable pieces") and a "Gift ideas for every golfer" product row, so whether gift shoppers buy more there is untested.

One change

Keep the gift ad copy unchanged and change only the link. Control: the best-sellers collection. Variant: the homepage, with its gift guide section and gift ideas row. Run it as a Meta A/B test with two otherwise identical ads, the same audience and an even budget split. If your carousel cards have their own links, change every card's link. A later test could try your Gift Ideas collection.

02 / Paid ads

Lead the brand ad with tee packs

Your two brand ads (one video, one image) share copy listing many products and are recorded landing on the homepage. The Golf Addiction Tee Pack is listed first when your store sorts by best selling and is featured on the homepage, so copy that leads with it is an untested alternative.

One change

Keep the homepage link, audience and creative (use the image ad for both). Variant draft: "Tee packs with a little irony. Designed in Milan for golfers who don't take the game too seriously. Shop the Golf Addiction Tee Pack." Control: the current brand copy. Run both as a Meta A/B test, even budget split. If it wins, a follow-up can test the tee pack's page.

03 / Your store

Show the three-pack beside the single tee pack

Your Upper Decky page offers a set of three under a "Make it a set" heading, already showing the set price against the higher full price. Whether presenting the set as a side-by-side option with the single pack changes how many shoppers buy a set is untested.

One change

Variant: show the single pack and the three-pack as side-by-side options at the buy button, with the set labelled "Best value: 3 packs for €26,00 instead of €30,00". Control keeps the current layout. Split visitors 50/50.

04 / Your store

Offer the eight-scent Club Champion where single scented packs are listed

Your all-products page, sorted by best selling, shows scented packs one by one at €12,90 each on its first page, among other tee packs. The Club Champion holds all eight scents in one gift, so the choice between one scent and the full set is worth testing.

One change

Variant: add a banner at the top of that listing reading "Can't pick a scent? Get all eight in The Club Champion, €65,00", linking to that product. Control shows the listing unchanged. Randomly split visitors 50/50.
