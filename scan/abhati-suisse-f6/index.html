01 / Proposed paid test

Test a gym-sweat build-up ad against your "up to 89%" breakage claim

The Nourishing Shampoo page carries two angles: the "up to 89%" breakage figure your homepage also uses, and a Build-up tag echoed by several reviews we read about gym or cycling-helmet sweat. Which angle wins first purchases from a cold ad click is not yet known.

One change

Proposed new ad test, not an observed ad. Both variants send clicks to the Nourishing Shampoo page. Target Switzerland with equal budgets per variant. Variant A: "Clinically proven to reduce breakage by up to 89%. Nourishing Shampoo, formulated in Switzerland." Variant B: "Gym or helmet sweat leaving your scalp congested? Nourishing Shampoo gently removes build-up." Keep the page's "up to" qualifier wherever the 89% figure appears.

02 / Your store

Offer the 1 Litre on the Nourishing Shampoo page

Your page recommends using Nourishing Shampoo "for a period of 3 months", but the sampled text does not say how long one 250 mL bottle lasts. The 1 Litre at CHF 99 costs CHF 45 less than four 250 mL bottles, yet this page, like your other 250 mL shampoo and conditioner pages, does not link to its 1 Litre.

One change

Show a 250 mL or 1 Litre size choice beside Add to cart for Swiss visitors; control keeps the current page. Draft line: "Use it every wash? The 1 Litre costs CHF 45 less than four 250 mL bottles." Use it only while the 1 Litre is CHF 99, and confirm litre margin and how long 1 Litre lasts against your 6-month after-opening limit first.

03 / Your store

Test a Volume Duo card on the fine-hair conditioner page

The Whipped Volumising Conditioner page is written for fine hair, and its routine cards start with the Volumising Shampoo. The shop also lists a Volume Duo as a shampoo and conditioner pairing, but the text we read does not say it includes this conditioner.

One change

If the Volume Duo pairs IMLAYA shampoo with this NAKKI conditioner (its listing text doesn't name them), make the Duo the first routine card with the line "Lift and bounce, shampoo and conditioner together." Control keeps the current routine cards. Only the first card changes. Frame the Duo card as an alternative to buying the conditioner alone, not an add-on.
