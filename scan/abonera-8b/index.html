01 / Proposed paid test

Test a matching-socks ad against a flexible-delivery ad

Your homepage leads with two different promises: matching socks that end the sock-drawer mess, and deliveries you can change, skip or end. It is not yet known which promise leads a new ad visitor to start an order.

One change

Proposed new Meta ad test, not an observed live ad. Both ads send clicks to your homepage sock picker, so only the message changes. Run it as a Meta A/B test with equal budgets. Variant A: "Slutt på singelsokker. Start på nytt med bare like bambussokker. Fargestripe viser størrelsen." Variant B: "Sokker på fast påfyll. Ingen bindingstid. Hopp over eller avslutt når du vil."

02 / Your store

Test a 10-pair sock-drawer start above the homepage picker

It is unclear whether a new buyer should start with one 5-pair refill or a full sock-drawer replacement. Your homepage shows packs of 10, 15 or 20 pairs below the sock picker, your FAQ lists 5, 10 or 15-pair starter packs, and the sampled product page sells 5 pairs per delivery.

One change

Route test: split homepage visitors at random. Control keeps the current "Kom i gang" sock-type picker first. Variant moves your existing "Bytt ut hele sokkeskuffen" block, with its "Se sokkepakkene" link, above the picker and leads it with "10 par like bambussokker med faste leveranser." We did not read the sock-pack page or the steps after the picker.

It is unclear whether a new buyer should start with one 5-pair refill or a full sock-drawer replacement. Your homepage shows packs of 10, 15 or 20 pairs below the sock picker, your FAQ lists 5, 10 or 15-pair starter packs, and the sampled product page sells 5 pairs per delivery.
