01 / Proposed paid test

Test a hunching ad against a burp-cloth ad

Your homepage names two feeding frustrations: being "hunched over trying to reach" the baby, and using "rolled up burp cloths". Neither Meta's nor Google's public ad library shows live ads for the brand, so it is open which should lead a new ad, and the product page already explains the adjustable angles.

One change

Proposed ad test, not a live ad. Send both to https://acutebabyco.com/products/the-wedge, with the same image, audience and budget. A: "Tired of hunching to feed? The Wedge brings baby up to you. Use it under your nursing pillow or on its own. Preorder now." B: "Still stacking burp cloths to get the angle right? Build 10°, 20° or 30° of support with The Wedge. Preorder now." Only the copy differs.

02 / Your store

Show three Wedge options as selectable cards

Your product page already shows The Wedge at $58, the BIG Support Bundle at $89.99 and the 2 Wedge Bundle at $99.99, with savings noted, but each bundle sits behind a View link. Showing what each option contains in place may help shoppers settle on one.

One change

Keep the existing prices and savings. Turn the three options into selectable cards on the product page, each with a one-line contents list, such as 'Wedge, spare cover, liners and scrunchie' versus 'Two Wedges and two scrunchies'. Split visitors 50/50.

03 / Your store

Test a one-package delivery note for accessories

Your product page already has an "Upgrade to a Bundle & Save" section, and Waterproof Liners at $24.99 and a Spare Cover at $19.99 are sold as separate items. Your shipping policy states accessories "will ship together in a single consolidated package" with the preorder item. The sampled product page text for that section does not mention this.

One change

In the existing accessory section of the product page, add one line: 'Add a Spare Cover or Waterproof Liners and get one package with your Wedge, shipped free to the contiguous U.S. (estimated late 2026).' Leave everything else unchanged and split visitors 50/50.
