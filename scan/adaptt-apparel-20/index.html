01 / Proposed paid test

Test recovery-first against style-first ad message

Your sweater page lists several reasons to buy, from mobility issues and post surgery to simply loving the look, and the home page leads with easy dressing and recovery. We could not read your Meta ads to see which reason leads today, and a recovery-only message may reach a narrower group than a style one.

One change

Proposed new ad test: send two ad messages to the same Elizabeth Women Zip Sweater page. A: 'Made for easier dressing after surgery or during rehab. Meet The Elizabeth zip sweater with easy zippers.' B: 'A zip sweater that looks as good as it feels. Meet The Elizabeth.' Run both in Meta's A/B test tool with the same audience, budget and images.

02 / Your store

Test a clearer size box on sweater page

The Elizabeth page says its garments "typically run one size larger than standard sizing", but that note sits below the buy button. Three of its 5'7 models wear small, medium and large, so height alone does not guide the choice, and returns must be unworn within 30 days.

One change

On The Elizabeth Women Zip Sweater page, turn the sizing note into a short "Which size?" box beside the size picker. Draft: "Typically runs one size larger than standard. Between sizes? Size down." Move the model notes already on the page (for example, Alex is 5'7 and wears a small) next to each matching colour photo. Split visitors 50/50 against the current page.

03 / Your store

Test a matching sweater prompt on pant pages

A shopper looking at one pant must decide whether to buy a single piece or the full set. The store calls its breakout design the Adaptt Track Suit, but pants and sweaters are separate products. Each pant is $79.99 and each sweater $84.99, so any single piece falls under the $100 CAD free-shipping line.

One change

On the Ann and Lyall pant pages, show a "Complete your Adaptt suit" block with the matching sweater in the same colour where available (Elizabeth for Ann, Billy for Lyall). Draft line: "Add the matching sweater and standard shipping is free on Canadian orders over $100 CAD." Show it only to visitors browsing from Canada, split 50/50.
