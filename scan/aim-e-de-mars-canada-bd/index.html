01 / Proposed paid test

Credit-back message versus ingredient message for Déesses Set ads

Your best-selling Déesses Set's $58.50 is credited toward a full bottle, while the homepage leads with ingredient transparency. We could not read your current Meta ads, so we do not know which reason they lead with.

One change

Proposed ad test, both landing on the Déesses Set page. Variant A: "Seven vials of French natural perfume for $58.50, fully credited toward a 30 ml or 50 ml bottle within 30 days." Variant B: "French perfume with every ingredient declared on the label. Try seven vials, then choose one." Split people new to the brand evenly, same budget. Confirm the credit promotion runs for the whole test.

02 / Your store

Compare the two discovery sets by scent style

Your two best sellers, the Déesses and Originelle sets, are seven-vial sets at the same $58.50, each described mainly by its scent list. A separate scent quiz exists, but the samplers page, which also offers the 2-vial sampler and Prestige Set, has no short side-by-side style summary of the two seven-vial sets.

One change

On the samplers collection page, add a one-line style guide under each set. Draft: "Déesses: solar florals, citrus freshness, woods and balm. Originelle: powdery, ambered and oriental scents, plus one fresh." Randomly split visitors between this and the current page. Change nothing else.

03 / Your store

Show each discovery set's own full-size bottles

The Déesses, Originelle and 2-vial sampler pages sampled all show the same six full-size products under the next-step heading, and only some of them are in the set being viewed. Whether a list matched to the set's own scents makes the move from trial to full bottle clearer is untested.

One change

On the Déesses and Originelle set pages, replace the shared block with full bottles from that set's own scents (for example Belle Aphrodite and Divine Isis on Déesses, Belle Rose and Natasha on Originelle). Draft heading: "Found a favourite? Your set credit applies to a 30 ml or 50 ml bottle within 30 days." Randomly split page visitors between this and the current block.
