Finding 1 · Shopper paths

38.9% of your variants are sold out while your 52 Meta ads run

You have 52 active ads on Meta, and 38.9% of your variants read out of stock right now. A click that lands on a sold-out option can't turn into an order.

Recommended next step

Pull sold-out products out of your ads and product feed until they're back in stock.

Finding 2 · Repeat purchase

We didn't find a rewards program on your store

A first-time buyer earns no points or credit toward a next order, so nothing on the store itself gives them a reason to come back.

Recommended next step

Add a simple points or next-order credit program and mention it on the order confirmation page.
