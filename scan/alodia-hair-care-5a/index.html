01 / Paid ads

Name the live system price in the ad

Your back-in-stock ad promises "a more accessible price" but names none. The system page says "Bundle & save" at $63.50, the same as the oil ($23), Creme Shampoo ($19.50) and masque ($21) bought separately, but it also lists Butter Creme as item 4, so shoppers cannot tell what the system holds or saves.

One change

Untested proposal. Control keeps the ad as is; the variant names the confirmed live price, such as "Wash Day Essentials System: [confirmed items] for [confirmed price]". First confirm what the system contains and its live price, and say "save" only if it costs less than the items bought separately. Split eligible viewers evenly; video and page stay the same.

02 / Paid ads

Compare founder story against customer voice ad

Your founder-story ad ("For years, my natural hair was stuck.") and your customer-voice ad ("Alodia has CHANGED how my hair feels.") both send people to the same system page and offer. If you have not already compared them at equal spend, a clean split would show which opening brings purchases at lower cost.

One change

Untested proposal. Run the two existing ads unchanged to the same system page, with equal budget and an even audience split. The videos and copy both differ, so this compares the whole ads; leave out or match the auto-generated versions of both ads.

03 / Your store

Test a masque pairing on the Nourish oil page

The oil page's use steps point to Creme Shampoo, and the sampled page text shows no masque suggestion near the purchase button, though the homepage loads Rebuy, a cross-sell app, so a widget we did not capture may show one. A masque reviewer calls the masque with the oil "an amazing length retention combo".

One change

Untested proposal. First check whether Rebuy already shows a masque offer on this page. Variant adds a block below Add to cart: "Pair it with the Deep Conditioning Masque. Add both in one click." The masque is not added by default. Control keeps the page as is. Randomize oil-page visitors 50/50.
