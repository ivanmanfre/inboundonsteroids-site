01 / Proposed paid test

Test outfit-versatility versus saving-led bundle ads

Your bundles page says to "Elevate and expand your holiday wardrobe", while listings also show a saving, such as Dawn x Eclipse at £125 against £148. It is unresolved whether packing-light versatility or the price saving is the stronger reason for a new visitor to buy a bundle.

One change

Proposed new ad test, not an observed live ad. Run a Meta split test with equal budgets and the same audience. Both ads show Dawn x Eclipse and link to the Bikini Bundles collection page. Ad A: "One bundle, endless outfits. Pack lighter and wear it beach to bar." Ad B: "Dawn x Eclipse: £125, £23 less than buying the three pieces separately."

02 / Your store

Test a how-it-fits panel on the Dawn x Eclipse set page

The Dawn x Eclipse set page lists its three pieces but tells shoppers to "Check the details on individual item pages". How they connect sits elsewhere: your Dawn page says it is compatible with the Eclipse top only and attaches to its push-down rings.

One change

Add a short panel above Add to cart on the Dawn x Eclipse set page: 'Your set: Dawn multiway piece (wear as a top or sarong), Eclipse reversible top, Eclipse reversible bottoms. Dawn attaches to the Eclipse top's push-down rings.' Keep the rest of the page identical.

03 / Your store

Test a top-alone versus full-set choice on the Eclipse top page

Your Eclipse top page says it is compatible with the Dawn top and Luna skirt, and your shop lists a Dawn x Eclipse set at £125 against £148. In the Eclipse page text we read, we did not find this set or its £125 price near Add to cart; a general bundles link may appear elsewhere on the page.

One change

Add a 'Complete the set' block under Add to cart on the Eclipse top page, shown to half of visitors at random. Draft: 'Top only £48, or the full Dawn x Eclipse set (Dawn top & sarong, Eclipse top and bottoms) for £125 instead of £148.'
