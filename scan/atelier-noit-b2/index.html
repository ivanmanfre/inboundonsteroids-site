01 / Proposed paid test

Test mini-candle-first ads against scent-story ads

A new shopper cannot smell a candle online, so your homepage offer of a £22 mini redeemable against a full size candle is a lower-risk ad message worth testing. A lone mini pays £3.95 UK shipping under your £45 free-delivery threshold, so watch whether that holds it back.

One change

Run two UK ads. One says "Not sure it's your scent? Try Flowers of Evil as a £22 mini, then redeem it against a full size candle." and links to the Flowers of Evil mini page. The other says "Flowers of Evil. An intoxicating floral with powdery orris, warm amber and a trace of spice." and links to the full-size page. Match the offer to your live redemption terms.

02 / Your store

Test a Nuit de Jasmin mini-first line beside Add to Bag

A shopper on the Nuit de Jasmin candle page must commit £55.00 at Add to Bag without smelling it, though the page lists the matching mini at £22.00 further down. Your homepage says a mini can be redeemed against a full size candle, so naming that route at the buy button is worth testing.

One change

Add one line under Add to Bag on the Nuit de Jasmin candle page: "Not sure yet? Try the Nuit de Jasmin mini first, then redeem it against a full size candle." Link 'the Nuit de Jasmin mini' to its mini candle page and match the line to the live redemption terms.
