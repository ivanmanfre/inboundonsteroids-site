01 / Your store

Test preselecting the one-month plan for new visitors

Your page marks the 3-month plan, billed $207 every 72 days, as most popular, while the 1-month box holds 24 sachets, matching the 24 days of use the guarantee asks first-time buyers to try. With the 90-day guarantee already shown, the open question is whether a smaller first charge that covers that trial changes overall revenue.

One change

Preselect the 1-month plan for new visitors, with the line 'Start with one box: enough for the 24 days our guarantee asks first-time buyers to try,' and keep the 3-month plan one click away.

02 / Your store

Offer Axolt Lift as an add-on on the Axolt product page after launch

Lift is not on sale yet: your homepage says it launches on 23 November and calls Axolt the everyday base. Once Lift sells, the open question is whether Axolt shoppers will add it alongside the daily sachet or swap one for the other.

One change

After launch, add an optional Lift add-on beside the plan choice on the Axolt product page. We did not see Lift's price, pack or checkout. Draft: 'Keep Axolt as your everyday base. Add Lift for the days that ask more: chewable tablets with caffeine and L-theanine.'
