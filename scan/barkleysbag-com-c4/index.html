01 / Paid ads

Test a won't-eat refund line in the freezer ad

The marrow page shows "30-day refund If your dog won't eat it", while the refund policy lists "Perishable goods (including frozen raw food and bones)" as non-returnable. These may cover different things, but a shopper could be unsure what the guarantee covers. A recent review on the page reads "Sadly my pup will not eat them. Money down the drain.." That is the worry the guarantee answers, yet none of the marrow ad texts we read mention the refund. We picked the freezer ad because it asks new shoppers to keep bones frozen and on hand before they know their dog will eat one. That is when a won't-eat refund matters most.

“belongs in the freezer”

One changeIf you refund without a return, say so on the policy page first. Then keep the same landing page, video, copy and 40% offer, and add only this line before the offer: "Dog won't eat it? Get a refund within 30 days." The variant keeps the freezer ad text word for word, from "Your food isn’t the only thing that belongs in the freezer." to "Try Barkley’s for 40% off your first order." Control is your current freezer ad. If you run this copy in more than one ad, pick one, duplicate it, and change only the added line. Give each version its own utm_content tag so orders and refund requests can be matched to the version that drove them. Run both versions in one A/B test at equal spend.

02 / Paid ads

Open the marrow ad with a customer's busy-time quote

The marrow ad that opens "Got a dog that never seems to run out of energy?" says "Keeps dogs happily occupied", as does the freezer ad. The 'simplest ones' ad says "Helps keep dogs happily occupied". None of these three marrow ads says how long. Your marrow page holds a review reading "Keeps them busy for a couple of hours". The review gives a time the ad copy does not.

From your public ad

“Keeps dogs happily occupied”

One changeKeep the same landing page and 40% first-order offer. Replace the opening line "Got a dog that never seems to run out of energy?" with the quote line. Variant: "'Keeps them busy for a couple of hours!' A customer review. Barkley’s Raw Beef Marrow Bones give them something worthwhile to settle in with. ✅ 100% raw beef ✅ One simple ingredient ✅ Keeps dogs happily occupied Perfect for the dogs that always need something to do. Try Barkley’s for 40% off your first order." Add "Always supervise while they chew." to both versions, so the opening line is the only difference between them. 03 / Your store

Test a free gift on the 3-bag subscription

On the marrow bone page, the 3-bag option shows "3 Bags 6LB total Save 15%". The page also says "Choose the 6 bag option to unlock 3 FREE gifts". Deliveries run "Delivered every 4 weeks 6 weeks".

One changeOffer one of the existing free gifts on the 3-bag subscription, with a line like "Subscribe to 3 bags and get a free gift on delivery 2". Control keeps gifts at 6 bags only. Split visitors 50/50 on the marrow bone page.
Sources & how to check the result +

What to measure
Winning metric: 12-week subscriber revenue per visitor, net of gift cost. Guardrails: first-order revenue per visitor and 6-bag share. Also watch: cancellations before delivery 2.
