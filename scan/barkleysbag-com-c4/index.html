01 / Paid ads

Make the refund promise clear before using it in ads

Your marrow page offers a 30-day refund if a dog will not eat the bones.

The policy describes refunds after returned items are inspected, while listing bones as non-returnable.

The freezer ad's text does not mention the guarantee.

One change

Proposed test: clarify the 30-day refund terms for non-returnable food on the policy page. Then add "Dog won't eat it? Get a refund within 30 days." before the freezer ad's 40% offer. Keep the video, product page and remaining ad copy unchanged.

02 / Paid ads

Test a customer quote as the marrow ad's first line

The marrow ad's text says "Keeps dogs happily occupied", without giving a duration.

A product-page review gives a more specific experience: "Keeps them busy for a couple of hours".

One change

Proposed test: With the reviewer's permission, replace "Got a dog that never seems to run out of energy?" with "Keeps them busy for a couple of hours", attributed as a customer review. Keep the page and offer fixed. Add "Always supervise while they chew." to both versions.
