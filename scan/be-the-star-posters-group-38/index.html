01 / Proposed paid test

Test the chant line against buy 2, get 1 free in new Villa ads

Your second best-seller, the Villa chant poster, starts at £7. Its page says "Bring the sound of Villa Park to your wall" and also offers buy 2, get 1 free. It is not known which of the two messages earns more profit from new ad spend.

One change

Proposed new Meta ad test, not an observed ad. Run two ads for UK Aston Villa fans, both linking to the Villa chant poster page. Ad A: "Bring the sound of Villa Park to your wall. Shop the Allez Allez Allez poster." Ad B: "Buy 2 posters, get 1 unframed free. Start your Villa wall." Only the message differs.

02 / Your store

Show the other Scotland matchday prints on the Haiti print page

Your Scotland v Haiti print page says "Collect the full series" and offers buy 2, get 1 free. When we loaded it, its "Perfect Match With" block showed the squad montage, lion crest and iconic goals posters, and the page linked to none of the other matchday prints such as Brazil or Morocco.

One change

Proposed, untested: add the other Scotland matchday prints, such as Brazil and Morocco, as a small linked set under the size picker. Draft line: "Build your Scotland World Cup set. Buy any 2 posters and your third unframed one is free."
