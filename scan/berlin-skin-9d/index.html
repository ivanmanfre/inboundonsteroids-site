01 / Proposed paid test

Test redness versus winter dryness ad angles

Your Blue Tansy Cream page states it is made "to reduce redness, calm irritation", while its review summary calls it popular for "winter dryness and harsh climates". We could not read your ad libraries, so we don't know which reason you lead with now; this test compares the two directly.

One change

Proposed new paid ad test, not an observed live ad. Run two creatives to the same Blue Tansy Cream page. Draft A: "Blue Tansy Cream: a calming botanical moisturizer made to reduce redness and calm irritation." Draft B: "Blue Tansy Cream: a rich botanical cream customers reach for in cold, dry winters." Only the message differs.

02 / Your store

Add a Clear Skin Bundle choice to the Sandalwood Cream page

Your Sandalwood Cream page lists Normal, Oily and Acne skin types, and your bundle page's Clear Skin option adds a mask, toner and cleanser to the same cream on a separate page. That page's description calls it "Clean Skin Bundle", so align the names before linking to it.

One change

Under Add to cart, show two choices: 'Just the cream' or 'Build the Clear Skin routine', which opens the bundle page with Clear Skin preselected. Draft: 'Start with the cream, or get the full routine in one order.' The control keeps the current page.
