01 / Paid ads

Send the coffee ad to the starter kit

The ad opening 'Keep the coffee' sends people to the $72 Chocolate Delight can page, and none of the 11 recorded ads link straight to your $95 starter kit page. The kit adds a whisk, a 4-sachet sampler and a gift box, but the can page's 30-day guarantee excludes starter kits.

One change

Duplicate the 'Keep the coffee' ad with identical copy and creative in a Meta A/B test, using the same audience, budget and dates. One version links to the current Chocolate Delight page, the other to the starter kit page with the Chocolate Delight Hot Ritual already selected.

02 / Your store

Correct the kit can's serve count and link to refills

Your kit page calls the can a 30-day supply, but the product panel lists 19 servings, so with the 4 sachets a daily user runs out in about 23 days. Your subscription page offers 10% off, free NZ shipping and flavour swaps, but the kit page we read does not mention it.

One change

Change the kit can label from "30-day supply" to 19 daily serves for every visitor. Then randomly show half of kit page visitors a line linking to your subscription page, while the other half sees no such line. Draft: "Your can holds 19 daily serves. Keep your ritual going with 10% off and free NZ shipping, and pause, skip or swap any time."

Full test plan & sources +

What we saw
Your kit page calls the can a 30-day supply, but the product panel lists 19 servings, so with the 4 sachets a daily user runs out in about 23 days. Your subscription page offers 10% off, free NZ shipping and flavour swaps, but the kit page we read does not mention it.

What to measure
Full-size can orders placed after the kit order, one-off or subscription, within 90 days, per kit page visitor in each group. Also watch kit sales, refund requests and subscription cancellations.

Test setup
Change the kit can label from "30-day supply" to 19 daily serves for every visitor. Then randomly show half of kit page visitors a line linking to your subscription page, while the other half sees no such line. Draft: "Your can holds 19 daily serves. Keep your ritual going with 10% off and free NZ shipping, and pause, skip or swap any time."
