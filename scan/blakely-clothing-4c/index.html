Finding 1 · Shopper paths

No express checkout button on Ultimate Active Leggings - Black

A shopper on Ultimate Active Leggings - Black has to add to cart, open the cart and start checkout before they can pay. There's no one-tap button for shoppers who saved their card or use a phone wallet.

Paid-media check

Testing sprawl with no consolidated winner

You have 5651 active ads spread across 43 distinct angles. Testing that wide without a consolidated winner spreads budget thin and makes performance hard to read. Pruning to the proven angles and concentrating spend behind them is the lever.

Most of your creative is dynamic catalog

Of the 120 ads we sampled, 80.8% run in a dynamic catalog format. When most of the creative is feed-driven, product feed quality becomes the creative that decides performance. Tightening feed titles, imagery, and hero products is the fastest win here.
