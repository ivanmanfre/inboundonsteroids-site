01 / Your store

Test a standard versus Off-Grid filter chooser

Your FAQ states the Off-Grid filter is NSF-certified while the standard 0.2 Micron Filter is not, and says to choose by source water and certification needs. The sampled standard pack page text gives only a general FAQ pointer, with no side-by-side choice.

One change

On the standard 3-stage 5-inch pack page, add a short two-column comparison above the description, linking to the Off-Grid pack. Draft: "Standard: 0.2 micron filter, FDA-approved materials per manufacturer. Off-Grid: NSF-certified, lab-tested, built for sources like lakes or streams. Not sure? See which fits your water source." Link that last line to the FAQ answer comparing the 0.2 Micron and Off-Grid filters.

02 / Your store

Test delivery-interval guidance on the filter subscription page

Your subscription page offers 20% off and free shipping, with delivery every 3, 6 or 9 months, and the sampled text shows no help choosing an interval. Your two 5-inch 3-stage pack pages say that with the sediment pre-filter you get 3-4 months of clean water, depending on water cloudiness.

One change

When a 5-inch 3-stage pack is selected, add one line under the frequency selector that echoes those pack pages. Draft: "With the sediment pre-filter, 5-inch 3-stage packs give 3-4 months of clean water, depending on how cloudy your water is. Every 3 months matches that." Add 6- or 9-month advice only if your team confirms it for part-time use.
