01 / Paid ads

Name the free gifts in the Welcome Kit ad

Your Welcome Kit ad's text says "receive free gifts with your order" without naming them. The page it leads to lists a Forever Tin and 5 laundry samples, so the ad copy can name items the destination already supports.

One change

Duplicate the ad and, in the copy only, replace the opening line with "Get our Dishwasher Welcome Kit with a free reusable Forever Tin plus 5 laundry samples. 🎁" Keep the image, headline, remaining text and link identical. Another ad to this page is a dynamic creative ad whose copy we couldn't read; if it already names the gifts, review its results first.

02 / Your store

Spell out autoship price after the first box

On the Clean Suite Kit page, autoship costs $88 today, then $105.57 every 3 months, shown as a separate 'Recurring' figure. The second charge is higher partly because autoship takes 20% off the first delivery but 10% off refills, which a buyer may not expect.

One change

Add one plain line under the autoship option: "Pay $88 today, then $105.57 every 3 months. Delay or cancel anytime." It joins the two figures already shown into one sentence; randomly split kit-page visitors so half still see the current layout.

03 / Your store

Test kit-first versus build-your-own as the homepage starting route

The homepage offers ready-made starter kits and a Build Your Bundle path, and the bundle page states a saving of up to 15%, or 35% with a subscription. Which route to lead with matters most for visitors who land on the homepage rather than an ad landing page.

One change

Add one lead button under the homepage hero and randomly split visitors: half see Starter Kits with its current line, "Everything you need to make the switch"; half see Build Your Bundle with "Pick only what you use: save up to 15%, or 35% with autoship." Destination and message both change, so a winner shows which button-and-message pair sells better, not the route alone.

What we saw
The homepage offers ready-made starter kits and a Build Your Bundle path, and the bundle page states a saving of up to 15%, or 35% with a subscription. Which route to lead with matters most for visitors who land on the homepage rather than an ad landing page.

What to measure
First-order purchases and revenue per homepage visitor in each version. Also check autoship sign-up share, average order margin, and that 100-day returns do not rise.
