01 / Proposed paid test

Test a sensitive-skin ad against a natural-glow ad

Your FAQ says BONNIE was "formulated specifically with sensitive skin in mind", and a recent homepage review from an eczema-prone shopper says it did not flare up their eczema. A cold ad has to open with either skin comfort or the glow result, and that choice is worth testing for first orders.

One change

Proposed new ad test, not an observed live ad. Both versions send to the GOLDEN product page. Version A: "A gradual tan made with sensitive skin in mind. Hydrating GOLDEN lotion, skin-first. Shop GOLDEN." Version B: "Build your glow at your own pace. GOLDEN gradual lotion for natural-looking colour. Shop GOLDEN." Run it as a Meta split test: one cold audience split evenly, equal budgets, same visual.

What we saw
Your FAQ says BONNIE was "formulated specifically with sensitive skin in mind", and a recent homepage review from an eczema-prone shopper says it did not flare up their eczema. A cold ad has to open with either skin comfort or the glow result, and that choice is worth testing for first orders.

02 / Your store

Caption the Duo and Trio by routine goal

The rendered GOLDEN page shows two bundle add buttons under "Complete the look", named only by their product lists. A shopper must work out alone whether to buy one lotion, a face-and-body pair, or a set that includes a moisturiser.

One change

Keep the same two bundles and prices, but caption the Duo "Face + body: build your glow head to toe" and the Trio "Build it, then keep it: includes GLAZED moisturiser to maintain your tan". The control keeps the current labels. Randomise GOLDEN page visitors 50/50.

What we saw
The rendered GOLDEN page shows two bundle add buttons under "Complete the look", named only by their product lists. A shopper must work out alone whether to buy one lotion, a face-and-body pair, or a set that includes a moisturiser.

03 / Your store

Suggest GLAZED in cart for single-tan orders

A shopper buying only GOLDEN or GLOW still has a separate decision about keeping the tan going. Your FAQ states "most people reapply every 2–3 days and use GLAZED", and the GLAZED page calls it a tan extender. Cart behaviour was not sampled.

One change

For carts containing only GOLDEN or GLOW, show a GLAZED add line reading "Keep your tan going: GLAZED is the moisturiser BONNIE suggests between tan applications." The control shows no extra line. Randomise eligible carts 50/50 and report GLOW-only carts separately.
