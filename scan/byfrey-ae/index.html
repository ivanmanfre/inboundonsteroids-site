01 / Proposed paid test

Sell newborn and 3-month buyers different first kits

Your Newborn Kit costs €45 and says “Fragrance-free. For sensitive skin, from 0 months.” The Wash & Care Bundle also costs €45, but its All-Round Wash is described on your Routine Bundle page as a shampoo and body wash “for skin and hair from 3 months”. The €65 Routine Bundle includes that same wash. Each €45 kit fits a different stage of a baby’s life, which gives a paid campaign two clear, honest offers to lead with.

One changeProposed paid test, not yet run: two German Meta routes with the baby’s age stated in the ad and on the page. Route A: “from the first bath, 0 months” to the Newborn Kit. Route B: “from 3 months, head-to-toe wash and care” to the Wash & Care Bundle. Keep budget rules and geography the same. The routes sell different products to different parents, so judge each on its own purchase economics before scaling either. On the call we would check which age groups and margins matter most to you.

02 / Your store

Offer the €65 Routine Bundle as the Wash & Care upgrade

The Wash & Care Bundle costs €45. Your FAQ says German orders under €55 pay €4.99 shipping, so it arrives at €49.99. The €65 Routine Bundle adds The No-Wash Wash, which sells for €22 on its own, and ships free. For a German buyer, moving from Wash & Care to the full routine costs €15.01 more, delivered. The three products cost €72 when bought separately.

One changeOn the Wash & Care page or in the cart, test one optional upgrade against the current page: “Add the bath additive and get the complete routine: €65 with free German shipping.” Show what each bundle contains and the delivered total for each, and keep the €45 choice. The cart’s recommendation area was still loading when we checked, so first confirm it does not already suggest this.

03 / Your store

Turn the routine add-ons into two age-labelled kits

The Oily One costs €28. Its “Complete your routine” block lists The No-Wash Wash and The All-Round Wash as separate €22 add-ons. You already sell each pairing as a €45 kit, €5 cheaper than buying them apart: the Newborn Kit (from 0 months) and the Wash & Care Bundle. The No-Wash Wash page, sold “from 0 months on”, also recommends The All-Round Wash with no age shown, although your Routine Bundle page says it is for babies from 3 months.

One changeOn The Oily One page, test two kit choices in that block against the current single-product cards: “From 0 months: Newborn Kit, €45” and “From 3 months: Wash & Care, €45.” Keep the oil-only purchase. On The No-Wash Wash page, show the age on The All-Round Wash card. Neither kit reaches the €55 free-shipping level, so present the €5 kit saving only.
