What we’d test first

01

Test autumn sun versus plant-based sourcing ad messages

Your homepage sells D3+K2 on weak autumn sun, and your product page also stresses algae-sourced D3 instead of lanolin. The five active Meta ads we read all sell magnesium, and we did not read the text of your other three active ads or past ads, so we found no ad testing which D3+K2 reason wins first orders.

Proposed test

Proposed new Meta ad test, not an observed live ad. Run two ads to the same D3+K2 product page. Ad A, season: "Your daily dose of sunshine, sorted for autumn. Plant-based D3+K2 softgels, lab-tested in Europe." Ad B, sourcing: "Vitamin D3 without the sheep's wool. Ours comes from algae, with K2 from natto." Keep audience and budget identical. Write both in Dutch, like your current ads.

How we’d measure it

Winner: new-customer first-order revenue per ad euro, same window, counted in your store from each ad's tracked link, in a Meta A/B split. Clicks are clues only. Watch guarantee refunds.

02

Match the D3 + K2 delivery gap to daily dose

Your D3 + K2 page offers delivery gaps from 4 weeks upward, with 60 softgels per pack and a dose of 1 to 2 softgels. Nothing in the sampled text links the dose to a gap, so a buyer has to work out which delivery gap suits them.

Proposed test

Show one line above the delivery picker to a random half of D3 + K2 product-page visitors: "1 a day? Choose every 8 weeks. 2 a day? Choose every 4 weeks." The other half see the page as it is now.

How we’d measure it

Split product-page visitors 50/50. Winner: revenue per visitor, renewals included, over the same 13 weeks for both versions. Subscription starts are early clues. Watch refunds and cancellations.
