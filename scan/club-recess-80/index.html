01 / Paid ads

Lead the dress ad with the easy-off shortie

Both Monaco Teal dress ads we read open with a lifestyle line ("A busy-day outfit formula", "The mom-on-the-move dress") and mention the easy-off shortie later, while your page calls it "The one everyone asks us about." These ads can rotate several text versions and we saw one each, so whether any opens with the shortie was not checked.

One change

Use a Meta A/B test to split one audience at random between two ads with the same dress page link, image and budget, changing only the opening line. Proposed, untested variant: "The athletic dress with a shortie that slides off without taking the dress with it. Pockets for a phone and a ball."

02 / Paid ads

Test a matching-set message in the tank ad

The navy Scoop Tank ad leads with a built-in bra review and no set, and your Bright White tank ads pair it with the Baseline Short. Your navy tank page says "Wear it with the Heritage Pleated Skirt as a set", and tank plus skirt is $156, over the $150 free-shipping line, so a skirt-set message is worth comparing.

One change

Keep the tank page link and creative the same, and change only the ad copy. Variant draft: "Start with the tank, finish with the set. Built-in bra, soft EveryHour knit, and a Heritage Pleated Skirt that matches."

03 / Your store

Offer a one-click paddle cover on paddle pages

On the sampled Barton Springs paddle page, the FAQ says "Covers are sold separately", so the paddle and cover are two separate decisions. The store lists a low-priced cover, so offering it at the moment of paddle choice may be worth testing.

One change

After checking that no existing cross-sell already offers the cover, randomize visitors to Classic paddle pages 50/50, keeping the control, paddle price and returns wording unchanged. The proposed, untested variant adds an optional tick box above Add to cart: "Protect the spin surface: add a Classic Paddle Cover for $12."
