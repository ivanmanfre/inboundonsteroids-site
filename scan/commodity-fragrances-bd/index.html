01 / Paid ads

Open the Ice(d) travel-size ad on the 10ml size

This Ice(d) ad promises travel sizes, but its landing page shows $160 beside Add to bag, with 10ml listed as one of three size choices. Matching the first price a click sees to the ad's travel-size promise is worth testing.

One change

Keep the Ice(d) ad unchanged. Send a random half of its clicks to the same Ice(d) page opened with the 10ml size already selected; the other half keep the current link.

02 / Your store

Put a try-first kit choice beside the Milk size options

Your returns policy gives full refunds only for unopened fragrances and suggests trying a Discovery Kit first. On the Milk page, the $18 Milk Scent Space Kit and its matching credit appear below the size choice and Add to bag, so offering this try-first route nearer the decision is worth testing alongside the existing 10ml size.

One change

Add one line directly under the Milk size choice linking to the Milk Scent Space Kit. Draft: "Not sure yet? Try Milk first in the $18 Milk Scent Space Kit. What you pay comes back as a code toward a 100ml bottle on our site, valid for 60 days."
