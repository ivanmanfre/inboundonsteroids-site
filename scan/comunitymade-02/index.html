01 / Proposed paid test

Test craft story against free exchange ad message

Women's Mateo, your top best seller, pairs a story of shoes handcrafted in L.A. from Italian leather with a free 30-day exchange promise, while its size guide notes an everyday rather than athletic fit. It is unresolved which reason should open an ad for new US shoppers, and no live Meta or Google ads were found.

One change

Run two new Meta ads to US shoppers new to the brand, both linking to the same Women's Mateo page: one saying "Handcrafted in Los Angeles. Italian leather, made to last." and one saying "Not sure on size? Free exchanges within 30 days on US orders."

02 / Your store

Make the extra laces a visible Westsider add-on

Your men's Westsider page offers an extra pair of self-colored laces for $20, requested through an order note, while the catalog already lists $20 Leather Laces as a product. On this page the add-on still depends on a manual step, and how many shoppers use it is unknown.

One change

Show an optional 'Add matching laces' choice with its $20 price near Add to cart on the men's Westsider page, using the existing $20 Leather Laces product or a self-colored version sold the same way.

Full test plan & sources +

What we saw
Your men's Westsider page offers an extra pair of self-colored laces for $20, requested through an order note, while the catalog already lists $20 Leather Laces as a product. On this page the add-on still depends on a manual step, and how many shoppers use it is unknown.

What to measure
Revenue per visitor versus the current page, with visitors randomized between versions over the same four weeks. Also check purchase rate, lace add-on rate and return requests.

Test setup
Show an optional 'Add matching laces' choice with its $20 price near Add to cart on the men's Westsider page, using the existing $20 Leather Laces product or a self-colored version sold the same way.

03 / Your store

Headline the fee credit on custom consultation bookings

Your Custom Shoe Consultation, fourth in your best-selling order, is a 30-minute in-person booking priced from $50, and the page says the fee is credited toward the final project. The credit sits in the description, so whether a short line beside the title helps shoppers commit to booking is an open question.

One change

Add one line beside the consultation title: 'Your consultation fee is credited toward your final custom shoe project.'

What we saw
Your Custom Shoe Consultation, fourth in your best-selling order, is a 30-minute in-person booking priced from $50, and the page says the fee is credited toward the final project. What to measure
