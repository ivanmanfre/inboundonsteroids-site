01 / Paid ads

Test a 7-week Flawless Defence refill to match about 50 days of daily use

Flawless Defence says 'Use daily' and 'Approximately 50 applications per bottle', but its subscription cites 'around 60 days of application' and ships every 2 months, so daily users may run out about 6 to 10 days early. The three Meta ads we read promote '40% off subscriptions', and subscribers who run short may reschedule, buy singles or cancel.

One change

Randomise Flawless Defence product page visitors between today's every 2 months subscription (A) and an every 7 weeks plan (B), as an untested bet that matching about 50 daily applications lifts profit per visitor. Keep the same price and 40% then 25% discounts, replace 'around 60 days of application' in B with 7 week wording, and count extra shipping if B ships apart from 8 week supplement deliveries.

What we saw
Flawless Defence says 'Use daily' and 'Approximately 50 applications per bottle', but its subscription cites 'around 60 days of application' and ships every 2 months, so daily users may run out about 6 to 10 days early. The three Meta ads we read promote '40% off subscriptions', and subscribers who run short may reschedule, buy singles or cancel.

What to measure
Decide on gross profit after discounts, refunds and shipping per randomised visitor, buyer or not, over the same 24 weeks. Keep each visitor's version sitewide. Report cancellations, reschedules and singles.

Test setup
