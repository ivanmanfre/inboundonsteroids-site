01 / Paid ads

Test a routine-led version of your baby ad

Your baby ad leads with ingredient count, but its collection promises "your complete baby routine, from bubbles to bedtime", with gift sets at $39.99 and $74.99. Other baby items cost $13.99 to $15.99, so a routine message may lead shoppers to add items toward your $49.95 free-shipping line.

One change

Keep the baby collection as the destination. Run the current ingredient-led ad against a routine-led version in a Meta A/B test, changing only the primary text. Draft: "Bubbles to bedtime, all in one. One fragrance-free baby routine, from bath to lotion."

02 / Paid ads

Send your mix-and-match ad straight to the Custom Bath Gift Set

Your ad promises seven scents to "make their own" and lands on the gift sets collection, which offers "mixable fun with curated sets" across eleven listed products. Your Custom Bath Gift Set page, listed in that collection, uses the ad's own words: "mix, match, and make bath time their own".

One change

This is a route test, not a copy test. Run the ad with unchanged copy as a Meta A/B test, splitting traffic between the current gift sets collection and the Custom Bath Gift Set page. If this ad targets families buying for themselves, run the same test with the Custom Bath Bundle page instead.

03 / Your store

Pre-fill a suggested trio in the custom gift set

Your Custom Bath Gift Set opens with "Choose Your 3 Bottles. This step is required" across twelve bottle options. Your store also sells pre-built sets ("Bath Gift Sets From $29.97"), and your homepage suggests a three-product "Get This Bath Time Blend".

One change

Inside the build-your-own flow, show half of visitors three suggested bottles already selected, labelled "Our starter trio, swap any bottle". The other half see the current empty picker. Choose the suggested scents from your own sales data.

04 / Your store

Test a Detangling Duo card on the conditioner page

Your conditioner page's pairing block shows only the Leave-In Spray Detangler, while your Cherry on Top® Detangling Duo Bundle ($22.99) sits elsewhere in the store.

One change

If the Detangling Duo is the conditioner plus the spray (its $22.99 price sits just under the $23.98 the two cost separately), show half of visitors a bundle card in place of the spray card, worded "Get both together for $22.99". The other half see the current spray card.
