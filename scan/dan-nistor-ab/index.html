Write your Shopify Plus migration brief before you ask any agency for a quote.

Without a brief, each quote prices a slightly different project. One agency assumes order history moves. Another leaves it behind. Then you end up comparing prices for different jobs.

A useful brief answers five questions:

  • What must not break on launch day?
  • Which data moves and which stays?
  • Where does every old URL redirect?
  • Which apps stay, change or go?
  • Who signs off on each decision?

I heard a line on David Senra's Founders Podcast that fits migrations well: "Action produces information."

So before the full move, run a small test. Move a sample of products, customers and orders. You find the problems while they are still small and easy to fix.

Here is a Shopify Plus migration brief you can fill in before you talk to any agency.

It comes with two worked examples, so you can see what a finished brief looks like.

Example one: say, a fashion retailer on Magento 2 with 5,000 products and stores for Ireland and the UK.

Example two: say, a skincare brand on WooCommerce with 200 products and a subscription offer.

Each brief covers:

  • What must not break on launch day
  • The data that moves and what stays behind
  • Redirects for every old URL
  • Apps and integrations, with what replaces each one
  • A small test migration before the full move
  • Launch week checks and who signs off

The subscription example also shows how to move active subscribers without breaking their next renewal.
