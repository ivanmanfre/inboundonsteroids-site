01 / Proposed paid test

Test itch-relief versus easy-dosing ad messages for NO-ITCH

Your NO-ITCH page states it uses natural herbal extracts to calm seasonal itching. It also says the pump bottle makes dosing simple. That gives two different ad angles, the problem and the ease of use, that can both point to one page.

One change

Proposed new Meta ad test. Run two Meta image ads with equal budget to the same NO-ITCH product page. Ad A draft: "Itchy season again? Meet NO-ITCH, an herbal liquid for dogs." Ad B draft: "Herbal itch support in a pump bottle that keeps dosing simple." Only the message changes.

02 / Your store

Test a NO-ITCH customer comment on its product page

Your NO-ITCH page shows "There are no reviews yet." Your homepage carries a customer testimonial about NO-ITCH, which could be tried on the product page itself.

One change

The variant adds the homepage testimonial below the size choices on the NO-ITCH page, labelled as a customer comment. The control stays as it is. Confirm you have permission to reuse it, and treat it as the customer's own statement.
