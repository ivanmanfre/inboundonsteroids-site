01 / Paid ads

Confirm the Costco city on Costco ad landing pages

Six Costco city ads we saw send clicks to product pages that never mention Costco or the city. Five go to the Sparkling 12oz page ($87 for 36 cans) and Ogden goes to Lemonade Salt drink mix, so a visitor looking for it in store gets no confirmation, though the page could still sell delivery.

One change

Give each Costco city ad its own tagged link to the same page, for example &costco=tulsa added to the existing link, and show a short banner only to tagged visitors. Draft: 'Now at Costco [city]: look for [product stocked there]. Prefer delivery? Order below.' Confirm which product each warehouse stocks before naming it, since we did not check this.

02 / Your store

Tell bundle shoppers how long 120 sticks lasts

The INSIDER Bundle subscription defaults to delivery every month for 120 sticks, about 4 a day, and the page does not say how long a bundle lasts. Your page states many folks find 1-2 sticks a day optimal and others use more, so the monthly default may not fit some buyers.

One change

Next to the delivery timing, test a short note against the current page, which already says subscriptions can be modified anytime. Draft: 'Many folks find 1-2 sticks a day works, so 4 boxes last about 2 to 4 months for one person. Sharing or using more? Keep monthly, or change timing anytime in your account.'

What we saw
The INSIDER Bundle subscription defaults to delivery every month for 120 sticks, about 4 a day, and the page does not say how long a bundle lasts. Your page states many folks find 1-2 sticks a day optimal and others use more, so the monthly default may not fit some buyers.

What to measure
Winner: subscription revenue per bundle-page visitor over 6 months, same for both versions. It weighs longer gaps against cancellations. Slow read; cancellations at 60 days are an early signal only.

Test setup
Next to the delivery timing, test a short note against the current page, which already says subscriptions can be modified anytime. Draft: 'Many folks find 1-2 sticks a day works, so 4 boxes last about 2 to 4 months for one person. Sharing or using more? Keep monthly, or change timing anytime in your account.'
