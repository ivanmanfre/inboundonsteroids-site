01 / Proposed paid test

Test a skin-tone concealment ad against a waste-saving ad

Your homepage says 1 bottle replaces 100+ sticker bandages, but its FAQ names color as the difference from other liquid bandages, and your Sand page describes a shade made to blend with natural skin tone. Both reasons to buy are on your site, so it is unclear which one works better as the first thing a new ad viewer sees.

One change

Proposed new Meta ad test, not an observed live ad. Use Meta's A/B test so each person sees only one ad, with equal budget and the same homepage. A: "A bandage that blends in. Brush on a skin-tone shade and keep going." B: "One bottle, 100+ bandages. Brush on, let dry, skip the sticker waste."

02 / Your store

Add a shade-matching guide to the Sand product page

One review on your Sand page says "The current selection is way too dark or tanned." That is a single comment, not a trend, but it suggests some very fair shoppers may want a lighter shade than Sand. Your pages already name shades by skin tone and list shade-range 2-packs.

One change

Add a short shade guide under the Sand price, with labelled Sand, Honey and Mocha swatches. Draft line: "Our lightest skin-tone shade. Between two tones? Choose the 2-pack that spans your two closest shades." Link to the matching 2-pack.

03 / Your store

Offer a second shade at the free-shipping line

Your FAQ states free US shipping on orders of $12 or more, while single-shade bottles such as Hibiscus Pink are listed at $10.00. A one-bottle order sits below that line, so it is unclear whether a second-bottle prompt beside the buy button would lift order value.

One change

On single-bottle pages, add a line beside the buy button. Draft: "Free US shipping starts at $12. Make it a 2-pack for $15 and you qualify." Link to a 2-pack that includes this shade.
