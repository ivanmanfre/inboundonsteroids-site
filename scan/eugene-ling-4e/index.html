What a buyer sees on your profile

Before someone books a call with you, they open your LinkedIn profile to check who you are and what you do. This is what they find on yours today, and what to change so they book.

  1. How a buyer books

    Add a Calendly booking link to your website field and to the end of your About. Close the About with: Book a thirty minute brand call with me at this link.

    Your profile lists no website, and your headline and About carry no link.

  2. Headline

    Rewrite it to name the buyer and the service: Brand strategy and identity for startup and consumer brand founders | Founder @ Sunny Side Up | Ex-Uber Creative Lead

    Your headline reads: Founder @ Sunny Side Up, Ex-Uber Creative Lead

  3. Banner

    Put one line of text over the ice image: Brand strategy and identity for startup and consumer brand founders. Add a second line: Book a brand call via the link on this profile.
