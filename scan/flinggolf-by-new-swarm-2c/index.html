Where we’d focus

What we’d test first

01

Bring your existing automated Fit Guide onto the Stinger product page

Your Stinger product page already links to the FlingStick Fit Guide. That guide offers an automated chooser and recommendations based on height and strength. Shoppers choose the Stinger length on the product page, while the guide is on a separate page.

Proposed test

Split Stinger page visitors evenly between the current page and a version with your existing Fit Guide chooser beside the size selector. Use its current height and strength recommendations to suggest a length. Keep the full guide link and let shoppers change the suggestion. Test bringing the existing guidance into the purchase flow.

How we’d measure it

Compare completed purchases per Stinger page visitor. Also track returns and exchanges for wrong length. Record picker use to understand the result; clicks alone do not establish a sales improvement.

02

Let Launch shoppers choose a designed FlingHead in the same purchase

The sampled Launch page tells buyers they can add a designed head: 'just add one to the cart', after choosing a Unique FlingHead on a separate page. The head upgrade is an optional second step away from the stick purchase.

Proposed test

Split Launch page visitors evenly between the current page and a version with a FlingHead picker beside the size selector. Show the default black head and available designed heads, with their actual additional prices. One Add to Cart action adds the stick and selected head. Keep the current fulfilment rule for replacing the default head.

How we’d measure it

Compare revenue per Launch page visitor. Check that the stick purchase rate holds and monitor incorrect head-swap orders. Record picker use to understand the result; clicks alone do not establish a sales improvement.
