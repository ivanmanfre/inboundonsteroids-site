01 / Paid ads

Test a specific sell-out line in your 'A little mystery' calendar ad

The "A little mystery" calendar ads we read say "Limited supply available." without giving any timing. Your calendar page states it "Ships now, sold out by November 5th last year" and that the 24-Day calendar has already sold out, so the ad may be able to carry a more specific scarcity line.

One change

Randomly split the same ad audience between the current ad and a copy variant. In the variant, replace "Limited supply available." with "Ships now. Last year it sold out by November 5th." Use the same fixed image set and the same 12-Day Advent Calendar page in both versions.

02 / Paid ads

Test a gifting angle against the countdown ad

Your recorded ad frames the calendar around making the days before the holidays delicious. Your product page describes it as "for gifting or treating yourself", so it is unclear which reason to buy works best in this ad.

One change

Randomly split the same audience between the current countdown ad and a gifting variant. Variant draft: "Hard-to-buy-for foodie? Give them 12 doors of Sichuan flavor surprises in a holiday gift box." Keep the image and product page the same, and run it at the same time on an audience kept separate from the sell-out line test, since the calendar may sell out within weeks.

03 / Your store

Test a three-flavor Trio option on the Original page

The Original Chili Crisp page sells 2-, 3- and 4-packs or a 16 oz jar, and its "Plays nice with" row shows other flavors' packs. The three-flavor Trio, your second best seller, sits on its own page, so choosing between single-flavor packs and a three-flavor set takes a separate click.

One change

Randomly show half of Original page visitors a short line beside the size choice: "Not sure which flavor? Try the Trio: Original, Sweet + Spicy and Xtra Crunchy in one gift-ready box." It links to the Trio page. Control keeps the current page.
