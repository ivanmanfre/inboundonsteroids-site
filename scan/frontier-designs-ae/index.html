01 / Paid ads

Show the wood kit as a clear package choice

On the Henry and Jackson pages, where recorded Meta ads send shoppers, the complete kit (save $250) is an optional add-on in step 3 (+$647 Henry, +$1,047 Jackson; the Logan and Jake share this block). The bundle page also promotes it, but we cannot tell whether buyers weigh kit against no kit before adding to cart.

One change

On the Henry and Jackson pages, when wood-burning is selected, replace the optional kit add-on in step 3 with two equal cards: "Fire pit only" and "Fire pit plus complete kit: lid, grate and cover, +$647, save $250" (+$1,047 on Jackson). Neither card is preselected. Randomly split each page's visitors between this and the current layout.

02 / Paid ads

Test a gas-led Logan ad against the current ad

Your homepage announces every pit is now available in gas, but the recorded text of the Logan Meta ad leads on transforming the outdoor space and Corten craftsmanship without naming the fuel choice. Gas Logans start at $6,273, so a message built around the new option is worth testing with paid traffic.

One change

Add a gas-led Logan ad on Meta and compare it with the current Logan ad, splitting one audience evenly with the same budget and Logan page. Draft: "Now in gas. The Logan in heavy Corten steel, for propane or natural gas. Gas from $6,273. Free freight delivery to the 48 contiguous states; ships in about 4 weeks."
