01 / Paid ads

Show both Soul Gel uses in your GLORIAH25 ads

One GLORIAH25 ad we read calls Soul Gel a "water-based hydrating lubricant designed to restore comfort", while your page says it is "not just a lubricant, it's skincare for down there" and "can be used daily". One subscription ad pitches a "regular intimate care routine", but the GLORIAH25 ads we read do not call it a daily moisturiser.

One change

Message test only: keep the same Soul Gel page, code and audience. Run the current ad against a variant: "Soul Gel: a water-based gel for daily hydration or comfortable glide in the moment. Take 25% off with GLORIAH25." The hypothesis is that showing both uses brings in more buyers.

02 / Paid ads

Test a clearer Liberation Oil ad promise

The Liberation Oil ad we read calls it "a daily vulva oil" for dryness and discomfort, but does not say whether it is a lubricant. Your page FAQ asks "Is Liberation Oil a lubricant?" and says it is "for external use only".

One change

Message test only: keep the same Liberation Oil page, offer line and audience. Run the current ad against a variant: "Menopause changing what your body needs? Liberation Oil is a daily oil for external use only. It is not a lubricant. 100% natural. Trusted by 1,500+ women. Free gift over £20." The hypothesis is that a clearer promise brings in more buyers.

04 / Your store

Explain the oil subscription terms in plain words

Your oil page offers a 25% subscription saving with "Swap products anytime" and a "2 month commitment", though deliveries run every 8 or 10 weeks. Your FAQ says "at least two deliveries before making any changes" but also that you can "adjust your delivery frequency at any time", so buyers may be unsure what they can change and when.

One change

Untested idea, once you confirm which terms apply: add a plain-words summary next to the subscription choice: "Save 25%, £18.75 a bottle. Minimum 2 deliveries, then change or cancel in your account." Randomly split oil page visitors 50/50 against the current wording.
