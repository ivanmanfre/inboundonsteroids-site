01 / Your store

State the one-arm use beside the hip seat's buy button

Your homepage describes carrying a child while doing jobs at home, and a product-page review says "ger händerna fria". Your FAQ requires one arm around the child. The buy-button text omits that requirement. Stating it before purchase would help buyers decide whether this seat suits how they expect to use it.

One change

Proposed, untested: add a short 'Bra att veta' line under the hip seat's buy button. Draft: "Höftsitsen avlastar rygg och armar, men du håller alltid en arm runt barnet."

02 / Your store

Offer the strap as the single carrier add-on

The carrier page's 15% add-on block lists three accessories; the strap, your second best-seller, fits the carrier's side D-rings. One sampled review uses both, and another says a 'bärrem' should be included.

One change

Proposed, untested: replace the three-item add-on list on the carrier page with the strap alone, keeping the existing 'Lägg till och få 15%' heading and letting the widget show the price. Draft: "Bär din GooGosa som en väska mellan bärstunderna. Lägg till Axelrem. Obs: remmen är för väskan, inte för barnet."

What we saw
The carrier page's 15% add-on block lists three accessories; the strap, your second best-seller, fits the carrier's side D-rings. One sampled review uses both, and another says a 'bärrem' should be included.

What to measure
