01 / Paid ads

Fix the wrong tag, then test naming vitamin C in the new eye mask ads

We read the captions of three creator ads for the new Brightening Eye Masks, all linking to its page. We did not watch the videos. Two of the captions never name the Brightening masks or vitamin C. One opens "Have you tried these ones yet?" and tags @stellaandgrace instead of @graceandstella. Only the third caption names vitamin C and says "Brighter-looking under-eyes in just 15–20 minutes."

One change

Ask the creator of the "Have you tried these ones yet?" ad to correct the tag to @graceandstella. With their permission, run that same video as two partnership ads built from the video file. Both link to the Brightening Eye Masks page. Variant A keeps their curiosity caption with the corrected tag. Variant B is an untested caption draft: "Tired-looking under-eyes? These vitamin C eye masks give brighter-looking under-eyes in 15 to 20 minutes." Keep the video, destination and tag identical; change only the caption.

02 / Your store

Match subscription timing to how fast masks run out

Your Energizing page offers Subscribe & Save and recommends using the masks "at least three to four times a week". Its FAQ also says they are "safe for daily use". At 3 to 4 a week, one 24-pair box lasts roughly six to eight weeks. On the default 24-pair size, the first delivery option shown is every month.

One change

Under Subscribe & Save for the default 24-pair size, test a box-length guide: "Using three pairs a week? 24 pairs last eight weeks. Using one pair a day? 24 days. Compare the delivery intervals with your routine." Keep the existing monthly, two-month and three-month choices. This guide is an untested proposal. Monthly delivery leaves a gap at daily use, so do not label it a full daily supply. We inspected only the default 24-pair size. Compare against the same page without this guide.

What we saw
Your Energizing page offers Subscribe & Save and recommends using the masks "at least three to four times a week". Its FAQ also says they are "safe for daily use". At 3 to 4 a week, one 24-pair box lasts roughly six to eight weeks. On the default 24-pair size, the first delivery option shown is every month.

What to measure
Primary: subscription revenue per product-page visitor at day 180. Early read: share of subscribers who delay or cancel before their second delivery. Secondary: share of buyers who choose Subscribe & Save, and share of subscribers still active at day 180.
