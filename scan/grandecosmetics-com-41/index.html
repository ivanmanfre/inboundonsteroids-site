01 / Paid ads

Add the 3-month course to the lash ad

Your ad says "Say hi to longer, thicker-looking lashes in 4–6 weeks" and links to the GrandeLASH-MD page. That page says to apply daily for a full 3 months, and none of the three ads we recorded linking to it mention the 3-month course in their text.

One change

Current ad unchanged. New ad: "Say hi to longer, thicker-looking lashes in 4–6 weeks, with full results at 3 months. One stroke, once daily." Same audience and same product page; only the 3-month clause changes. Hypothesis: stating the course length before the click may reduce early stops and returns.

02 / Your store

Relabel lash serum sizes as a course

Your safety FAQ describes the travel size as a kickstart and the full size as the full enhancement period. The product page size selector shows only volume and supply length.

One change

Current version: size labels as they are. New version: keep the mL and supply length visible and relabel the options "6 weeks: kickstart", "3 months: the full enhancement period" and "6 months: course plus maintenance". Only the labels change, on the GrandeLASH-MD page.

03 / Your store

Offer the conditioner beside lash serum sizes

Your GrandeREPAIR page says to use it with the serum "to help lengthen while you strengthen". In the sampled rendered GrandeLASH-MD page, the area after the size choice shows a free gift, subscription options and Add to Bag, with no conditioner add-on visible. We did not check the cart or any pop-up recommendations.

One change

Current version: unchanged. New version: an optional tick box under the size choice, "Add GrandeREPAIR conditioner", with the line "Apply after your serum dries. Not for use with lash extensions." If the free first-order gift is already a travel GrandeREPAIR, offer the full size to one-time buyers only.
