01 / Paid ads

Name the Starter Kit and its price in the Sizzle ad that links to it

One Drizzle ad and one Sizzle ad we read offer glass or squeeze bottles, but each ad's recorded link goes to the $49 Starter Kit page, with no glass choice and $37.00 recurring orders. Your separate kit ad ("Save 35% on 6 months worth") names refills and a funnel, but none of these three ads states $49 or $37.

One change

Keep the Sizzle ad's link to the Starter Kit page, and test the current Sizzle ad against a version that names the kit, its price and the refill price. Draft: "Drizzle + Sizzle, 2 refill cans and a funnel, shipped free. $49 to start, then $37 per refill order. Pause or cancel anytime." First confirm the limited-time $49 price, terms and refill timing are live.

02 / Your store

Add a Duo or Trio guide under the oils

The sampled homepage body asks visitors to choose among three single oils and the Trio, while the $37 Duo appears on other sampled pages. Each oil has a clear one-line job, such as "Made for eating, never heating", so a set guide could ease the first choice, though none of the Meta ads we recorded link to the homepage.

One change

If your Google ads or other traffic lands on the homepage, add one line under the oil row linking to the existing Duo and Trio pages, and show half of visitors the current homepage. Draft: "Finishing and everyday cooking? Start with the Duo. Frying at high heat too? Get the Trio."

What we saw
The sampled homepage body asks visitors to choose among three single oils and the Trio, while the $37 Duo appears on other sampled pages. What to measure
