01 / Paid ads

Test travel ad landing on 20g travel paste page

The travel video ad records only the homepage as its destination, where full-size Grin Pro pastes lead and travel items sit lower under Must-Haves. The store sells a 20g Enzyme Whitening tube that its page calls convenient for travel or trying before the full size, so a destination that matches the ad's travel message is worth testing.

One change

Keep the travel video and caption unchanged and randomly split its clicks between the homepage (control) and the 20g Enzyme Whitening travel page. Only the destination changes; check that the 20g page's pack labels read correctly before launch.

02 / Your store

Add a pack-versus-subscribe price line on Strengthening paste

On the Natural Strengthening page, $6.40 a tube is shown both for buying the 8 Pack once and for subscribing to one tube, in separate selectors. The 8 Pack version we checked lists subscription at $40.96 for 8 tubes, so the two savings combine, but we saw no line comparing these routes.

One change

Add one plain line above the selectors: "Subscribe to 1 tube: $6.40. Buy 8 once: $6.40 a tube. Subscribe to 8: $40.96 for 8 tubes." Add "Pause or cancel anytime" only if the homepage subscription terms apply here; prices and selectors stay unchanged, and control is the current page.
