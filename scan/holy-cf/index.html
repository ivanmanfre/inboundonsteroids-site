Finding 1 · Shopper paths

23.3% of your variants are sold out while your 1243 Meta ads run

You have 1243 active ads on Meta, and 23.3% of your variants read out of stock right now. A click that lands on a sold-out option can't turn into an order.

Recommended next step

Pull sold-out products out of your ads and product feed until they're back in stock.

Out of stock right now: 23.3% of your variants. See this on your storefront

Finding 2 · Shopper paths

No express checkout button on Syrup Starter Set

A shopper on Syrup Starter Set has to add to cart, open the cart and start checkout before they can pay. There's no one-tap button for shoppers who saved their card or use a phone wallet.

Recommended next step

Turn on dynamic checkout buttons in your theme's product page settings.
