01 / Proposed paid test

Test values-led versus styling-led Goddess Blouse ads

Your homepage leads with a kindness story, including a lining stitched with your mantra. The chambray Goddess Blouse page separately stresses versatile styling. These are two different reasons to buy that could be compared in ads.

One changeProposed new Meta ad test. Run two ads to the same Goddess Blouse (Chambray) page. Ad A, values-led: "Kindness in every stitch. Meet the Goddess Blouse, with our mantra embroidered on the tie." Ad B, styling-led: "One blouse, desk to dinner. Easy to dress up or down." Only the message changes.
Sources & how to check the result +

What to measure
Winner: first-order revenue per visitor to the blouse page, same audience and budget for both ads. Guardrail: gross margin after refunds on blouse orders.

02 / Your store

Test a fit and returns note by the size picker

Your FAQ states products are true to size. Your refund policy sets a 14-day notice window and makes the customer pay return postage unless the item is faulty or damaged.

One changeOn the M.A Legacy and D.W Inspire pages, test a short note near the size picker: "True to size. Full-price items: tell us within 14 days and return within 30 days of receipt. Sale items: return within 14 days. You pay return postage unless faulty or damaged." Keep the current pages as the comparison.
