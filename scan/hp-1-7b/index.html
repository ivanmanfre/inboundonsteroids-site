01 / Your store

Frame the magnesium add-on as the free shipping route

Your shipping policy charges £2.99 below £35, so a one-time capsule order costs £32.98 while the £35.99 magnesium bundle ships free, about £3 more. The page shows "Free shipping on orders over £35" near the button, but the bundle block we read does not link the +£6 add-on to free shipping for one-time buyers.

One change

For visitors choosing one-time purchase, add a line to the bundle block: "Add Magnesium for +£6.00 and UK mainland shipping is free." The control keeps today's bundle text. First confirm at checkout that the bundle total qualifies for free shipping.

02 / Proposed paid test

Test a no-crash ad message against a creatine-for-your-brain message

Your homepage hero leads with no crash, while your creatine block speaks to people who already take creatine for training, so the two may pull different buyers. The pages we read do not show which sells better, and we could not read your Meta ads.

One change

Proposed ad test, not a live ad. Split one Meta audience at random between two ads to the capsule page, equal budget and placements. A: "Focus without the crash. HP-1 Focus Capsules, built for long days." B: "You know creatine from the gym. HP-1 puts it in a focus capsule." B's copy speaks to people who already train. Check both ads against your approved claims before they run.

What we saw
Your homepage hero leads with no crash, while your creatine block speaks to people who already take creatine for training, so the two may pull different buyers. The pages we read do not show which sells better, and we could not read your Meta ads.

What to measure
Meta A/B split, four weeks. Primary: first-order gross profit per £ spent, orders matched to each ad. If subscription starts per £ disagree, call it unresolved. Track 30-day refunds.
