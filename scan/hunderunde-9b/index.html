02 / Your store

Show Sparplan beside one-time purchase after weight selection

On the sampled Bauchbalance page, the weight step comes first and the Sparplan explanation sits further down under its own question heading. The Sparplan help page says deliveries can be paused or skipped, which is a useful reassurance to bring closer to the choice.

One changeAfter the dog's weight is entered, show one-time purchase and Sparplan as two side-by-side options. Add one line under Sparplan: "Pausieren oder überspringen, wenn der Vorrat reicht." Control keeps the current layout.
Sources & how to check the result +

What to measure
Winning metric: first-order purchase revenue per visitor on the Bauchbalance page. Guardrail: 60-day guarantee refund requests. Track second-delivery renewals separately after a full delivery interval.

03 / Your store

Replace related toppings with one Schleckmatte routine add-on

The sampled Bauchbalance page closes with a row of other toppings under a generic related-products heading. The Schleckmatte page describes the mat as part of the daily topping routine, so a single complementary add-on is worth comparing against that row.

One changeOn the Bauchbalance page, replace the related-toppings row with one Schleckmatte card. Draft copy: "Topping drauf, Schleckmatte dazu: die tägliche Routine wird ein ruhiger Moment." Control keeps the current row.
Sources & how to check the result +

What to measure
Winning metric: purchase revenue per visitor on the Bauchbalance page. Diagnostic: share of orders including the Schleckmatte. Guardrail: Bauchbalance topping purchase rate.
