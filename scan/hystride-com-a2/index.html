01 / Paid ads

Send the 'Built for the track' ad to your frame guide

The 'Built for the track' ad talks about photochromic lenses and named frames, but its recorded link opens a collection labelled '23 of 23 frames', where six listed items are spare lenses or bundles. The homepage already has a guide that sorts frames by lens type and light.

From your public ad

“photochromic lenses that adapt to changing light”
View the actual ad ↗
One changeKeep the ad copy unchanged and split its traffic between the current collection link and a link that opens directly at the homepage's 'Which frame, and why' section, or a copy of that guide on its own page. Visitors then pick by lens type and light before browsing frames.

The packing-list ad we read names the Midnight Pink goggle and lists swappable lenses as one feature among several, but its recorded link opens the Arctic Silver page. That page says spare lenses fit the frame you already own.

From your public ad

03 / Your store

Add a face-fit guide linking Speed Pro and Apex

Your Apex page describes a wider frame, built for average to larger faces. Your Speed Pro page has a fit section headed 'Know it fits before it arrives', but the sampled Speed Pro text does not point wider-face shoppers to Apex.

One changeShow half of Speed Pro visitors a two-line fit guide under the size section. Draft: 'Most faces: Speed Pro. Other sport frames pinch? See Apex, with a taller lens and removable nose grip.' Link the second line to the Apex page.
