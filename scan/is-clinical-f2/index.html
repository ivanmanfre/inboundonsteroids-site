Finding 1 · Repeat purchase

We didn't find a rewards program on your store

A first-time buyer earns no points or credit toward a next order, so a reward is not one of the reasons they have to come back.

Recommended next step

Add a simple points or next-order credit program and mention it on the order confirmation page.

Paid-media check

Your longest-running ads are past the fatigue window

You have 111 active ads, the oldest running 157 days across 20 distinct angles. Half your live ads have been running 49.5 days or longer. The oldest one opens with "A balanced microbiome is essential for overall skin health.". Past the first several weeks on one angle, fewer people click and each click costs more. A steady creative refresh cadence is the lever that holds performance from sliding.

Testing sprawl with no consolidated winner

You have 111 active ads spread across 20 distinct angles. Testing that wide without a consolidated winner spreads budget thin and makes performance hard to read. Pruning to the proven angles and concentrating spend behind them is the lever.

Most of your creative is dynamic catalog

Of the 96 ads we sampled, 71.9% run in a dynamic catalog format. When most of the creative is feed-driven, product feed quality becomes the creative that decides performance. Tightening feed titles, imagery, and hero products is the fastest win here.

Next account check

Your Google ads on record are almost all text

Of the 29 Google ads we found on record for your domain, 23 are text. Text-only coverage leaves the visual inventory unclaimed on a network you are already paying to be on, so the image and video placements are open ground rather than a new channel to justify.
