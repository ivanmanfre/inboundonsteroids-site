Where we’d focus

What we’d test first

01

Send the reglazing ad to the reglazing offer

The ad links to the lens consultation page. That page explicitly includes reglazing alongside other prescription needs. J.HYDE also has a dedicated reglazing page with its own price guide, frame assessment details and appointment links. There are two relevant routes to compare.

Proposed test

Keep the current ad and compare its consultation-page destination with the existing reglazing page. Use the same audience and creative so the landing page is the main change. The reglazing page gives visitors the frame assessment, price guide and booking route for that specific service.

How we’d measure it

Cost per completed reglaze appointment and the share of bookings that attend. Track all qualified lens bookings too, so a change in booking type is not mistaken for growth.

02

Explain the prescription buying steps beside Add to cart

Legacy already explains the prescription follow-up below Add to cart: after checkout, customers can book an eye test or lens consultation. The page says the next steps and booking details arrive in the confirmation email.

Proposed test

Test a short summary beside Add to cart: “Buy your frame. Check your confirmation email to book an eye test or lens consultation.” Keep the current lens price and full explanation. This is a placement test of information the page already provides.

How we’d measure it

Completed frame orders that progress to a lens consultation and prescription order. Watch cancellations and prescription-related support questions alongside checkout completion.

03

Show how a designer frame becomes a reglazing appointment

The current ad leads with bringing an existing designer frame for Zeiss lenses. The reglazing page explains that a frame is assessed before work begins and that the service takes place in a studio. That assessment can form the story of a new creative test.

Proposed test

Make a short video variant with three scenes: the customer’s existing frame, the frame being assessed, and digital lens measurements in the studio. Proposed closing line: “Keep the frame you love. Book a lens consultation.” Run it alongside the current ad and send both to the current lens consultation page during this creative test.

How we’d measure it

Cost per qualified, attended reglaze appointment. Track unsuitable-frame enquiries as a guardrail, since the offer depends on frame condition.

Finding 1 · Shopper paths

Your best seller, J.HYDE Legacy, shows no customer reviews

A shopper on J.HYDE Legacy sees no star rating and no reviews from past buyers, so nothing on the page shows that other customers bought it and were happy with it.

Recommended next step

Add a review app to your product pages and ask recent buyers for a review after delivery.
