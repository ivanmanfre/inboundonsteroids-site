01 / Proposed paid test

Test long-wear matte against matte-plus-gloss ad message

Your two best sellers are Lijepo Plum Matte, which promises "12 Hour Lock In Color", and the Čisto clear gloss, which "Can Be Worn Alone Or Over Matte Color". Meta's Ad Library shows no ads running on your page right now, so no current public ad shows which of these two promises should lead.

One change

Proposed new ad test, not an observed ad. Run two Meta ads to the same best-selling collection page. A: "Color that stays put. Matte liquid lipstick with 12 hour lock in color." B: "Wear matte. Add clear gloss. Two looks, one lip." Only the message differs.

02 / Your store

Offer clear gloss on the Plum matte page

Your homepage says the clear gloss can be worn over matte colors. The sampled Lijepo Plum Matte page's application steps cover only the liner and the lipstick, so it is an open question whether shoppers see gloss as a pairing from that page.

One change

Split visitors on the Lijepo Plum Matte page between the current page and a version with one optional line under Add to cart: "Finish with shine. Add Čisto clear gloss over your matte." The line has a one-click add of the $17 Čisto Gloss Clear Gloss Finish. The control page stays as is.

What we saw
Your homepage says the clear gloss can be worn over matte colors. The sampled Lijepo Plum Matte page's application steps cover only the liner and the lipstick, so it is an open question whether shoppers see gloss as a pairing from that page.

What to measure
Compare first-order revenue per visitor for both versions, using the same visitors over the same period. Also check that the share of visitors buying the matte lipstick does not fall.

03 / Your store

Show ready-made lip sets first in best sellers

In the sampled best-selling listing text, the three bundle sets come after the single lipsticks. Monica's Style shows a $101.00 price struck down to $88.00, so a set could mean a larger order and fewer shade decisions.

One change

Split visitors on the best-selling collection page. The variant adds a top row with the three sets, labeled "Ready-made looks: matched shades, priced below buying separately". The control keeps the sets after the single lipsticks.
