01 / Paid ads

Send run short ad clicks to shorts first

Your matching-sets ad says it features the Accelerate Run Short, but it links to a 71-result collection. That page opens with a sports bra and leggings, with running shorts further down, so shoppers have to scroll to find the advertised short.

One change

Keep the ad unchanged and randomly send half of its clicks to a view of this collection that shows the Accelerate 3" running shorts first. Draft heading: "The Run Short edit: Marshmallow x Cocoa Powder."

02 / Paid ads

Add a run kit row to Synergy landing page

Your Synergy run capsule ad is aimed at runners and lands on a 50-product collection. On that page, the run belt and the quarter socks that carry a 3 For $50 offer sit well below the shorts, bras and tees, so runners may miss these add-ons.

One change

For half of ad clicks, add a short row at the top of the Accelerate: Synergy collection showing a Synergy running short, run belt and quarter socks. Draft heading: "Complete your run: short, belt and socks."

03 / Paid ads

Name the Rep short multi-buy in men's ad

The caption on your men's best sellers video ad names no product or offer, though we did not review the video itself. The page it links to tags several best sellers with multi-buy offers, including the Rep 5" Performance Short at A$65 with 2 For $100.

One change

Keep the current ad and destination, and run a second version with the same video whose text names the Rep 5" short and its multi-buy. Draft: "Show up anyway. Rep 5" Performance Shorts, 2 For $100." Confirm the offer rules and margins before launch.
