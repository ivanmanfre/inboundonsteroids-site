01 / Proposed paid test

Test barrier-care versus ritual-use ad messages for Smoked Bay

Your page states Smoked Bay Body Ritual works as "a nightly facial treatment, a full-body oil" or bath oil. Your barrier repair page lists "Signs your skin barrier may need support" and recommends it first. It is unclear which reason to buy should lead your next paid ad.

One change

Proposed new ad test, not an observed live ad. Run two Meta ads with the same image, audience and Smoked Bay Body Ritual product page destination. A: "For skin that feels tight or over-treated. A rich oil for face and body." B: "One oil for bath, body and bedtime. Smoked bay laurel scent." Split the audience randomly.

02 / Your store

Add a Smoked Bay versus Wild Path scent note with mini route

Your Smoked Bay page already links a sample bottle, and one reviewer loved the oil but won't rebuy because their husband said they "smelt like an old bonfire". Wild Path reads "Fresh florals meet sweet scorched grass" and shares the same juniper wood oil, so a side-by-side note could help shoppers choose before buying full size.

One change

On the Smoked Bay page, add a two-column scent note: Smoked Bay, "Smoked bay laurel meets warm herbal, earthy undertones"; Wild Path, "Fresh florals meet sweet scorched grass". Both list juniper wood oil, so never call Wild Path smoke-free. Link to the Discovery Sets page. Draft: "Two scents, one ritual. Try minis first." Randomly split visitors; control is the current page.

03 / Your store

Test an Evening Ritual card on barrier repair page

Your barrier repair page already shows "The Nourished Skin Ritual" with cleanser, eye serum and facial oil, plus separate add-to-cart buttons. Your Evening Ritual kit pairs a cleanser, a body ritual oil used as a night facial, and the eye serum. Which format suits visitors reading about barrier care is untested.

One change

Randomly show half of barrier repair page visitors one Evening Ritual card in place of the three separate product buttons. Draft: "Barrier care in order: cleanse, nourish, treat. See the Evening Ritual." It links to the kit page. Control keeps the current ritual block.
