01 / Proposed paid test

Test dry-hair convenience against breakage proof in new mask ads

The Multitask Repair Mask page gives two reasons to buy: wear on dry hair for five minutes or overnight, and a tested claim of 6X less breakage. We could not read your ads, so if you have not already tested these two messages, this would show which drives more purchases.

One change

Proposed new Meta ad test, not an observed ad. Use Meta's A/B test to run two ads to the mask product page with the same audience, budget and format; only the message differs. Ad A: "Mask on dry hair. Five minutes or overnight, then shampoo as usual." Ad B: "6X less breakage.*" Footnote: "*With repeated use prior to washing with a non-conditioning shampoo vs. repeated washing without mask."

02 / Your store

Lead Good Roots routine block with the set

The Good Roots page has a Complete Your Routine block for single products, while the Low Maintenance Edit set, listed at $120 against $130, sits in a later You may also like list. Showing the set inside the routine block may make the larger purchase easier to consider.

One change

On the Good Roots Volume Mist page, show The Low Maintenance Edit as the first card in the routine block and keep the single mask and serum cards below it. Draft line: "Get the full routine. Repair, volume and restyle in one set." Randomize visitors 50/50.

03 / Your store

Show a wash-day to day-2 routine on the mask page

Your mask page says you can sleep in the mask before wash day, and your serum page describes restyling on day 2 or beyond. The mask page's routine block lists other products but does not link those two moments into one routine.

One change

On the Multitask Repair Mask page, replace the routine block's plain cards with a two-step card pair and keep the Good Roots card below it. Draft: "Step 1: Mask before wash day. Step 2: Serum to restyle on day 2 and beyond." Randomize visitors 50/50.
