Reach teams planning SEO and content for the Korean market.

Written for marketing and SEO leads who want their Korean content to show up on both Naver and Google.

Michaela ParkSearch SEOul

A Korean page written from an English brief rarely works on Naver and Google at the same time.

Every SEO hits the same wall at some point. Why does Naver move so differently from Google? At Search SEOul 2026 we gave that question its own session.

Sungwon Hwang of Blogtalk has run a Naver blog himself for 12 years. He unpacked how Naver's search ecosystem really works.

If you are planning Korean content, start the brief before the translation:

  • Pick search terms from Korean data. Don't take them from your English list.
  • Decide which pieces live on your site and which on Naver Blog
  • Write a separate outline for each platform
  • Add short, clear answers that AI search tools can quote
  • Have a native reader check prices, holidays and examples

The Korea content brief is filled in twice so you can see every field done: once for a scheduling app and once for an outdoor gear store.

Say a US scheduling app opens a Korean site

01The brief at a glance

For example, say a US scheduling app is opening a Korean site. The team wants Korean team leads to find it when they look for easier ways to set up meetings.

  • Market: South Korea, Korean language
  • Reader: team leads who book many internal and client meetings
  • Goal: one guide on the site and one Naver Blog post that links to it
  • Main question to answer: how do I set up a meeting without long email threads?
  • Reviewer: a native Korean speaker who has used scheduling tools at work

Fill in this block first. Every later field should match this reader and this main question.

02Keyword map: translated terms next to Korean search terms

The English brief says "meeting scheduler". The direct translation is 미팅 스케줄러. It reads fine, but it may not be what people type.

Put three columns side by side:

  1. Translated term: 미팅 스케줄러
  2. Terms Korean users may type: 회의 일정 잡기 (setting up a meeting), 일정 관리 앱 (schedule app), 구글 캘린더 연동 (Google Calendar sync)
  3. Where checked: Naver's keyword tool for Naver, Google Keyword Planner for Google

Main term for the guide: 회의 일정 잡기
Supporting terms: 일정 관리 앱, 구글 캘린더 연동

Check the two tools separately. They can point to different words, and the site guide and the Naver Blog post can each lead with the term that fits its platform.

03Platform plan: what lives on the site and what lives on Naver

Naver results lean on Naver's own services, such as Naver Blog, Cafe and Knowledge iN. An article on your site alone may be hard to find there, so plan two pieces.

Site guide (for Google and AI search):

  • Title: 회의 일정 잡기, 메일 왕복 없이 끝내는 방법
  • Full step-by-step guide with screenshots of the app
  • Register the site in Naver Search Advisor so Naver can crawl it

Naver Blog post (for Naver):

  • Title: 팀장이 회의 일정 잡을 때 쓰는 방법 정리
  • Shorter, first-person tone, more photos
  • Links to the site guide for the full steps

Keep the facts identical in both pieces. Only the tone and length change.

04Site guide outline, filled in

H1: 회의 일정 잡기, 메일 왕복 없이 끝내는 방법

  1. 첫 문단: answer the main question in two sentences. Share your open times as a link, the other person picks one, and the meeting lands in both calendars.
  2. 왜 메일로 일정 잡기가 오래 걸리나: the back-and-forth problem, shown with a simple internal meeting.
  3. 단계별 방법: connect Google Calendar, set open hours, share the link, confirm.
  4. 카카오톡으로 링크 보내기: how to share the link in a KakaoTalk chat, since many teams coordinate there.
  5. 자주 묻는 질문: three short questions and answers.
  6. 다음 단계: sign-up link and Korean support contact.

Write each section heading in Korean from the start, working from the keyword map.

05Answer block for AI search

AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity tend to quote short, direct answers. Put one near the top of the guide and use the same style in the FAQ.

Example answer block:
회의 일정은 가능한 시간을 링크로 공유하면 빠르게 잡을 수 있습니다. 상대방이 편한 시간을 고르면 양쪽 캘린더에 바로 등록됩니다.

(Meetings can be set quickly by sharing your available times as a link. The other person picks a time and it goes into both calendars.)

FAQ questions to answer in the same plain style:

  • 구글 캘린더와 연동되나요? (Does it sync with Google Calendar?)
  • 무료로 쓸 수 있나요? (Can I use it for free?)
  • 외부 고객과도 쓸 수 있나요? (Can I use it with outside clients?)

Name the product and the market plainly in these answers so a tool can tell what the page is about.

06Local check before publishing

Give the native reviewer this list along with the draft:

  • Prices shown in won
  • Dates in Korean order: year, month, day
  • Holidays that affect scheduling, such as Chuseok and Seollal
  • KakaoTalk mentioned where teams actually share links
  • Korean workplace terms in the examples, such as 팀장 and 주간 회의
  • No sentence that sounds translated when read aloud

Ask the reviewer to mark anything they would not say at work. Fix those lines before writing the Naver Blog version.
