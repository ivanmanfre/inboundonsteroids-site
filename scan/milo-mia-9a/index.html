02 / Your store

Show the 30-day bundle as a side-by-side choice

On the Lachs & Kartoffel page, the starter bundle appears as one line of text between the size choice and the Add to cart button. Your page states it includes Reboot and free Belly Bites.

One changeReplace the text line with two labelled choices: 'Nur Nassfutter' and 'Sanfter Start: Futter + Reboot + Belly Bites im 30-Tage-Bundle'. Split visitors evenly between the current line and the two-choice layout, changing only this block.
