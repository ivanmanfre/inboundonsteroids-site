02 / Your store

Add a day-30 next step to Reboot page

Your Reboot page states it is designed for 30 days and its FAQ asks what happens after that. The page does not point to a specific next product, so a defined next step is worth testing.

One changeVariant B adds a short block titled "Dein Plan nach Tag 30" with two options: continue with wet food by dog size, or add Belly Bites daily. Draft: "Tag 1 bis 30: Reboot. Ab Tag 31: ruhige Routine mit Nassfutter." Control has no block.
