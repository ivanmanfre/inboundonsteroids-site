01 / Proposed paid test

Test two ad messages for the Starter Kit

Your Starter Kit page frames the kit for coffee alternative beginners and as a way to make café-quality lattes at home. Your product pages also state the energy comes without the jitters or the crash, a different reason to buy.

One changeProposed new ad test, not an observed live ad. Send both messages to the Starter Kit page with the same audience and budget. Variant A: "Coffee crash by 3pm? Morning Made says its matcha gives steady energy without the jitters. Start with the Starter Kit." Variant B: "Café-style matcha lattes at home. Everything you need, in one kit."
Sources & how to check the result +

What to measure
Purchases per visitor and first-order revenue per visitor on the Starter Kit page, same for both variants. Guardrail: product margin after kit contents and refund requests.

02 / Your store

Compare 60 and 180 serve kits side by side

Your Essentials Kit page lists 60 serves at $45, and your Mega Bundle page lists 180 serves at $99. A direct size comparison on the kit page is worth testing for people already considering the smaller kit.

One changeAdd a two-row size comparison above Add to cart on the Essentials Kit page, linking to the Mega Bundle. Draft: '60 serves, $45. 180 serves, $99: three times the serves for about 2.2 times the price.' The control keeps the page as it is.

Purchase revenue per Essentials Kit page visitor, both arms. Guardrail: gross profit per visitor and refund requests.

03 / Your store

Clarify subscribe versus one-time on Starter Kit

Your Starter Kit page shows a subscription first order and a one-time purchase at the same $47. Your guarantee page says the refund covers the first subscription order only, so the two choices may be hard to tell apart.

One changeShow two plain choice cards on the Starter Kit page. Draft: 'Subscribe: $47 today, then $31 per delivery. Guarantee covers this first order.' and 'One-time: $47.' The control keeps the current selector.
Sources & how to check the result +

What to measure
First-order revenue per Starter Kit visitor in both arms. Track second-delivery revenue separately over 12 weeks, with refund and cancellation rates as guardrails.
