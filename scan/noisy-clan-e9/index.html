Finding 1 · Shopper paths

Your best seller, Music Theory Circle of Fifths, is sold out

Shoppers who click through to Music Theory Circle of Fifths reach a Sold out button, so anyone who came for it can't order it today.

Recommended next step

Restock it, or move an in-stock product into its best-seller and homepage spots until it's back.

Best-selling order on your store: Your best seller, marked sold out. See this on your storefront

Finding 2 · Shopper paths

24.4% of your variants are sold out while your 5 Meta ads run

You have 5 active ads on Meta, and 24.4% of your variants read out of stock right now. A click that lands on a sold-out option can't turn into an order.

Recommended next step

Pull sold-out products out of your ads and product feed until they're back in stock.

Paid-media check

Your longest-running ads are past the fatigue window

You have 5 active ads, the oldest running 100 days across 1 distinct angle. Half your live ads have been running 72 days or longer. The oldest one opens with "Learn this and more with Guitar Scale and Chord Compasses.". Past the first several weeks on one angle, fewer people click and each click costs more.
