02 / Your store

Match the toilet refill to how long a box lasts

The Sunday Morning toilet box contains 25 cubes. Your page says to use one cube and clean once or twice a week, yet its subscription option is monthly at $16.19. For one bowl cleaned at that rate, a box lasts 12.5–25 weeks. Multiple bowls or heavier use change that calculation. This suggests a refill-choice test; it does not establish oversupply, cancellations or lost revenue.

One changeCompare the current monthly offer with a usage-led choice: ask how many bowls are cleaned and how often, then suggest a refill interval while retaining monthly and one-time options. For one bowl used once or twice weekly, test 3- and 6-month choices, with price and discount clearly stated. Start with toilet-only purchases; a laundry box has a different usage rate. Validate intervals and shipping economics before launch.
Sources & how to check the result +

What to measure
Compare contribution per enrolled toilet subscriber over the same six-month period. Track subscription take-up, second deliveries, skips, cancellations and fulfillment cost. Count all orders in each group; fewer shipments alone do not prove improvement.

03 / Proposed paid test

Show the cube at work in new laundry creative

Your laundry page sells the switch away from jugs: “Say goodbye to bulky jugs and bottles.” It also gives creative a concrete action to show: “Toss one cube in the drum and throw your laundry on top for the freshest clean.” It adds “Works in cold water.” and says the cubes tackle odors and fight stains. These are your own published product claims, not independently verified results, so any ad should use them exactly as stated.

One changeProposed paid test, not yet run: after the landing-route test, compare a jug-swap ad with a new ad that shows the actual cube going into a drum, followed by the cold-water and odor claims from your page. Send both to the same Barefoot Somewhere laundry page with the same audience, format and offer. Use genuine product footage and keep each claim exactly as substantiated.
