01 / Paid ads

Test an ad timing line that includes chilling time

Your Garden Gang ad says "Ready in just 5 minutes" and sends people to the Ultimate Gummies Bundle page. That page says to "Place the filled moulds in the fridge for at least 2 hours", so a parent who clicks expecting gummies in 5 minutes learns on landing that they need at least 2 hours to set.

One change

Proposed ad copy test, with both ads going to the same bundle page. Keep the current ad as the control. In the variant, change only the timing line to: "5 minutes to mix, then set in the fridge for at least 2 hours." Split the same audience and budget evenly between the two ads using Meta's A/B test.

02 / Proposed paid test

Test two collagen ad angles to one page

Your Collagen Beauty page makes two different pitches: "NOT ALL COLLAGEN IS THE SAME" (a research-led comparison) and a scoop that is "Unflavoured and grit-free" (an easy daily habit). It is unresolved which opening message should bring new visitors to this page.

One change

Proposed new paid creative test, not an observed live ad. Run two Meta ads to the same Collagen Beauty page for the same new-customer audience. Ad A draft: "Not all collagen is the same. Know exactly which one is in your coffee." Ad B draft: "One scoop. Unflavoured. Stir it into coffee, a smoothie or porridge."

03 / Your store

Offer Joints + Bones as an order add-on

It is unresolved whether pairing a second collagen should be a direct add-to-order offer. Your page says to "pair your Collagen Beauty with Collagen Joints", and your home page lists Collagen Joints + Bones as its own product. Your page also gives a "maximum of 20 grams" for combined collagen.

One change

Keep the current pairing block as the control. In the variant, add an optional Collagen Joints + Bones add-on beside it, with draft copy: "Pair it with Collagen Joints + Bones. Add both to this order." Before launch, confirm one serve of each stays within your recommended 20 g daily collagen maximum (Collagen Beauty alone gives 11.7 g), and show it beside the add-on. Randomly split product-page visitors 50/50.
