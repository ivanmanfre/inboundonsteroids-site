Offer in-stock capsules when Non-Bio sheets sold out

On 21 October 2026, Non Bio was sold out on your third-best seller, Laundry Detergent Sheets, and its options area showed no pointer to your in-stock Non-Bio capsules. Those capsules cost about 33p a wash (£9.99 for 30), above the 18p your sheets headline quotes, so the message leads on sensitive skin, not price.

Untested proposal: add a short message and link under the sold-out Non Bio option. Draft: "Non-Bio sheets are sold out. Need Non-Bio for sensitive skin? Our Sensitive Clean Non-Bio Laundry Capsules (30 Wash) are in stock at £9.99."

What we saw
On 21 October 2026, Non Bio was sold out on your third-best seller, Laundry Detergent Sheets, and its options area showed no pointer to your in-stock Non-Bio capsules. Those capsules cost about 33p a wash (£9.99 for 30), above the 18p your sheets headline quotes, so the message leads on sensitive skin, not price.

What to measure
Until Non Bio restocks, show half of visitors who pick Non Bio the message. Compare orders and revenue per visitor, capsules included, with the rest. Too few orders means unproven.

Test setup
