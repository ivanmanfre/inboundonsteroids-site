01 / Proposed paid test

Test mood versus summer-holiday ad messages

Your homepage says "Candles made for the moment you are in, not just the room you are in". Your Endless Summers and Vitamin Sea pages promise "the essence of summer vacations throughout the year", so through a UK winter it is worth knowing which promise brings buyers.

One changeRun two new ads to the same best-selling collection page. Ad A: "Candles made for the moment you are in. Shop the best sellers." Ad B: "Summer holiday scents for your home, all year. Shop the best sellers." Put each ad in its own ad set with the same audience and the same daily budget, so the opening line is the only difference.
Sources & how to check the result +

What to measure
Completed orders per visitor for each ad, counted in Shopify from a separate tracking tag on each ad's link, not Meta's own reporting. Also watch cost per order.

02 / Your store

Show set options on single candle page

In the page text we read, neither the Summer On Repeat (£80) nor The Trio (£120) page says what the set contains, and the Product Information section is empty on both. If the contents only appear in the photos, a shopper coming from the fully described £44.00 Endless Summers page has to work out what the extra money buys.

One changeAdd each set's contents and price per candle to its page first, then show half of Endless Summers visitors a panel under Add to cart linking to both sets, drafted as "See Summer On Repeat (£80) and The Trio (£120)". The other half see the page as it is.
Sources & how to check the result +

What to measure
Completed-order revenue per page visitor for each half. Also watch profit after set pricing, and the share of orders returned.

03 / Your store

Add scent notes to best-seller candle cards

In the sampled best-selling list, Vitamin Sea shows its name and £44.00 price. Its product page states the scent as Eucalyptus & Verbena, one click further in.

One changeShow half of visitors a one-line scent note under each candle name on the collection page, such as "Vitamin Sea: Eucalyptus & Verbena". Take the notes from each product page. The other half see names and prices only.
