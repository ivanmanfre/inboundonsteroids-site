01 / Proposed paid test

Test two ad messages for SPF day cream

Sweet Grass Day Cream SPF30 is your best-seller and, at £105, your priciest product. Its page claims "no white cast" and a four-week study of 20 people where 90% "experienced smoother, softer skin", so it is unclear which reason wins more first orders from an ad.

One change

Proposed new paid ad test, not an observed live ad. Send both versions to the Sweet Grass Day Cream SPF30 page and change only the message. A: "SPF30 that sinks in with no white cast. Silky, fresh finish." B: "In a four-week clinical study of 20 people, 90% experienced smoother, softer skin." Use only claims you can substantiate for ad review.

02 / Your store

Add a day routine panel to Day Cream page

Your Day Cream page says to use it after Sage Toner and Sweet Grass Day Serum, and the three together cost £251, above your £200 free-delivery threshold. The contents of the page's You May Also Like area were not observed.

One change

Show half of Day Cream visitors a "Complete your morning routine" panel under Add to Cart, listing toner, serum and SPF cream. Draft: "Toner, Serum, SPF30 Day Cream. Free delivery on orders from £200." The other half sees the current page.

What we saw
Your Day Cream page says to use it after Sage Toner and Sweet Grass Day Serum, and the three together cost £251, above your £200 free-delivery threshold. The contents of the page's You May Also Like area were not observed.

03 / Your store

Add a cleanse and tone pair to cleanser page

Your Tobacco Cleanser page tells shoppers to follow with Sage Toner, so the cleanser leads naturally to a second product. A one-tap way to add both was not observed in the sampled page text.

One change

Add a "Cleanse + Tone" option under Add to Cart that puts Tobacco Cleanser and Sage Toner in the basket together, at their normal prices with no discount. Draft: "Step 1 Cleanse. Step 2 Tone. Add both." Half of visitors see it; the rest see the current page.

Full test plan & sources +

What we saw
Your Tobacco Cleanser page tells shoppers to follow with Sage Toner, so the cleanser leads naturally to a second product. A one-tap way to add both was not observed in the sampled page text.

What to measure
Compare first-order revenue per visitor on the cleanser page between the two versions, using randomly split visitors and the same dates. Also check refunds and profit per order.

Test setup
Add a "Cleanse + Tone" option under Add to Cart that puts Tobacco Cleanser and Sage Toner in the basket together, at their normal prices with no discount. Draft: "Step 1 Cleanse. Step 2 Tone. Add both." Half of visitors see it; the rest see the current page.
