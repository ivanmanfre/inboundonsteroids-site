02 / Your store

Explain the refill schedule before checkout

The Original page already offers Subscribe & save 8%. One-time purchase is selected when the page opens. Delivery choices from 2 to 6 weeks appear after selecting subscription.

One changeKeep one-time purchase selected. Test adding this sentence beside Subscribe & save before a shopper selects it: "Save 8% on refills. Choose delivery every 2 to 6 weeks." Compare with the current page. Keep prices, pack sizes and the purchase choices unchanged.
Sources & how to check the result +

What to measure
Compare contribution profit per new customer and the share placing a second paid order within 12 weeks. Track first-order conversion, refunds and cancellations before the second shipment. Use customer cohorts, since delivery intervals differ.
