Finding 1 · Shopper paths

5.7% of your products are sold out while your 68 Meta ads run

You have 68 active ads on Meta, and 5.7% of your products read out of stock right now. A click that lands on a sold-out option can't turn into an order.

Recommended next step

Pull sold-out products out of your ads and product feed until they're back in stock.

Out of stock right now: 5.7% of your products. See this on your storefront

Finding 2 · Shopper paths

No express checkout button on your product pages

A shopper on All-Weather Outdoor Protective Cover for Single Lounger Set has to add to cart, open the cart and start checkout before they can pay. There's no one-tap button for shoppers who saved their card or use a phone wallet.

Recommended next step

Turn on dynamic checkout buttons in your theme's product page settings.

Express checkout button on the product page: Not on the page. See this on your product page

Finding 3 · Repeat purchase

We didn't find a rewards program on your store

A first-time buyer earns no points or credit toward a next order, so a reward is not one of the reasons they have to come back.

Recommended next step

Add a simple points or next-order credit program and mention it on the order confirmation page.

Rewards program: None found on your storefront. See this on your storefront

Paid-media check

Most of your creative is dynamic catalog

Of the 68 ads we sampled, 67.6% run in a dynamic catalog format. When most of the creative is feed-driven, product feed quality becomes the creative that decides performance. Tightening feed titles, imagery, and hero products is the fastest win here.
