01 / Paid ads

Test range ad on homepage concern picker

A range ad names three products and says "Shop the range at pincwellness.com", but its recorded destination is the single-product Calendula page, which opens with a question about antibiotics. Further down, your homepage has a concern picker ("What do you need today?"), a possible alternative landing for a range message.

One change

Keep the ad copy unchanged and send half of eligible ad clicks to the current Calendula page and half to the homepage concern picker. Your Microbiome and pH Support collection page may match the ad more closely, but we did not read it.

02 / Paid ads

Test an after-antibiotics hook on the probiotic ad

The probiotic ad leads with how probiotics take a different route and links to the probiotic page. Your page also describes use after antibiotics, a second reason to buy that the ad text does not mention.

One change

Run a creative-message test with the same probiotic page link. Variant draft that avoids asking viewers about their own treatment: "After a course of antibiotics, or for long-term intimate wellness, Pinc places two Lactobacillus strains directly where they're needed. Insert before bed." Control keeps the current message.

What we saw
The probiotic ad leads with how probiotics take a different route and links to the probiotic page. Your page also describes use after antibiotics, a second reason to buy that the ad text does not mention.

What to measure
Purchases and revenue per 1,000 people reached, same audience and budget split evenly between both ads for 14 days. Check cost per purchase and refund requests do not worsen.

Test setup
Run a creative-message test with the same probiotic page link. Variant draft that avoids asking viewers about their own treatment: "After a course of antibiotics, or for long-term intimate wellness, Pinc places two Lactobacillus strains directly where they're needed. Insert before bed." Control keeps the current message.

03 / Your store

Test a reason line on the wash pairing card

Your wash page already suggests pairing with probiotic suppositories "both inside and out" in a tip, and shows a "Pairs well with" card for them. In the sampled text, the card itself names the product without saying why the two are used together.

One change

Keep the existing card and add one line: "The wash cleans the outside. Suppositories support the inside." Control keeps the card as it is today.
