Finding 1 · Shopper paths

Free shipping starts at $45, and your typical product costs $18

A shopper buying one typical item at $18 pays for delivery unless they add more to reach $45.

Recommended next step

Test a threshold closer to one or two items, or a bundle priced just above $45.

Paid-media check

Heavy spend riding on very few angles

You have 56 active ads built on 3 distinct angles. That much spend on so few messages is a single point of failure. When the winning angle fatigues there is no tested backup to move budget into, so a wider angle-testing bench is the lever.

See this in the Meta Ad Library

Next account check

We widen the tested bench so the winning message never becomes a single point of failure.

Most of your creative is dynamic catalog

Of the 52 ads we sampled, 57.7% run in a dynamic catalog format. When most of the creative is feed-driven, product feed quality becomes the creative that decides performance. Tightening feed titles, imagery, and hero products is the fastest win here.
