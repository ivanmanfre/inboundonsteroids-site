Where we’d focus

What we’d test first

01

Test a Whitening Pen add-on in strips-only carts

When we added strips (£25.99) to the cart, the drawer offered "Buy 2, save 15%" and three add-ons under £7: a toothbrush, mini toothpaste and floss. Your gift set pairs whitening with the £17.99 Whitening Pen for top-ups between treatments, but the cart we saw did not offer it.

Proposed test

Proposed, untested: for carts holding only the strips, randomly show half of them the Whitening Pen as a fourth suggestion: "Top up between treatments. Add the Whitening Pen, £17.99." The other half keep today's cart. Buy 2, save 15% stays in both.

How we’d measure it

Assign each cart when strips are first added. Compare order revenue per assigned cart, minus delivery the store pays, over 4+ weeks. Checkout completion and returns must not worsen.

02

Test a strips and powder duo choice on the strips page

Your strips page offers one-time purchase or subscribe and save next to Add to cart. The Strips & Powder Duo at £49.99 shows only further down, under "People also love". Your powder FAQ suggests using the powder alongside the strips.

Proposed test

Proposed, untested: on the strips page, randomly show half of UK visitors the duo as a third option beside one-time and subscribe: "Pair your strips with the daily powder. Strips & Powder Duo, £49.99." The other half see the current page.

How we’d measure it

Compare first-order revenue per strips-page visitor over 4 or more full weeks. Strips subscription sign-ups, purchase rate and refunds must not worsen. First-order revenue alone cannot show repeat revenue.
