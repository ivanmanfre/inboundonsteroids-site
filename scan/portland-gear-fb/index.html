Finding 1 · Shopper paths

No express checkout button on Cascade Backpack - Standard

A shopper on Cascade Backpack - Standard has to add to cart, open the cart and start checkout before they can pay. There's no one-tap button for shoppers who saved their card or use a phone wallet.

Recommended next step

Turn on dynamic checkout buttons in your theme's product page settings.

Testing sprawl with no consolidated winner

You have 304 active ads spread across 32 distinct angles. Testing that wide without a consolidated winner spreads budget thin and makes performance hard to read. Pruning to the proven angles and concentrating spend behind them is the lever.
