01 / Proposed paid test

Test craft versus travel-blanket ad messages

Your Shadow Cashmere Carré page says the shawl is hand-loomed in Nepal and can also be a small travel blanket, and your home page gives travel its own Go range. Your Facebook Page shows no live Meta ads, so a new ad could lead with either the craft or the travel use; we have not tested which works better.

One change

Proposed new Meta ad test with two messages and one destination, the Shadow Cashmere Carré | Blue & Green product page. Variant A (craft): "Hand-loomed in our Kathmandu Valley atelier and piece-dyed by hand. Pure Grade-A cashmere." Variant B (travel): "One cashmere square that doubles as a small travel blanket." Keep the image, audience and budget the same in a Meta A/B test.

02 / Your store

Add gift packaging and monogram choices on product page

The Shadow Carré page says gift packaging and monogram are available on request. Your refund policy also lists personalized items as non-returnable, so a clear option could state that upfront.

One change

On the Shadow Carré page, show a gift choice above Add to Cart: 'Add gift packaging' and 'Add monogram (personalized, non-returnable)', with a one-line lead-time note. Control keeps the current on-request wording. Confirm monogram lead time and any price before launch.
