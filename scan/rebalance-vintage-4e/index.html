Where shoppers drop off

  1. 38.4% of your variants are sold out while your 46 Meta ads run

    You have 46 active ads on Meta, and 38.4% of your variants read out of stock right now. A click that lands on a sold-out option can't turn into an order.

    The fix

    Pull sold-out products out of your ads and product feed until they're back in stock.

    Out of stock right now: 38.4% of your variants. see this on your storefront

  2. No express checkout button on Save The Trees Hat

    A shopper on Save The Trees Hat has to add to cart, open the cart and start checkout before they can pay. There's no one-tap button for shoppers with a saved Shop Pay or wallet account.

    The fix

    Turn on dynamic checkout buttons in your theme's product page settings.
