01 / Proposed paid test

Fix the REBL Bundle size copy, then test a pick-two bundle ad

The REBL Bundle page offers a two fragrance bundle "with our full sized REBL Exclusives" for $85.00, yet lists each pick as "Sample 2ml / 0.07 fl oz". Fix that conflict before sending ad clicks there; we did not check the $75 Custom Duo Set, which may overlap.

One change

Once the bundle page shows one consistent size, run a new ad for a bundle of in-stock picks sending clicks to the REBL Bundle page against a single in-stock REBL exclusive ad sending clicks to that scent's page, with matched audience and budget. Draft: 'Two REBL exclusives, your pick. Build your bundle for $85.'

02 / Your store

Test a Vanilla Muse travel spray add-on offer

The Vanilla Muse page's paired-purchase widget asks shoppers to add the Fragrance Discovery Kit and REBL Bundle, for a total of $179.93 including the bottle. The store also sells a 10ML Vanilla Muse travel spray, a smaller same-scent add-on that this widget does not offer.

One change

Randomly split Vanilla Muse page visitors. Control keeps the current widget; the variant pairs the bottle with the Vanilla Muse travel spray instead. Draft: 'Add the matching 10ML travel spray, $14.98.'
