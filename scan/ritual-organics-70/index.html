01 / Proposed paid test

Test ingredient-first versus one-step routine ad message

Your homepage leads with six ingredients you can pronounce, and also promises a routine that shrinks from five steps to one. We could not read your Meta ad library, so if you have not already split these two messages in paid ads, which wins more new paid buyers is still open.

One change

Proposed new ad test, not an observed live ad. Both ads send visitors to the same sun balm product page. Ad one hook: "Six ingredients. You can pronounce all of them." Ad two hook: "Five steps down to one. Moisturizer, primer and daily balm in one jar." Keep format, audience and budget identical, and split randomly.

02 / Your store

Test coverage-based labels on the jar quantity picker

The sampled picker labels choices by pack count and savings, and the page already says a jar lasts 2-3 months. Your FAQ states a jar lasts closer to one to two months when also used on arms and chest, but the picker does not link jar count to how someone uses it.

One change

Proposed test, not yet run: show half of visitors the same tiers and prices with a short coverage note under them: "Face and neck: about 2-3 months per jar" and "Adding arms and chest: about 1-2 months per jar". The other half sees the current labels.
