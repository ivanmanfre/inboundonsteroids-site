01 / Proposed paid test

Test a 'we've chosen your inserts' ad against a stimming-cover ad

Your Neurodivergent Bundle page says shoppers need not choose inserts or waste dated pages, while your Roarganiser page lists a built-in stimming tool on the cover. We couldn't read your current Meta ads, so if you haven't already compared these two promises, they are distinct opening messages worth testing against each other.

One change

Proposed new Meta ad test, with both ads sent to the Neurodivergent Bundle page. Ad A: "Your planner inserts, chosen for you. Pick a colour, start whenever you're ready." Ad B: "A soft planner with a built-in stimming tool on the cover. Inserts included." Run as a Meta A/B test with the same audience, budget and destination; only the message changes.

02 / Your store

Test one-tap insert pairing on Daily Planner

Your Daily Planner page suggests combining it with the Weekly Planner and other inserts, and the two are third and fourth in your best-selling list. One homepage review mentions regularly reordering inserts and extras, so a one-step way to add the Weekly Planner may be worth testing.

One change

Add a short choice above the buying button: "Add a Weekly Planner too", which puts both inserts in the basket in one step. Compare it with the current page, which keeps its related products list and any upsell offer.

What we saw
Your Daily Planner page suggests combining it with the Weekly Planner and other inserts, and the two are third and fourth in your best-selling list. One homepage review mentions regularly reordering inserts and extras, so a one-step way to add the Weekly Planner may be worth testing.

What to measure
Revenue per visitor to the Daily Planner page, visitors split evenly between both versions over the same window. Also check the share who buy the Daily Planner at all.

Test setup
Add a short choice above the buying button: "Add a Weekly Planner too", which puts both inserts in the basket in one step. Compare it with the current page, which keeps its related products list and any upsell offer.

03 / Your store

Link Roarganiser page to ready-chosen bundle

Your Roarganiser page says the organiser is sold separately unless bought in a bundle, so inserts are a separate decision. Your bundle page asks whether planner inserts feel overwhelming and offers a ready-chosen set, which may suit visitors unsure where to start.

One change

On the single Roarganiser page, add one line under the buying button linking to the Neurodivergent Bundle: "Not sure which inserts you need? See the ready-chosen bundle." Compare against the current page, with visitors split evenly.
