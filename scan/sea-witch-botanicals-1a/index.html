Finding 1 · Shopper paths

Free shipping starts at $60, and your typical product costs $24.50

A shopper buying one typical item at $24.50 pays for delivery unless they add more to reach $60.

Recommended next step

Test a threshold closer to one or two items, or a bundle priced just above $60.

Free-shipping threshold: $60, against a $24.50 median product price. See this on your storefront

Finding 2 · Shopper paths

22.2% of your variants are sold out while your 41 Meta ads run

You have 41 active ads on Meta, and 22.2% of your variants read out of stock right now. A click that lands on a sold-out option can't turn into an order.

Recommended next step

Pull sold-out products out of your ads and product feed until they're back in stock.

Testing sprawl with no consolidated winner

You have 41 active ads spread across 21 distinct angles. Testing that wide without a consolidated winner spreads budget thin and makes performance hard to read. Pruning to the proven angles and concentrating spend behind them is the lever.
