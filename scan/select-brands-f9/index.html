01 / Paid ads

Test a 60-second bean-to-cup ad against the reminder ad

The sampled espresso ad opens with a reminder. Your page states the machine grinds, tamps and brews in under 60 seconds, which the ad text does not mention.

One change

As an untested proposal, split the audience your current reminder ad reaches between that ad and a new version, both linking to the current product page. New draft: "Fresh espresso in under 60 seconds. Grinds and brews at a touch. Your TRU is still here. $899.99 at Select Brands."

03 / Your store

Add a Double Flip vs 3-in-1 comparison box

Your Mickey range includes the six-waffle Double Flip ($99.99) and the 3-in-1 waffle-and-donut maker with three plate sets ($89.99): close in price, different jobs. Each page describes only its own machine, though both offer an "Add to compare" button.

One change

On the Double Flip and 3-in-1 product pages, add a short "Double Flip or 3-in-1?" box. Draft: "Double Flip ($99.99): 6 Mickey waffles at once. 3-in-1 ($89.99): one large Mickey waffle, 4 icon waffles or 4 donuts with swappable plates." Randomly show it to half of visitors on each page.

Your Mickey range includes the six-waffle Double Flip ($99.99) and the 3-in-1 waffle-and-donut maker with three plate sets ($89.99): close in price, different jobs. Each page describes only its own machine, though both offer an "Add to compare" button.
