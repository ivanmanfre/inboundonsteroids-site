Paid-media check

Your longest-running ads are past the fatigue window

You have 178 active ads, the oldest running 68 days across 30 distinct angles. Half your live ads have been running 49 days or longer. The oldest one opens with "Something blue. 💙 Triple Lipid-Peptide Cream’s original formula is back (!". Past the first several weeks on one angle, fewer people click and each click costs more. A steady creative refresh cadence is the lever that holds performance from sliding.

Testing sprawl with no consolidated winner

You have 178 active ads spread across 30 distinct angles. Testing that wide without a consolidated winner spreads budget thin and makes performance hard to read. Pruning to the proven angles and concentrating spend behind them is the lever.
