01 / Paid ads

Test a usage guide on the hair serum page

The page says the serum is for daily scalp use but, in the text sampled, gives no amount per application. One review says the 30 ml bottle ran out in two weeks and asks for clearer amount and frequency guidance. That makes the 30 ml versus 50 ml choice hard to judge.

One change

Split ad traffic between the current page and a version with a short usage guide for each size, with the ad unchanged. Draft: 'How to use: [X] pipettes, [Y] times a week. 30 ml lasts about [Z] weeks.' Fill the numbers in from your formulation team.

02 / Paid ads

Test a no-white-film opening line on the dry shampoo ad

The dry shampoo ad opens with a founder story, then promises a fresh feel in one minute without white cast. Your page states it leaves no visible white film. It is unclear whether the founder story or the concrete result line is what moves people to buy.

One change

Run the current ad against a copy with the same image and landing page but a new first line. Draft: 'Torrschampo utan vit hinna. Nytvättad känsla på 1 minut.' The founder sentence moves to the end. Only the opening line changes.
