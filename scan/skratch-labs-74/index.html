Your longest-running ads are past the fatigue window

You have 166 active ads, the oldest running 108 days across 21 distinct angles. Half your live ads have been running 59 days or longer. The oldest one opens with "Skratch Labs is a sports nutrition company based in Boulder, Colorado.". Past the first several weeks on one angle, fewer people click and each click costs more. A steady creative refresh cadence is the lever that holds performance from sliding.

Most of your creative is dynamic catalog

Of the 96 ads we sampled, 61.5% run in a dynamic catalog format. When most of the creative is feed-driven, product feed quality becomes the creative that decides performance. Tightening feed titles, imagery, and hero products is the fastest win here.
