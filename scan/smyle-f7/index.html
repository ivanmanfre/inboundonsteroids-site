Where we’d focus

What we’d test first

01

Test the Duo-pack as the initial selection on your eBrush landing page

Your Sonic Electric Toothbrush landing page already offers a Duo-pack and a 1-pack, with 50% off the second brush and a 60-day trial. In our English EU browser check, the 1-pack was selected when the page opened.

Proposed test

Split visitors to this landing page evenly. Keep the current 1-pack selection in one version; select the existing Duo-pack initially in the other. Let shoppers switch back to one brush easily. Keep prices, discounts, colors and trial terms unchanged. Test whether the initial selection changes total sales, rather than adding a new bundle.

How we’d measure it

Compare revenue per landing-page visitor. Check profit per visitor after the second-brush discount and refunds during the 60-day trial. Single-brush and duo order counts help explain the result.

02

Show the existing 12-week whitening refill plan beside Add to cart

Your whitening refill product data already lists a recurring plan called Delivery every 12 weeks. In our English EU browser check, the purchase area showed an Add to cart button without a subscription selector. Your sensitive refill page shows an explicit subscription choice.

Proposed test

Compare the current whitening refill page with a version showing the existing Delivery every 12 weeks plan beside Add to cart. Keep one-time purchase as the initial choice. Connect the selector to the existing plan, show its actual price and renewal terms, and keep the product and prices unchanged. Split page visitors evenly. Confirm the live plan can be purchased before starting the test.

How we’d measure it

Compare refill revenue per page visitor over 180 days. Check profit after fulfilment costs, cancellations and refunds in both versions. Track plan uptake and completed renewals to explain the result.
