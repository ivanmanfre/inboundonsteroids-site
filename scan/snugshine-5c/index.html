01 / Paid ads

Test gift-led ad copy against current lights-out ad

Your four active Meta ads share one text that opens on a bedtime problem, 'Does your child find lights-out hard?', and the three we read link to your Instagram profile, not a product page. Your product page calls the set 'A ready-made gift set' and homepage reviews mention Christmas and birthday gifts, but the ad text never mentions gifts.

One change

Copy your lights-out ad, link the copy to the Glimmer set page instead of Instagram, and run it against a new gift-led ad to the same page in a Meta split test with the same audience and equal budget. Draft gift-led ad: 'A unicorn gift that glows at night. Glimmer, her storybook and a projector torch in one ready-made set.'

02 / Your store

Test a Plush and Storybook choice without the torch

The set page sells plush, book and torch together, with batteries required for the torch and not included. One review says the buyer wished they had bought the toy and not the whole package that included the torch, so the torch may hold back some buyers.

One change

Randomly show half of visitors a second choice on the set page, beside the current full set. Draft label: 'Plush + Storybook: the glowing unicorn and her bedtime story'. Set its price only after checking margin; this is a package test, not a copy tweak.

What we saw
The set page sells plush, book and torch together, with batteries required for the torch and not included. One review says the buyer wished they had bought the toy and not the whole package that included the torch, so the torch may hold back some buyers.

What to measure
Purchases and first-order revenue per visitor, same visitors and window for both versions (about 4 weeks). Also check margin per visitor so a cheaper choice does not cut profit.

Test setup
Randomly show half of visitors a second choice on the set page, beside the current full set. Draft label: 'Plush + Storybook: the glowing unicorn and her bedtime story'. Set its price only after checking margin; this is a package test, not a copy tweak.

03 / Your store

Offer the Bedtime Routine Pack as a Glimmer add-on

The set page's 'You may also like' row shows the Colouring Pack and Affirmation Colouring Book, not the Bedtime Routine Pack, but the page also loads an upsell app whose offers we could not see. Your homepage ties Glimmer to a complete bedtime routine, and the 8-page routine pack costs £5.00, so it fits as an add-on.

One change

On the Glimmer set page, add a tick-box under Add to Cart: 'Add the Bedtime Routine Pack (8 printable pages) for £5.00.' Randomly give half of visitors this version and keep the current page for the rest. If your upsell app already offers this pack, skip this test.
