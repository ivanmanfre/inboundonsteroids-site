01 / Paid ads

Settle one pH duration across baby ads and page

The store gives two different pH-duration figures for the baby cream. One sampled baby ad says it is designed to "correct and stabilize baby skin pH for up to 6 hours", while the baby cream page it links to states 12+ hours. Another sampled baby ad also says 12+ hours.

One change

First confirm which pH duration your testing supports for the baby cream; if only 6 hours holds, change the page and every 12 hour ad now rather than test an unsupported claim. If 12+ hours holds, run a copy of the 6 hour video ad, changing only that line and any on-screen mention to 12+ hours, and send both versions to the same baby cream page.

02 / Your store

Show the two-step routine beside kit subscription

The kit page offers "Subscribe & Save 15%" and names a 2-Step treatment, but when to use each cream only appears lower under How to Use. Your contact page says subscribers get 40% off the Starter Kit and that a subscription "includes two shipments" before you can cancel; the kit page text we read does not mention that minimum.

One change

First confirm the current kit subscription discount and minimum-shipment terms, and show both the same way on the kit and contact pages for all visitors. Then, for half of kit page visitors only, add one line under the kit purchase options: "Your routine: Active Flare-Up Cream for 2 weeks, then Long-Term Cream for at least 4 weeks."
