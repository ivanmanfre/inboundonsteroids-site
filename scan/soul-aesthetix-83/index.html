01 / Paid ads

Test sending the $35 Soul Discovery ad to its own product page

Three of your eight live ads, including the 'Save $115' Soul Discovery ad ($35), link to the sale collection. That ad says "see expiry dates on the product page" and sends shoppers to the sale collection, whose full text we did not read. All 13 products carry a markdown, so the set is likely one of several items there.

One change

Untested proposal: use Meta's A/B test to split the 'Save $115' ad between the sale collection and the Soul Discovery product page, with identical copy, audience and budget, before the offer ends 30 November. That page shows the serums' 11 October best-before date, now passed, which may lift trust or cut orders. The split shows which destination earns more per click. It cannot isolate the effect of the date.

02 / Your store

Add a one, two or four set picker

The Soul Discovery page shows one set with a quantity box and Add to cart. The two-set and four-set options appear on your collection pages, but we saw no link to them on this page, so shoppers here cannot easily compare them. None of your eight live ads link here.

One change

Untested proposal: add three choice cards beside Add to cart for 1, 2 or 4 sets ($35, $60, $99.95), each linking to the existing listing. Draft line: "Choose one, two or four sets. Same 30-day money back guarantee." The current single-set page stays as the comparison. Each card should repeat the best-before dates, since the serums' 11 October date has passed. Watch refund requests closely.
