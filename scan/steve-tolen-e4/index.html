01Posts

Reach recruitment leaders who want more BD conversations.

Steve TolenStolen+Co

Your years in recruitment are the best reason for a buyer to take your call.

At a Calls That Convert workshop, it struck me again how often recruiters undervalue that experience. Ten or twenty years of talking to clients and candidates every day gives you a view of the market that most hiring managers never get.

Experts want to deal with experts.

So before you pick up the phone, ask yourself three things:

  1. What trend am I seeing in my market right now?
  2. How could it affect the person I want to talk to?
  3. What is my take on it, from everything I have seen?

Then open the call with that trend and your view on it. Ask how it looks from their side, and listen.

It won't guarantee a sale. It does give the other person a reason to talk, and that is where trust starts.

Your experience is an asset, so use it.

02Lead magnets

Turn readers into a list you own.

Written for recruitment leaders who want a worked example of turning one market trend into a call opener, questions and follow-up for their next BD call.

Steve TolenStolen+Co

A good BD call starts with something worth saying.

The Market Take Call Plan is a worked document that shows how to get there. It takes one trend you are seeing in your niche and turns it into a call opener, a set of questions and a follow-up plan.

It includes two filled-in examples:

  • A nursing agency calling the Director of Nursing at an aged care group
  • A tech recruitment firm calling a Head of Engineering at a robotics scale-up

Each one runs through the same steps:

  1. The trend in one sentence
  2. How it affects that buyer
  3. Your take, drawn from your own experience
  4. The opener, written out word for word
  5. The questions to ask, and what to listen for
  6. The follow-up touches after the call
  7. How to check cost, timing and value before you propose

Use the examples as a model, then write your own for the next person on your call list.

Most of the work is in steps 1 to 3. Get those right and the call is far easier to open.
