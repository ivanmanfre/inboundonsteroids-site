03 / Proposed paid test

Settle one guarantee promise, then test it against a usage-led ad

The NextGen page promises a full refund with no questions asked; its guarantee page allows approval or denial at your sole discretion and names Tavo Replenisher. Your article pages add “Less Stressed & Irritated Or A Full Refund” and “7 Nights Of Euphoria Or A Full Refund”, so a proposed guarantee ad needs one consistent promise.

One changeProposed Meta test, once usage, guarantee terms and stock are settled: compare a usage-led opening (evening routine, if approved) with “Try LiquidCalm with a 60-day money-back guarantee.” Use the same product image, Irish audience, budget and LiquidCalm page. Change only the opening.

What to measure

04 / Your store

Test which pack new shoppers see selected first

The page describes 35 nights; its 20-stick default shows €21.95 and Free Shipping, below the shipping policy’s €30 threshold. The 40-stick button shows €35.90. Its €17.95 per-bag price matches a subscription plan, alongside no subscription required copy. The sold-out error left the actual charge and terms unconfirmed.

One changeProposed test, after both packs can be bought and their prices, delivery fees and one-off or subscription terms are confirmed: compare 20 sticks selected first with 40 selected first. Keep the buying terms and copy unchanged. Check your buying widget supports this. Run it after the ad-message test, with the same ad in both versions.
