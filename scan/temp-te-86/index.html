Finding 1 · Shopper paths

Your best seller, Sequin V-Neck Mini Dress, is sold out

Shoppers who click through to Sequin V-Neck Mini Dress reach a Sold out button, so anyone who came for it can't order it today.

Recommended next step

Restock it, or move an in-stock product into its best-seller and homepage spots until it's back.

Best-selling order on your store: Your best seller, marked sold out. See this on your storefront

Finding 2 · Shopper paths

9.9% of your products are sold out while your 6 Meta ads run

You have 6 active ads on Meta, and 9.9% of your products read out of stock right now. A click that lands on a sold-out option can't turn into an order.

Recommended next step

Pull sold-out products out of your ads and product feed until they're back in stock.

Out of stock right now: 9.9% of your products. See this on your storefront

Finding 3 · Shopper paths

No express checkout button on Sequin lace camisole top

A shopper on Sequin lace camisole top has to add to cart, open the cart and start checkout before they can pay. There's no one-tap button for shoppers who saved their card or use a phone wallet.

Recommended next step

Turn on dynamic checkout buttons in your theme's product page settings.

Paid-media check

Your longest-running ads are past the fatigue window

You have 6 active ads, the oldest running 189 days across 2 distinct angles. Half your live ads have been running 62 days or longer. Past the first several weeks on one angle, fewer people click and each click costs more. A steady creative refresh cadence is the lever that holds performance from sliding.
