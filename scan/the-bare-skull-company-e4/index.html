Where we’d focus

What we’d test first

01

Test the Starter Kit page for the "Love your bald man" ad

The "Love your bald man" ad sells the moisturiser and lands on its page, which already suggests the Starter Kit. Another ad aimed at partners, "He won’t research it, so we did", lands on the Starter Kit page with different copy, so comparing them can't show whether the page or the message made the difference.

Proposed test

Keep the "Love your bald man" ad copy unchanged and run a Meta A/B test on one audience: one version lands on the moisturiser page, the other on the Starter Kit page. Only the landing page changes. This is an untested proposal.

How we’d measure it

Winner: first-order revenue per ad click, one audience split at random over the same dates. Also watch purchase rate and profit per order; review guarantee claims six weeks after purchase.

02

Test a Starter Kit hero button against moisturiser

The homepage tells visitors that most men start with the moisturiser, while its FAQ on which kit to start with says most men start with the Starter Kit. One answers the single product question and one the kit question, so which should lead the hero button is untested.

Proposed test

Split new homepage visitors 50/50. Control keeps the hero button "Shop Skull Matte Moisturiser". The variant reads "Shop the Starter Kit" with the line "Cleanser + moisturiser. Two minutes a day." and links to the Starter Kit page. Nothing else changes.

How we’d measure it

Winner: first-order revenue per visitor, comparing both versions over the same period. Also watch orders per visitor. Hero button clicks are for information only.
