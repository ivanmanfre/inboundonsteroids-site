Where we’d focus

What we’d test first

01

Test heritage ads against fabric-first ads on your best seller, the Ní mo Rí é tee

Six of the 11 ads we read use your heritage story and link to all products or your homepage. The catalog ad, "Oversized, unisex and built to last", links to your best-selling olive Ní mo Rí é tee, whose page opens with fabric and fit. Landing on different pages, these ads can't show which message sells this tee.

Proposed test

Proposed, untested: run both in one Meta A/B test with the same olive Ní mo Rí é tee page on the domain your ads already use, audience, budget and visuals. A (culture): "Wear your culture. The oversized Ní mo Rí é tee, Irish streetwear with a bold back print." B (fabric): "Heavyweight 260gsm organic cotton. Oversized fit, bold Irish back print."

How we’d measure it

Winner: olive tee purchases per €100 spent, same A/B window, at least two weeks and a purchase count set in advance. Pause both if a size sells out.
