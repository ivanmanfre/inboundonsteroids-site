Finding 1 · Shopper paths

Your best seller, Banana-Banana Deep Conditioner Sachet 6-Pack, is sold out

Shoppers who click through to Banana-Banana Deep Conditioner Sachet 6-Pack reach a Sold out button, so anyone who came for it can't order it today.

Recommended next step

Restock it, or move an in-stock product into its best-seller and homepage spots until it's back.

Best-selling order on your store: Your best seller, marked sold out. See this on your storefront

Finding 2 · Shopper paths

Your shipping policy page is blank

A shopper who checks what delivery costs before buying finds an empty page, and your homepage mentions no free-shipping offer.

Recommended next step

Publish your real rates and delivery times, and add a free-shipping threshold just above your typical order.

Shipping policy page: Blank, and no free-shipping offer on your homepage. See this on your storefront

Finding 3 · Shopper paths

The 3 five-star reviews on your best seller don't show to shoppers

The rating is in your product page code, but the star badge under the product name renders empty and the review list is blank, so a first-time shopper sees no proof from past buyers.

Recommended next step

Move to a review app that renders on your theme and import the reviews you already have.

Rating in your product page code: 5.0 stars from 3 reviews. See this on your product page

Finding 4 · Repeat purchase

No subscribe or refill option on Banana-Banana Conditioner

Banana-Banana Conditioner is something a customer uses up, but its page only sells one-time orders, so the next order depends on them remembering to come back.

Recommended next step

Add subscribe and save to Banana-Banana Conditioner, with a small discount on each repeat delivery.

Subscribe option on the products you sell most: None on the 5 we checked. See this on your product page

Banana-Banana Conditioner · From $60

Finding 5 · Repeat purchase

Your sign-up form sits in the footer with nothing offered for joining

There's no sign-up pop-up and no reason to leave an email, so a first visit that ends without an order leaves you no way to reach that shopper again.

Recommended next step

Add a sign-up pop-up with a first-order offer so first-time visitors join before they leave.

Sign-up pop-up: No pop-up running, footer form only. See this on your storefront
