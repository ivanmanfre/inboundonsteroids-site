02 / Your store

Recommend one pack after pet size choice

Your Pet page asks for the pet's size before showing two pack options. Your discovery page calls the 12-vial pack a simple start for smaller pets, so pack choice is already tied to pet size.

One changeAfter a shopper picks a size, preselect one pack and show a short reason beside it, with the other pack still selectable. Draft: "Best fit for your medium dog: the 30-vial pack covers a longer routine." Control keeps both packs shown equally.
Sources & how to check the result +

What to measure
First-order revenue per Pet page visitor in both arms. Guardrail: return requests within 30 days and share of orders on the smaller pack.

03 / Your store

Tie subscribe-and-save to when the pack runs out

Your Pet page already offers a subscription discount alongside the one-time purchase. The option reads as a plain discount and does not link the delivery schedule to how long the chosen pack lasts.

One changeOn the Pet page, rewrite the subscription option so it states the saving and when the next pack ships, based on the selected pack. Draft: "Subscribe and save 10%. Your next pack ships as this one runs low." Control keeps the current wording.
Sources & how to check the result +

What to measure
First-order revenue per Pet page visitor in both arms. Track second-shipment revenue separately over at least 16 weeks. Guardrail: subscription cancellations and refund requests.
