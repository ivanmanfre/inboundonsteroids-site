01 / Proposed paid test

Test craft versus fit-proof ads for roll neck sweaters

A visitor cannot try on a hand-loomed sweater, and returns are at the buyer's cost within 14 days, so fit doubt matters, especially for buyers abroad. The Gorse page, your best seller, states origin and wool content, has a size chart and a review about fit, so the lead message is the open question.

One change

Proposed new ad test, not an observed live ad. Run two Meta ads to the same Gorse sweater page. Ad A: "Hand-loomed in Donegal. Roll neck sweater, 100% pure new wool." Ad B: "A customer review says: Sizing is perfect. Check the size chart, pick your size." The page marks that review as rewarded, so check ad disclosure rules first.

02 / Your store

Offer in-stock wool socks beside the sweater Add to cart

The sampled Gorse sweater page presents a single-item purchase, while the shop also lists wool socks at a much lower price. That gives a small, low-commitment item to pair with a sweater, which is worth testing as a way to raise order value.

One change

Show half of sweater-page visitors an optional in-stock sock selector under Add to cart with the line: "Add Donegal wool socks to your sweater." The other half see the page unchanged.

Full test plan & sources +

What we saw
The sampled Gorse sweater page presents a single-item purchase, while the shop also lists wool socks at a much lower price. That gives a small, low-commitment item to pair with a sweater, which is worth testing as a way to raise order value.

What to measure
First-order revenue per eligible sweater-page visitor, comparing the sock version against the unchanged page over the same dates. Also watch sweater purchase rate and return requests.

Test setup
Show half of sweater-page visitors an optional in-stock sock selector under Add to cart with the line: "Add Donegal wool socks to your sweater." The other half see the page unchanged.

03 / Your store

Show all roll-neck colours on each sweater page

The sampled sweater pages disagree on the colour range: Gorse says five colours, Lough Balshard four classic colours, Pint of Plain a variety, and the best sellers list five roll-neck colours. One reviewer wrote they would come back for other colours, so showing the whole range is a decision worth testing.

One change

First confirm the real colour count. Then show half of visitors a named colour row linking to every roll-neck colour under the size chart. The other half see the current page.
