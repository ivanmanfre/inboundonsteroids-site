01 / Paid ads

Swap "launching soon" for a ship date on the Whiskey Cloak ad page

Your live Meta ad, running since 11 November, sells the €59 Whiskey Cloak as a Christmas gift, and the page it links to repeats the free coasters offer clearly. Lower down, that page says "First batch launching soon" and gives no dispatch date, so a gift buyer cannot tell whether it will arrive before Christmas.

One change

Untested proposal: use Meta's A/B test to split the ad between the current page and a copy where "First batch launching soon" is replaced by one dated line beside Add to cart. If it is in stock: "In stock. Order by [last posting date for that country] for Christmas delivery." If made to order, give the ship date. Same ad, audience and budget.

02 / Paid ads

Test craft versus fit-proof ads for Gorse, your reviewed best seller

The one live ad we found backs the Whiskey Cloak, whose page has no reviews. Gorse, first in your best-seller list at €115, shows 5.00 out of 5 from 21 reviews, and we found no ad for it. A sweater cannot be tried on and the buyer pays return postage, so a fit review is worth testing as ad copy.

One change

Untested proposal: run two Gorse ads to the same sweater page. Ad A: "Hand-loomed in Donegal. Roll neck sweater, 100% pure new wool." Ad B: "A customer review says: Sizing is perfect. Check the size chart, pick your size." The page marks that review as rewarded, so check ad disclosure rules first. One Gorse size shows sold out today, so check every size before spending.

03 / Your store

Test one clear returns line where your policy pages disagree

Your refund policy page still shows the placeholder "[INSERT RETURN ADDRESS]". It says 30 days and that you send a return label, then later that the buyer pays return shipping. Your Delivery & Returns page says 14 days. The shipping policy page has a title and no text. A buyer abroad weighing a €115 sweater may read these first.

One change

Untested proposal: show half of sweater-page visitors one line under Add to cart: "Returns within [X] days, unworn. You pay return postage." The other half see the page unchanged. Before it starts, make the policy pages state those same terms so the line and the pages agree.

04 / Your store

Offer in-stock wool socks beside the sweater Add to cart

Socks rank right after your five roll neck sweaters in the best-selling list, at €16 against €115, and the sampled Gorse page offers nothing alongside the sweater. That makes socks a natural, low-commitment add-on worth testing to raise order value.

One change

Untested proposal: show half of sweater-page visitors an optional in-stock sock selector under Add to cart with the line: "Add Donegal wool socks to your sweater." The other half see the page unchanged.

What we saw
Socks rank right after your five roll neck sweaters in the best-selling list, at €16 against €115, and the sampled Gorse page offers nothing alongside the sweater. That makes socks a natural, low-commitment add-on worth testing to raise order value.

What to measure
First-order revenue per eligible sweater-page visitor, comparing the sock version against the unchanged page over the same dates. Also watch sweater purchase rate and return requests.
