01 / Paid ads

Test the page's £230 vs £69 cost comparison as an ad

The two founder videos we read cite spending almost £200 a month. Your product page carries a sharper side-by-side of indicative separate supplement cost against the Uthful subscription price. None of the three ads we read, of 18 active, uses it.

One change

Run a new ad built on the page's comparison against the over-35 founder video, both sent to the same product page and audience. Draft: "Buying this stack separately: about £230 a month at our indicative prices. Uthful on subscription, in one daily scoop: £69 a month."

02 / Paid ads

Name the later free gifts beside the subscription plans

Paid visitors are told about £60+ of free gifts, but the page text sampled values only the Welcome Kit at £40. The month two and three gifts are mentioned without names or values, so the full subscription offer is less concrete at the moment of choosing a plan.

One change

In the subscription plan box, name the month two and month three gifts and their values, using only the store's real figures. Draft: "Welcome Kit worth £40 now, plus [gift] in month two and [gift] in month three."

03 / Your store

Offer new solo subscribers the two-person plan after checkout

Your page already offers a two-person subscription with two 30-day supplies for £120, against £69 for one person. That existing higher-value plan could be offered to solo buyers once the first order is placed.

One change

On the order confirmation page, randomly show half of new solo subscribers a button to switch to the two-person plan. Draft: "Make it two? Switch to the 2 person plan for £120 a month from your next delivery."

Full test plan & sources +

What we saw
Your page already offers a two-person subscription with two 30-day supplies for £120, against £69 for one person. That existing higher-value plan could be offered to solo buyers once the first order is placed.

What to measure
Compare both groups on subscription revenue per new solo subscriber over the same 60 days (two deliveries). Also check cancellations and refund requests in that window.

Test setup
On the order confirmation page, randomly show half of new solo subscribers a button to switch to the two-person plan. Draft: "Make it two? Switch to the 2 person plan for £120 a month from your next delivery."
