Finding 1 · Shopper paths

7.3% of your variants are sold out while your 58 Meta ads run

You have 58 active ads on Meta, and 7.3% of your variants read out of stock right now. A click that lands on a sold-out option can't turn into an order.

Recommended next step

Pull sold-out products out of your ads and product feed until they're back in stock.

Finding 2 · Shopper paths

No express checkout button on VersaTails Dog Bed Cover, Replacement, Washable

A shopper on VersaTails Dog Bed Cover, Replacement, Washable has to add to cart, open the cart and start checkout before they can pay. There's no one-tap button for shoppers who saved their card or use a phone wallet.

Recommended next step

Turn on dynamic checkout buttons in your theme's product page settings.
