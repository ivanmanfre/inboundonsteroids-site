01 / Your store

Clarify the daily serving on the NMN page

The Brain Longevity Complex page says "Six actives in one daily capsule" and "in one capsule a day", but its FAQ says "Three capsules a day, with food" and another line says "three delayed-release capsule". Shoppers see this conflict while choosing between subscribing and a one-time purchase.

One change

Once the store confirms the correct serving, fix every serving mention for all visitors first, not as a test: the headline, "One capsule. Six actives.", the comparison table and the FAQ. Then test one serving line under the subscribe and one-time options, for example "Serving: [confirmed number] capsules daily with food".

02 / Your store

Test a free-delivery Omega prompt on Magnesium

At £24.99, a one-time Magnesium-only order falls under the £35 free-delivery line shown on the Magnesium page and FAQ, and the Magnesium page shows no pairing prompt beside Add to Cart. The collection page marks Omega as sold out, and we did not check delivery terms for subscription orders.

One change

Once Omega is back in stock, add a line after Add to Cart on the Magnesium page: "Add Vegan Omega Brain Support+ and get free express delivery."
