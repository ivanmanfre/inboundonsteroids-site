Finding 1 · Shopper paths

A $29.99 creatine order is below your $100 free-shipping threshold

The advertised powder page shows $29.99 for the selected one-time purchase and free shipping from $100. One tub is $70.01 below that threshold. Shipping is calculated at checkout. That makes delivery cost worth checking on entry orders; it does not establish a checkout drop-off rate.

Recommended next step

Check completed-order contribution after discounts and shipping before changing the threshold. Test clear delivery-cost expectations on the creatine landing page against the current page, and compare completed purchases and contribution per visitor.

Finding 2 · Repeat purchase

Your 60-serving creatine defaults to a refill every 21 days

The selected Peach Mango Rings powder has 60 servings. The page says to use one scoop daily, but Subscribe & Save is selected with delivery every three weeks. At that stated daily use, the next tub is scheduled while 39 servings remain. This is a replenishment timing mismatch; the public page does not tell us whether customers skip or cancel.

Recommended next step

Test the available eight-week interval for this 60-serving powder: 56 days is closer to the stated 60-day supply. Compare successful second charges, skips, cancellations and contribution over the same 120-day window. Check actual usage and delivery lead time before choosing the default; use separate timing for other sizes and formats.

Paid-media check

Test powder value against gummy convenience

The selected flavored powder and watermelon gummies both show a $25.49 subscription price. The powder has 60 servings; the gummy label shows 30 servings of five gummies. That works out to about $0.42 versus $0.85 per serving, before shipping and tax. The sampled public ad copy emphasizes taste, easy mixing and no shaker required; it does not establish which ads are winners.

Next account check

Test a powder creative that explains cost per serving against a gummy creative showing convenience while traveling. Send each to its matching product page with serving count and refill timing clear. Compare new-customer acquisition cost and contribution per acquired customer using actual ad-account and order data.
