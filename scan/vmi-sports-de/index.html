Where shoppers drop off

  1. Free shipping starts at $100, and your typical product costs $29.99

    A shopper buying one typical item at $29.99 pays for delivery unless they add more to reach $100.

    The fix

    Test a threshold closer to one or two items, or a bundle priced just above $100.

    Free-shipping threshold: $100, against a $29.99 median product price. See this on your storefront

  2. 31.4% of your variants are sold out while your 11 Meta ads run

    You have 11 active ads on Meta, and 31.4% of your variants read out of stock right now. A click that lands on a sold-out option can't turn into an order.

    The fix

    Pull sold-out products out of your ads and product feed until they're back in stock.
