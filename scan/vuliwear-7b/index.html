01 / Your store

Offer the Signature Set on the BLKNBLU page

Your homepage offers eyewear with the BEBA Crossbody at 12% off, and the shop lists a BEBA Signature Set at $638.00. The sampled BLKNBLU page text shows a single Add to cart and no set option.

One change

Show half of BLKNBLU visitors a card under Add to cart: "Carry them protected. Get the Signature Set and save 12%." It links to the set page, once the set is confirmed to include BLKNBLU. The other half see the page unchanged.

02 / Your store

Show US shipping and return terms beside the price

The sampled BLKNBLU and VAMP pages say shipping is calculated at checkout. Your shipping policy states free US shipping, and your returns policy states a 15-day refund or exchange window.

One change

Show half of US visitors on the BLKNBLU and VAMP pages the line "Free US shipping. Return or exchange within 15 days, in original packaging." The other half keep the current shipping line.
