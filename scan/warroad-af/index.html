Finding 1 · Shopper paths

9.8% of your products are sold out while your 1042 Meta ads run

You have 1042 active ads on Meta, and 9.8% of your products read out of stock right now. A click that lands on a sold-out option can't turn into an order.

Recommended next step

Pull sold-out products out of your ads and product feed until they're back in stock.

Out of stock right now: 9.8% of your products. See this on your storefront

Finding 2 · Shopper paths

No express checkout button on Elite TILO Neck & Wrist Top - Youth

A shopper on Elite TILO Neck & Wrist Top - Youth has to add to cart, open the cart and start checkout before they can pay. There's no one-tap button for shoppers who saved their card or use a phone wallet.

Recommended next step

Turn on dynamic checkout buttons in your theme's product page settings.

Express checkout button on the product page: Not on the page. See this on your product page

Paid-media check

Testing sprawl with no consolidated winner

You have 1042 active ads spread across 38 distinct angles. Testing that wide without a consolidated winner spreads budget thin and makes performance hard to read. Pruning to the proven angles and concentrating spend behind them is the lever.

Next account check

By day two we are cutting losers and scaling winners, managing to Total Profit rather than platform metrics.

Most of your creative is dynamic catalog

Of the 114 ads we sampled, 67.5% run in a dynamic catalog format. When most of the creative is feed-driven, product feed quality becomes the creative that decides performance. Tightening feed titles, imagery, and hero products is the fastest win here.
