01

Test US direct sales on one product with a capped ad budget

Your site is set up for retailers: "Free Freight on Preseason Orders", a store locator covering only Spain, Andorra and Udine, and showings at Outdoor Retailer and GOA. Your homepage pitches hiking pants for "the Appalachian Trail to the Rocky Mountains", but US hikers find no nearby store, and the Three-Season pants page shows "0,00 €, 100,00 €".

Proposed test

If US direct sales are open for discussion, sell the Men's Three-Season Hiking Pants in dollars with US shipping, duties and returns covered, at your planned US retail price so stockists are not undercut. Install the Meta pixel, then run one new Meta ad to US hiking audiences on a fixed budget, leading with its 4-way stretch and PFC-free DWR finish. This is an untested proposal.

How we’d measure it

Cost per US order, from store records, over 14 days, against a threshold set before launch that covers shipping and duties. A first signal of US demand for retailer talks.
