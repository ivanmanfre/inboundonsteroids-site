01

Offer Bonder with Primer

Both pages say the products are used together. The pairing appears in their product descriptions.

Proposed test

Add a Bonder + Primer button to the Primer page, with a short explanation of the two steps. Compare sales with the current page.

How we’d measure it

Sales per visitor to the Primer page. Also check Primer-only orders and returns.

Bring RYAN’s handling advice closer to the buy button

Purchases per RYAN page visitor. Also check returns and comments about lamp heat.

Finding 1 · Repeat purchase

No subscribe or refill option on Builder Gel PHILIP

Builder Gel PHILIP is something a customer uses up, but its page only sells one-time orders, so the next order depends on them remembering to come back.

Recommended next step

Test a subscribe-and-save option on Builder Gel PHILIP, with a small discount on each repeat delivery.
