Where we’d focus

What we’d test first

01

Show that 10 last-chance pouches ship free

Your last-chance flavors such as Pumpkin Spice sell at $15.00, down from $29.00, and your FAQ says "orders of $150 or more ship free". Ten of those pouches come to exactly $150. The homepage text we read never connects the two, so a shopper weighing frozen shipping has to work out the free-shipping point alone.

Proposed test

Split homepage visitors 50/50. The variant adds one line above the Last Chance Flavors grid: "10 last-chance pouches = $150, and orders of $150 or more ship free." The control keeps the current page. First check whether the $150 threshold counts the order before or after discount codes, and word the line to match. Nothing else changes. This is an untested proposal.

How we’d measure it

Winner: profit per visitor after shipping costs, both versions over the same dates. Also watch revenue per visitor and the share of orders at $150 or more.
