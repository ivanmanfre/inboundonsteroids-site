We dropped a creator this year over one video. Big Polish channel. The video before it grew hard, the kind of number you screenshot. Then the next one landed badly, low views, a run of things going wrong at once, and he's off the list. Nobody has called him since. I was in the conversation where it happened. 2. In Serbia it is Viber from your calls

Your internal call, 17 Aug. You and Bruna going through which chat apps each market actually uses.

DS
Davorin SmitFounder, ARCH · Creator-led UA for games & apps1d · 🌐
Every community playbook says the same thing. WhatsApp groups, broadcast channels, that is where the audience nobody is buying sits. We went through our own markets last week to check. In the Balkans, WhatsApp communities mostly are not there. What people actually use is Discord, Facebook, sometimes Telegram. Instagram broadcast channels, yes, those work. And in Serbia the number one chat app is Viber. My own relatives there install WhatsApp only so they can talk to us. Some of our creators have run Viber groups. That is not in anybody's deck. If a plan for that region says WhatsApp communities, nobody checked.

3. Where the threshold sits from your calls

Your internal call, 17 Aug. The flat fee plus CPI shape, from the meeting action plan.

DS
Davorin SmitFounder, ARCH · Creator-led UA for games & apps1d · 🌐
We are testing a pay shape on creator deals: a smaller flat fee, plus a bonus once installs pass an agreed number. The reasoning is dull. A flat fee buys a video. It doesn't buy anything after the video is up, and what happens after the video is up is most of the result. The part we have not solved is where the number goes. Put it low and it's a fee with extra paperwork, and everyone knows it. Put it high and the creator reads the bonus as decoration and prices the flat fee as if the bonus does not exist. Which they should, if it doesn't. I do not have a rule for it yet. Right now we are setting it per campaign, off what that creator did last time, which works and does not scale. If you have run these, I want to know what you anchored the threshold to.
