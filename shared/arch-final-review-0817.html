1. One video is one data point from your calls

Your internal call, 17 Aug. You on the big creators who got dropped after one bad video.

DS
Davorin SmitFounder, ARCH · Creator-led UA for games & apps1d · 🌐
We have run a lot of big creators who did not perform, and I think we were wrong about a lot of them. The pattern goes like this. A creator does one campaign, the video lands badly, and they come off the list. Ours and everyone else's, because everyone is looking at the same number in the same week. The video before that one may have been the best thing they published all year and nobody goes back to check. One video is one data point. We have been treating it as a verdict. So there is a tier of creators sitting in that hole right now. Proven once, unlucky once, priced as a risk by every agency at the same time. I want to go back through ours and re-test them on a different shape. Smaller flat fee, bonus if installs pass a number agreed up front. That way finding out costs us almost nothing on the ones we are still wrong about.

2. Every video went through me first from your calls

Your internal call, 17 Aug. You describing how the CEO used to check every video before it went out.

DS
Davorin SmitFounder, ARCH · Creator-led UA for games & apps1d · 🌐
Before we grew, no video left the agency until I watched it. I had the most context in the building, so I could usually tell which ones would underperform before there was any data on them. A creator can follow a brief exactly and still hand you something that will do nothing. I can't be that filter anymore. So it moved. On a new client, someone senior watches every single video for the first two months, before the client sees a frame of it. After that we loosen it, once we know we're reading quality the same way they are. It costs hours we don't bill. I haven't found the version of this that scales.

3. 40 scouted, 6 pitched from your calls

Your internal call, 17 Aug. Bruna's scouting count for one campaign track: 40 found, 6 pitched.

DS
Davorin SmitFounder, ARCH · Creator-led UA for games & apps1d · 🌐
We scouted 40 creators for one campaign track last month and pitched 6. The other 34 were fine. Real audiences, real content, nothing wrong with any of them. They did not fit the brief, and a creator who does not fit the brief costs you more than an empty slot does, because you find that out after the video is live. Pulling the list is the easy half. The expensive half is the 34 you throw away, and knowing fast enough that they go in the bin. Studios who bring scouting in-house budget for the list. Almost nobody budgets for the reject rate. We have people whose entire week is that, and it is the least glamorous thing we sell.

4. The coupon code is not for the discount from your calls

Your internal call, 17 Aug. You and Bruna on coupon codes and keeping creator installs out of organic.

DS
Davorin SmitFounder, ARCH · Creator-led UA for games & apps1d · 🌐
Every campaign we set up now ships with a coupon code, and it's there for one reason. So the installs can be told apart from organic. Without it the numbers arrive as a lump. Downloads went up during the campaign window. Some of that was us, some was the store, some was whatever else the studio had running that week, and nobody can separate them afterwards. Then the campaign gets judged on a figure that was never ours to claim or to answer for. Surfshark runs a code. That is where we took it from. If you can't tie an install back to a specific video, what you have at the end of the month is a month. It won't tell you which creator to book again.

5. In Serbia it is Viber from your calls

Your internal call, 17 Aug. You and Bruna going through which chat apps each market actually uses.

DS
Davorin SmitFounder, ARCH · Creator-led UA for games & apps1d · 🌐
Last week WhatsApp communities came up as a channel idea and nobody in the room pushed back on it. In the Balkans WhatsApp communities barely exist. What people are actually in is Discord, Facebook, sometimes Telegram. Instagram broadcast channels, yes, those are everywhere. And in Serbia the number one chat app is Viber. I have a lot of relatives there and they install WhatsApp only so they can talk to us. I know creators who have run Viber groups. It is not in anybody's channel deck.

6. Nobody is charging for broadcast channels yet from your calls

Your internal call, 17 Aug. You on Instagram broadcast channels and what creators don't charge for them.

DS
Davorin SmitFounder, ARCH · Creator-led UA for games & apps1d · 🌐
Has anybody worked out what an Instagram broadcast channel is actually worth? It is a dedicated group, everyone in it opted in, and when a creator posts there I'd guess the open rate is close to everyone. Nothing else that creator owns behaves like that. I haven't seen one price it as a deliverable. Most will drop your link in there if you ask nicely, as a favour on top of the video they were already paid for. I don't think that lasts. The first agency that starts paying for placement in those channels sets the rate for everybody. Until then it is free reach sitting inside a deal you already signed. 📈
