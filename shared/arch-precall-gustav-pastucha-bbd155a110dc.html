More detail

What we sent him: the TrainStation 3 audit
  • Link: madebyarch.com/trainstation-3-audit. Sent 3 Sep. Page is dated 27 August 2026 and headed "Prepared for Pixel Federation".
  • What it measures: TrainStation 3 on YouTube over 30 days, 29 Jul to 27 Aug 2026, read through the YouTube Data API. Views are a snapshot from 27 Aug.
  • Headline: 0 declared paid placements among 56 outside TrainStation 3 videos. The page says "No declared paid coverage in our TrainStation 3 sample." 12 videos across the whole sample carry YouTube's paid-placement flag and none of them is about the game.
  • Volume: YouTube reports 136,022 results for "trainstation 3" in 30 days (40,976 in 7 days). Ten queries returned 430 distinct videos: 56 were TrainStation 3 from outside creators, 361 were off-topic (real railways, LEGO, language lessons, other games), 10 were TrainStation 2 and 3 came from the studio's own channel.
  • Who covers it today: 14 outside channels. The biggest is TapCheat (656K subscribers) with one tutorial at 123 views. One 52-subscriber fan channel posted 33 of the 56 videos. The best outside video is M_Kulas at 259 views.
  • The studio's own channel: TrainStation Games, 14.6K subscribers, 3,271,728 lifetime views across 198 videos. Its 3 uploads in the window drew 2,819 views, against 1,796 for all 56 outside videos combined. Its all-time best is a May 2025 developer diary at 622,881 views.
  • What the outside videos are: 50 of 56 are raw gameplay (89.3% of videos, 67.8% of views). 2 cover an update (371 views), 1 is a guide (123), 1 is a cheats and mods video (81), 2 other (4). The page reports no backlash and no spend showcases.
  • Who is missing: six creators with no TrainStation 3 video in the sample, in approach order: Master Hellish, Wolf Gaming, Boston By Rails, Matgonian, P.LOH, Infinite Rail Tracks. Details in the table below.
  • The close: "Let's get TrainStation 3 covered." A button to book 30 minutes with Davorin. The page also says "ARCH has rolled with over 5,000 influencers across both Americas, Europe, MENA and Australia."
  • Method notes on the page: paid status is the uploader-declared YouTube flag, so an undeclared deal would not show. No watch time, demographics, CPM or rate cards. Search results are a page-one floor.
