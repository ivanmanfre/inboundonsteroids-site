One of our campaigns looked like it cratered overnight. Nobody here believed the number. Took some digging. Most of the drop turned out to be tracking. Instagram sometimes shadowbans a reel. When that happens, the DMs that carry our tracking links quietly stop delivering. They go out for the first fifteen minutes, maybe an hour. Then nothing. Visibility on the reel drops around the same time. So the dashboard shows a collapse while the installs keep landing, uncounted. We only caught it because the drop was too big to be real. A smaller one would have sailed through. I keep wondering how many already have. If a creator campaign of yours ever falls off a cliff overnight, check the DMs before anyone blames the creative.

A creator earned €20,000 from us on one CPI campaign. The installs were that good. The same creator on a flat fee, one video: under expectations. On paper that looks like an inconsistent creator. The difference was the deal, though. Our read: on CPI he gets paid per install, so the video gets pushed, timed, promoted to his audience like the result is his problem. On a flat fee, the upload is where his job ends. We still book flat fees all the time. But a flop gets one extra check now, before anyone blames the creator: which deal shape was he on when it happened.

800 names, give or take, sit on one scouting list of ours. So take this as experience talking: manual scouting finds better creators than the platforms do. Around fifty of those came from very little trying, by hand. Every discovery tool we've paid for taught us the same thing. This is not a guess. The software is the limited part of it.
