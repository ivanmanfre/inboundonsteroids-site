AKG MEDIA

Compare your creator shortlist

A working resource for AI and tech marketing teams. Review each creator against your product, audience and campaign goal.

Prepared as a working sample for AKG. Your team can adapt these checks before using the resource with prospects. Recorded evidence supports a decision; campaign performance still needs measurement.

1. Buyer fit

Which buyer does this creator reach? Record evidence from relevant videos and audience information.

2. Product use case

Can the creator demonstrate a real task your product helps the audience complete?

3. Sponsored content

Review comparable sponsored placements and their observed performance separately from organic hits.

4. Commercial history

Record relevant advertisers that returned, what they bought again and what remains unknown.

5. Creative proposal

Describe the actual video or integration. Agree claims, room for the creator’s voice and review ownership.

6. Campaign economics

Record the quote and your allowable acquisition cost. Check the conversions required to cover the placement.

7. Execution and measurement

Confirm dates, rights, tracking and who will judge the result against the original campaign goal.

Review open questions before deciding. A strong result in one area cannot resolve missing audience fit or unsupported economics.
