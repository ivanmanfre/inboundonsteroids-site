CarouselCreator selection

Aryan Gulzar · YouFounder, AKG MediaDraft ·

Gamma had 3M users when we started working with them in August 2023. They've since passed 100M, and we've tested hundreds of creators for them across TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.

Before any creator gets booked, I ask one question.

Can they show the product working in 30 seconds?

Gamma made that easy. Type a prompt, get a deck, and the viewer gets it before the video's halfway through. A lot of AI products need more explaining than that, and the creator ends up carrying it.

If they pass, I go through the rest of my list:

→ Who actually watches them, and would those people pay for this? → Can their usual format carry a demo? → Which competitor tools have they shown this month? → Has anyone booked them more than once?

It's all in the carousel, plus how I'd brief them and run month one 👇

If a creator fails the 30-second test, I don't care how good the rest of their numbers look.

Text postProduct fit

Aryan Gulzar · YouFounder, AKG MediaDraft ·
···

The quickest way I can tell if an AI company is ready for influencer marketing is whether I understand the product within about 30 seconds of looking at it.

Wispr Flow passes straight away. You talk, it types. OpusClip too, a long video goes in and short clips come out.

A creator can open tools like that, do one thing with them and show the result, and the viewer gets it before they scroll.

Then there's the very LLM-heavy, very intricate stuff, like agent frameworks or eval tools. Great products, but someone watching on TikTok has no idea what they're looking at.

Those can still work, mostly on LinkedIn and X, but the angle has to be really specific. You're talking to the few thousand people who'd recognise their own job in the demo, and the creator needs to be one of them.

So that's the first thing I ask a founder to do on a call. Show me, 30 seconds, no slides.

Animated infographicCreator brief

Aryan Gulzar · YouFounder, AKG MediaDraft ·
···

Slightly controversial, but I think most creator briefs are written for the person approving them, and the creator is an afterthought.

You know the ones. Four pages long, eleven key messages, and the actual use case buried somewhere near the end.

The creator gets it on a Tuesday, has to film by Friday and does what anyone would do. They skim it and make the video they were going to make anyway. Then everyone blames the creator.

Here's how I'd fit it on one page 👇

→ The one task the viewer should see the product do → Who that viewer is, and why they'd care → Which claims legal needs to check → How much room the creator has to do it their way → Who signs off, and by when

The last one sounds boring, but it's the one that holds a video up for days while a product question sits in someone's inbox and nobody knows who's meant to answer it.

If your brief has a table of contents... trust me, the creator didn't read it.

Text postCampaign testing

Aryan Gulzar · YouFounder, AKG MediaDraft ·
···

Eduardo Sanchez-Ubanell sat on our panel at SF Tech Week. He built Wispr Flow's creator program to 200+ creators and 70 million views.

Around the same time, a brand came to us ready to give up on influencer marketing. Their whole test was three creators across two platforms, which made me cringe a little after hearing Eduardo talk.

Three creators on two platforms is too few to learn anything from. A good result and a bad result tell you the same thing, because you still don't know if it was the creator, the platform or the hook.

What I'd do with month one: pick one platform and one message, and spend the budget on enough creator profiles to actually compare them. Tech reviewers, productivity creators, students, whoever fits the product. Every creator gets their own tracking link before outreach starts.

Month two, keep the profile that won and change the message.

The bit I still haven't cracked is the person who watches, doesn't click, and signs up directly a week later. We catch some of it with search volume and branded mentions. Has anyone found a clean way to catch the rest?
