Creator briefing · Text post

Aryan Gulzar · YouFounder, AKG MediaDraft ·
···

I'd love to know how many creator briefs were written by people who never had to make the video.

A brief can be technically complete and still leave the creator doing a lot of guesswork. They need to know who the product is for, what someone should see it do, and which claims have to be checked.

Then give them room to use the format their audience already likes. If they usually teach through a screen recording, let them show the product. If they tell stories, give them the use case and let them find the opening.

The approval side needs to be clear as well. Who answers a product question? Who signs off a claim? When does a revised cut need to come back?

I'd want those answers in the brief before it reaches the creator.

Campaign testing · Text post

Aryan Gulzar · YouFounder, AKG MediaDraft ·
···

The first month of a creator campaign should leave you with something useful to test next.

If the creator, platform and message all change between placements, you’ve given yourself several possible explanations for the result. It gets messy when you’re deciding where the next budget should go.

I’d start with a comparison the team can explain. If we’re comparing creator profiles, keep the platform and message consistent. Record what changed, then review the creative and the agreed tracking together.

The comments can also show which parts of the product people understood or asked about. Put those observations beside the numbers and keep the unanswered questions in the readout.

I’d write down which creator profile to compare next, and what we need to keep consistent.
