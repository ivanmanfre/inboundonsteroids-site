Campaign angles to investigate.

These are proposed tests. Creator selection, commercial terms and expected results would follow research and AKG review.

Marketing team

The demonstrationStart with a product-update video and show a localised version for a second market.

Creator researchFind marketers who publish hands-on campaign workflows. Check audience geography and the quality of their comparable integrations.

Educator

The demonstrationTake one short lesson through translation, then review the output with someone fluent in the language.

Creator researchLook for course creators who explain their production process. Check whether their audience actively makes or buys video tools.

Founder

The demonstrationShow how a founder could update a product explainer using an approved digital twin.

Creator researchReview founder and software-education channels. Record existing HeyGen coverage and partnerships before selecting a contact.

The checks before a recommendation.

Audience evidence

Review recent relevant videos, geography and buyer relevance. Separate sponsored placements from organic results and record what remains unknown.

Commercial fit

Confirm the programme owner, available budget, territories, creator rates, rights and current agency relationships. The public pages leave these questions open.

A measurable test

Agree what counts as a useful result. Where access allows, track the route from a creator-specific link to sign-up, first video and paid conversion.

Public activity supports a research hypothesis. Creator availability, audience data and conversion economics need direct validation. AKG reviews the final shortlist and recommendation.
