Design considerations

A compact discussion brief; no responses required.

Jaw width and corner height

Compare right-only replacement with bilateral replacement. Keep A’s right-side direction as a reference; choose the left width and both corner heights explicitly.

Masseter and implant contour

Assess intraoral repair and the possibility of incomplete coverage. The right implant contour should remain acceptable if repair contributes less than hoped.

Lift, eyelids and left rim

Set the lift vector and anchoring, protect the lower lids, and inspect the confirmed left transition. Assess upper- and lower-eye fat sites separately.

Nose and breathing

Choose the appearance direction while preserving the liked profile. Assess the reported collapse and support need independently of the photo edits.

Final plan and sign-off

Confirm the procedure list, independent segmentation, VSP/CAD sign-off, manufacture, quote and recovery. The call’s “80% improvement” was acceptance of an imperfect result, not an outcome prediction.

What stays

Retain the chin and infraorbital projection. No fat beside the nose or into the upper lip is requested. Bilateral jaw replacement and rhinoplasty remain choices.
