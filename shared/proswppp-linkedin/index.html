From lead to conversation

  1. 1

    Check the lead

    Confirm the person, company, project and who will handle replies. Hold anyone still receiving emails or already talking with your team.

  2. 2

    Check the connection

    Review past messages. Skip the invite if already connected. Wait if a request is pending. Otherwise, send one connection request.

  3. 3

    Send the first message

    After a new acceptance, aim to send it that day once the checks pass. Account limits or late updates may cause a delay.

  4. 4

    Follow up if there is no reply

    Send up to two automatic reminders, then stop. You can change the wording and gaps in the next section.

  5. 5

    Your team takes the conversation

    Stop the remaining LinkedIn follow-ups. A draft reply is prepared for your team to review and send through LinkedIn.

A reply ends the LinkedIn follow-up.

This also applies to a confirmed email reply, a request to stop, or someone on your team taking over.
