Part 1

Bid invite follow-ups

For the jobs GCs invited us to bid on BuildingConnected, ConstructConnect, iSqFt and the others. Most of these are customers, so the emails stay short and sound like you wrote them.

How you upload

  • Two buttons on the import page: CMD upload and Bid invites upload
  • One CSV per platform tab, September onward
  • Add a Platform column: BC, CC, ISQFT or Other

When emails go out

  • Nothing before the bid date
  • First email right after upload if the bid date has passed, otherwise 2 business days after the bid
  • Early morning or late afternoon in their time zone, so it lands on top of the inbox

Customers

  • If the company is a customer in Pipedrive, they get one email only
  • After that, follow-up is yours from the Pipedrive activity
  • Everyone else gets up to 3 emails over about 6 weeks

No pile-ups

  • Several bids for the same contact = one email that names the projects
  • Nobody gets two emails from us within 3 days, across every sequence
  • Sent from the customer's owner in Pipedrive, or rotated across the team mailboxes

When they reply

  • They come out of every sequence right away
  • The lead turns manual and you get a reply drafted, ready to copy and paste

Project names

  • Short name, scope words dropped, SWPPP at the end
  • "UTMB CLC Parking Lot, All Trades" becomes UTMB CLC Parking Lot SWPPP
  • Send me your rules and I'll add them

The emails

Email 1After the bid date. Everyone, customers included.
Subject: {project short name}
Hey {first name}, did y'all win {project short name}? You should have a bid from us on the SWPPP. Let us know when you need it.

Several bids for one contact:

Subject: Your recent bids
Hey {first name}, did y'all win any of these? {Zavala SWPPP}, {Fort Bend SWPPP}, {Wharton SWPPP}. You should have SWPPP bids from us on each one. Let us know when you need us.
Email 2About 4 weeks after the bid. Not customers.
Hey {first name}, checking back on {project short name}. If it went your way, we can have the SWPPP ready before you mobilize.
Email 3About 6 weeks after the bid. Not customers.
Hey {first name}, last note on {project short name}. If you won it, happy to get the SWPPP going. If it went another way, no worries.

Part 2

LinkedIn: elephant hunting

This runs on its own, from your LinkedIn and Sales Navigator. It stays separate from Pipedrive and the email sequences, so AGC, LBA and bid invites keep getting email and calls only. We aim at the people who decide who does the SWPPP, in four groups.

Who

  • Directors of Preconstruction first, then Chief Estimators, then Senior PMs
  • Construction companies with 200+ staff, in the states we work
  • People active on LinkedIn in the last 30 days go first

How each person gets it

  • Connection request with no note
  • First message the day they accept
  • Not accepted after 14 days: one InMail from your Sales Navigator credits

Pace

  • Week 1: 5 invites a day. Week 2: 10. Then 15
  • Weekdays 8am to 5pm Central
  • About 250 to 300 people in the first month, then we look at the results

When they reply

  • Their messages stop right away
  • You get the reply by email with a suggested answer
  • Reminder after 1 business day if it's still open, again after 3

A. New in the seat

Main group

Started the role in the last 3 to 6 months. New people rebuild their sub list.

Message 1Day they accept
Hey {first name}, appreciate the connection. Congrats on the new role at {company}. Who's handling the SWPPPs over there these days?
Message 23 business days later
Hey {first name}, first few months in a new seat usually means rebuilding the sub list. Worth having a SWPPP guy on it who actually picks up the phone?
Message 37 business days after message 1
Hey {first name}, I'll stop sliding into your DMs about stormwater after this one 😄 If a SWPPP ever lands on your desk, send it my way.

B. Your old contacts who moved

Warmest

From your old customer list: the Randys who left for another company. They already know you.

Message 1Day they accept
Hey {first name}, saw you made the move to {new company}. Congrats man. We miss doing your SWPPPs from the {old company} days. Who's handling stormwater over there?
Message 24 business days later
Hey {first name}, if y'all have anything coming up at {new company} that needs a SWPPP, send it over and we'll take care of you like old times.

C. Whoever replaced them

Warm

The Steves: the new precon or estimating lead at a company that used to send us everything.

Message 1Day they accept
Hey {first name}, appreciate the connection. We've done the SWPPPs for {company} for a while, most recently {last project}. Wanted to say hi since you're the new go-to for precon.

The last project is only named when Pipedrive or Trello shows it. Otherwise that part is left out.

Message 24 business days later
Hey {first name}, anything coming up at {company} that needs a SWPPP? Happy to bid it.

D. Talking about stormwater

Signal

People posting or commenting about SWPPPs, stormwater or permits. They reply more, so they go first.

Message 1Day they accept
Hey {first name}, appreciate the connection. Saw your post about {topic}, {one line about it}. We write SWPPPs for GCs in {state}, so that one hit home.

The line about their post is written for each person and checked before it goes.
