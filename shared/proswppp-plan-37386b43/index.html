1. Bid invites

  • Nothing before the bid date
  • Customers get 1 email
  • Max 1 email per company a day
  • Nobody twice in 3 days
  • Reply = out of everything
Email 1after the bid
Hey {first name}, did y'all win {project}? You should have a bid from us on the SWPPP. Let us know when you need it.
Email 13+ bids
Hey {first name}, did y'all win any of these? {project 1}, {project 2}, {project 3}. You should have SWPPP bids from us on each one. Let us know when you need us.
Email 2~4 weeks
Hey {first name}, checking back on {project}. If it went your way, we can have the SWPPP ready before you mobilize.
Email 3~6 weeks
Hey {first name}, last one on {project}. If you won it, let's get the SWPPP going. If not, we'll get 'em on the next one.

2. LinkedIn

From your LinkedIn, on its own. Directors of precon and chief estimators at 200+ person GCs. Invite with no note, 5 a day to start. Replies get emailed to you.

A. New in the seat (last 6 months)

Message 23 days later
Hey {first name}, new guy always gets handed the sub list and the stormwater binder nobody wants. Happy to take the binder off your hands.
Message 37 days later
Hey {first name}, last one, I promise 😄 If a SWPPP ever lands on your desk, I'm the guy who actually picks up the phone.

B. Your old contacts who moved

Message 1they accept

Message 24 days later
