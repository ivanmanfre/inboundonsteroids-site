03 · What we changed

What we saw, what we changed, what it moved

What we saw
Cold prospecting was accepting at 8%. The warm lane, people already active around DTC conversations, was accepting at 30%.
What we changed
Retired the cold engine on 15 August and moved the whole budget to warm.
What it moved
One acceptance now costs 3.3 invitations instead of 12.4. The final week accepted at 27% against 12% in week one.
What we saw
Posts were running long and reading like agency marketing rather than like you talking to a store owner.
What we changed
Audited all 242 pieces in the library, rewrote the voice rules, rebuilt the generator against them.
What it moved
New posts ship shorter and in your register. The two strongest posts this month both carried a hard number and a clear position.
What we saw
Nothing in the content plan said what each post was for, so the feed had no shape.
What we changed
Every post now declares its job: two for reach, two for trust, one for buyers, each week.
What it moved
The board shows the balance live and flags a week that drifts off it.
What we saw
A booking arrived with no context, so prep started from a blank page.
What we changed
A booking now builds a briefing page automatically: their store scanned, their paid media read, and the gaps between what they told us and what we measured.
What it moved
On the first one, the prospect said he was spending across Meta, Google and TikTok while his Meta account showed no active ads and Google had been quiet 14 days.
04 · Month two

Where the next month goes

What month one actually was
One ramp week, two at half speed, and one at full volume. The two booked calls came out of 507 invitations sent mostly at half the current rate, and the final week alone ran 190 invitations and 17 replies whose conversations are still open. Held at that volume for a full month, the same rates put us in the region of four to six calls. Two things decide whether we land there: keeping the prospect list filled, and lifting the share of replies that turn into a call.
Outbound
Hold 200 or more invitations a week on the warm lane, and keep the reservoir filled so the volume is sustainable rather than a spike.
Content
Two reach, two trust, one buyer post a week. A detector now flags your winning posts on conversation rather than impressions, and one proven winner gets reworked for your audience each week.
