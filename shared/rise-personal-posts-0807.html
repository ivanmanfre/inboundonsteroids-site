Week 2The valuation trap
MD
Mattan Danino · 1st CEO @ RISE DTC 2h ·
···
I've had a few pretty good conversations with buyers about selling my agency (they all ended the same way). Every one came back to the same issue 🤯 The valuation. An agency that runs on its founder is worth a lot less without him in it. So the offer ends up looking like this: → Stay on for 24 or 36 months → Become an employee of the agency you just sold → Walk away with no job and no agency And the money? About a couple of years of what I was already making anyway. I felt pretty silly about it 😅 The trap is building an agency where everything runs through you. I only found out what that costs when I tried to price it. It's still my fear on the agency side.

Week 4The 30-employee agency
MD
Mattan Danino · 1st CEO @ RISE DTC 2h ·
···
I used to have 29 or 30 employees, servicing about 100 clients. Sounds like a win, right? It was quite a headache 😅 That was WEBITMD, the agency I started back in 2008. It's still running today. Full service, a bit of everything, too many services under one roof. Every client pulled the team in a different direction, and nobody gets really good at one craft that way. RISE is me doing the opposite. One type of client, one job: paid ads for DTC brands. The creative, the tracking, the financial modeling, all of it exists to make the ads perform. When it's the only outcome you sell, the reps add up fast... every account makes the next one sharper, because it's the same craft every single day. I already ran the bigger version of this. I'm not trying to get back there. I don't want that, actually. These days I just want us to be known as the guys who do this ONE thing, for the hundreds of brands who need that one thing.

Week 5Client 50 of 50
MD
Mattan Danino · 1st CEO @ RISE DTC 2h ·
···
I once paid a lead gen agency 2,500 or 3,000 a month. Can't even remember the exact number 😅 Yes, an agency owner paying another agency for leads. I know. Here's what I got. An initial person who takes the call. A closer. Then you're inside a copy-paste GoHighLevel system with a couple of basic looking ads. And they run it 50 times over. The guy had something like 50 clients on the same setup. Do the math, that's over 120K a month. We ended up not working with him because I realized it's sort of a joke. He didn't care. He moved on to the next one. And look, we run a playbook at RISE too, every brand we take gets the same core system, so I get the model. What I couldn't get past was being one of 50 accounts the guy had no reason to look at. The wild part is it worked, 50 clients say it worked. I still didn't want to be one of them.
